## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Dying Sea is a landscape of stark, blinding beauty. Endless white salt crust covers what was once an ocean floor, dotted with rusted shipwrecks, coral reefs exposed to open air, and shallow pools of magenta brine.

The Salt Monks wear heavy, salt-hardened robes and bone goggles to shield their eyes from the blinding glare.

**Tone:** Mythic, solemn, austere, and atmospheric. Blends desolate wasteland survival with ancient monastic mysticism.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Blinding white salt flats meeting a pale turquoise sky. Magenta brine pools shimmering with salt crystals. Massive ribcages of prehistoric sea beasts sticking out of dry mud.
2. **Sound:** The crunch of salt crust under boots. The whistling wind blowing through hollow coral formations. The low, subsonic rumble of the sleeping Leviathan beneath the salt floor.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Sharp, stinging salt in eye corners and cuts. The intense smell of ocean brine, dried seaweed, and baking sun.

--

## 2. The Dying Landscape

**The High Monastery of Salt:** Carved into the cliffs overlooking the former coastline, housing the sacred Salt Vaults.

**The Brine Pits:** Shallow, hyper-saline pools where monks harvest glowing blue preservation crystals.

**The Leviathan Trench:** The deepest part of the dried sea bed where the ancient creature lies dormant under a miles-wide crust of salt.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Order of the White Crystal:** Monks dedicated to preserving life, history, and relics using sacred salt.

**The Brine Scavengers:** Desperate wasteland nomads who raid salt convoys to sell preservation crystals to surface warlords.

**The Awakening Cult:** Fanatics who believe the awakening Leviathan will flood the world once more, and actively break the salt seals holding the creature down.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Brine Preservation

Track **Brine Preservation** from 0 to 6.

**Preservation Effects:**
- **0-1:** Normal human state. Vulnerable to heat, disease, and rot.
- **2-3:** Salt-touched. Immune to poison and disease; skin takes on a tough, leathery texture.
- **4-5:** Calcified. Physical damage reduced by 1; disadvantage on Presence and empathy checks due to emotional numbness.
- **6:** Salt Pillar. Body crystallizes into solid salt statue; mind preserved in psychic stasis.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Salt Harvester

You walk the brine flats with long wooden scrapers and collection baskets, harvesting rare preservation crystals.

- **Drive:** *"Gather enough sacred salt to preserve the monastery archives."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

### The Monastery Archivist

You use salt-curing rituals to preserve the bodies and memories of deceased elders.

- **Drive:** *"Keep the history of the Dying Sea preserved for future ages."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Trench Sentinel

You patrol the Leviathan Trench with harpoon spears, defending salt seals from scavengers and cultists.

- **Drive:** *"Prevent the Awakening Cult from breaking the ancient salt seals."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Breach at Trench 4

Cultists have detonated explosive charges along the rim of the Leviathan Trench, shattering a fifty-foot salt seal. Black brine and ancient dormant spores are bubbling up from the fissure, while massive seismic tremors crack the foundation of the High Monastery.

The player characters must trek out across the blinding salt flats, defeat the remaining cultists, and execute the sacred Salt-Sealing Ritual before the Leviathan fully wakes and shatters the sea bed.
