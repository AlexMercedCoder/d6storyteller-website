## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Bureau operates out of Far-Side Lunar Observatory Base Alpha. Isolated from Earth's electromagnetic noise, eighty scientists, technicians, and security officers spend years in near-silence monitoring deep space telemetry.

**Tone:** Intellectual, eerie, and atmospheric. The setting balances hard science fiction with cosmic dread, drawing inspiration from signal intelligence and first-contact protocols.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The stark white landscape of the lunar surface outside, contrast with dark, blue-lit server banks and cascading green waveforms on control room monitors.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic hum of liquid-nitrogen cooling pumps. The static crackle of deep-space audio feeds. The eerie, harmonized tone of Signal 88-Delta playing through lab speakers.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Cold metallic console surfaces. The smell of hot electronics, dry oxygen mix, and vacuum-sealed coffee packs.

--

## 2. The Facility

**Control Room Alpha:** The nerve center. Features a spherical holographic projection table displaying incoming frequency arrays.

**The Cryo-Server Core:** A heavily shielded subterranean vault housing quantum processors that crunch petabytes of raw signal data.

**Quarantine Module Beta:** An isolated living sector built to contain staff affected by cognitive contamination or psychogenic outbreaks.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Research Directorate:** Dedicated to complete translation of the signal regardless of personal cost, believing it represents the greatest scientific discovery in human history.

**The Security Directive:** Paranoid military officers who view the signal as a weapon or bio-cognitive threat. They stand ready to purge the server banks if translation threatens station safety.

**The Echo Cult:** A secretive group among the observatory staff who believe the signal is a divine intelligence calling humanity to transcend physical form.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Signal Resonance

Track **Signal Resonance** from 0 to 6.

**Resonance Effects:**
- **0-1:** Baseline. Clear analytical focus. No abnormal perceptions.
- **2-3:** Minor auditory hallucinations (hearing whispers in white noise), slight time perception shifts.
- **4-5:** Major cognitive synesthesia (seeing sound, hearing light), involuntary shared dreams among staff.
- **6:** Total Signal Immersion. Physical breakdown or spontaneous consciousness displacement.

--

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Signal Analyst

You turn raw radio waveforms into mathematical data. You can hear patterns in static that automated algorithms miss.

- **Drive:** *"Decode the core message before the military shuts down the array."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### The Station Medical Officer

You monitor the physical and mental health of the crew. You are the first to notice when staff members start reporting identical symptoms.

- **Drive:** *"Keep the research team sane and prevent a station-wide cognitive outbreak."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### The Security Chief

You maintain station lockdown protocols and monitor internal security. You carry sidearms in a facility where violence was once unthinkable.

- **Drive:** *"Ensure no hazardous cognitohazard escapes Far-Side Observatory."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The First Translation Node

The team successfully deciphers the first block of Signal 88-Delta. Immediately, the station's main power grid fluctuates, and three researchers in the server room collapse into a catatonic state, speaking fluently in an unrecorded ancient dialect.

The Security Directive orders an immediate lockdown and data quarantine. The players must investigate the server core, treat the affected staff, and decide whether to proceed with node two before security wipes the drives.
