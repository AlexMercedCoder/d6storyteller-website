## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Spanning the high-altitude cloud forests of the Central American Volcanic Arc (from southern Mexico through Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, to Costa Rica), *The Serpent of the Cloud Forests* brings players into a high-fantasy, ecologically rich mythic setting. An ancient order of guardians - the **Keepers of the Feathered Serpent** - embody the grace and fury of the sacred Resplendent Quetzal and *K'uk'ulkan* (the Feathered Serpent). They protect high-altitude mist-springs, ancient moss sanctuaries, and sacred bird nesting sites from silver miners and deforestation.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Iridescent emerald and crimson quetzal plumage flashing through ethereal white cloud mist; ancient tree ferns three stories tall covered in glowing violet orchids; active volcanic craters venting turquoise steam in the distance.
2. **Sound:** The crisp, bell-like calls of the Resplendent Quetzal; the rushing sound of mountain waterfalls plunging into thermal pools; wind whistling through high pine-oak ridges.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Cool, damp fog condensation clinging to your skin; the crisp scent of pine resin and wet oak bark; thermal warm spring water against mountain cold.

**The Core Tension:** Harmony vs. Extraction. The cloud forests are the water towers of Central America. Destroying the high canopy threatens the water supply of the entire region.

--

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

The Resplendent Quetzal (*Pharomachrus mocinno*) and the Feathered Serpent (*K'uk'ulkan / Q'uq'umatz*) are foundational cultural symbols across Mesoamerica and Central America, symbolizing freedom, divine breath, and cosmic harmony. The cloud forests of Central America represent some of the most biodiverse ecosystems on Earth.

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** The Quetzal is a national symbol of freedom and ecological beauty across Central America. This setting emphasizes environmental preservation and mythic reverence for nature.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Order of the Emerald Feather (Q'uq'umatz Keepers)
Mystics who weave cloud mist and channel the wisdom of the Feathered Serpent.
- **Cultural Association:** Mist-weavers, bird-callers, and highland environmental guardians.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can step into a cloud bank and reappear a mile down the ridge"*
  - *"The sacred quetzals warn me of danger before human eyes can see it"*

### The Volcanic Spring Wardens (Xetulul Guardians)
Warriors who protect the thermal springs and sacred volcanic vents of the high arc.
- **Cultural Association:** Geothermal specialists, thermal healers, and ridge defenders.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can channel thermal volcanic heat into my staff during battle"*
  - *"Built to withstand the coldest high-mountain blizzards"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Feathered Flight & Mist Weaving

Characters track a **Quetzal Plume Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Grounded):** Normal rules. Mist weaving is unavailable.
- **3–4 (Cloud-Woven):** +1d6 to Finesse and Mind in highland terrain. Can glide on cloud currents and blend invisibly into fog. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5–6 (Serpent Apex):** +1d6 to Force and Finesse. Full flight and storm call. Failure causes smoke-choking penalties if away from high altitude.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Quetzal Mist-Weaver
- **Concept:** A priestess of the Feathered Serpent who protects cloud forest nests.
- **Descriptors:** *Wears iridescent emerald spirit feathers*, *Master of mist-weaving magic*, *Communicates with highland birds*.
- **Drive:** *"Protect the sacred cloud forest canopy for future generations."*
- **Flaw:** *"Weakens quickly when descending to hot, dry lowlands."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### Archetype 2: The Volcanic Ridge Warden
- **Concept:** A warrior who guards high-altitude thermal vents.
- **Descriptors:** *Master of geothermal fire*, *Unshakable mountain defender*, *Wields an obsidian-edged staff*.
- **Drive:** *"Guard the thermal springs from mining pollution."*
- **Flaw:** *"Stubborn as mountain basalt."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### Archetype 3: The Highland Herbalist
- **Concept:** A scholar of rare cloud forest orchids and medicinal flora.
- **Descriptors:** *Knows every rare orchid and remedy in the cloud forest*, *Gentle healer*, *Keen observer of nature*.
- **Drive:** *"Catalog and preserve the healing gifts of the canopy."*
- **Flaw:** *"Reluctant to engage in physical violence."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 4 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Siege of the Mist Spring

- **Framing:** A silver mining company brings heavy logging machinery and dynamite to clear a sacred cloud forest ridge.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Stopping logging crews from felling a thousand-year-old ceiba tree.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Dynamite charges set on the spring head; 4 beats to detonation.
  3. *Opportunity:* Summoning a dense cloud fog to blind the mining crew and disarm the charges.
- **Antagonist:** *Director Silas Vance* (Ruthless Mining Magnate).
  - *Traits:* Heavy armored steam tractor, mercenary guards.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Overconfident / ☐ Machinery Sabotaged / ☐ Forced to Retreat.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

The cloud forests of Central America are among the most precious ecosystems on Earth, embodying the mythic beauty of the Resplendent Quetzal and the Feathered Serpent. This setting celebrates highland conservation and indigenous ecological wisdom.
