## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The most feared warrior in three kingdoms retired, had an epiphany, and became a motivational speaker. He is, objectively, quite good at it. The methods are unconventional.

Grug the Unstoppable's approach to life coaching is entirely sincere. He is not performing barbarian tropes for a civilian audience. He genuinely believes that the philosophy of the warrior -- commitment, clarity, rage management, rest, tactical thinking -- provides exactly the framework that most people are missing in their lives. His corporate clients are often surprised to discover he's right.

The comedy emerges from the collision of genuine warrior wisdom with civilian context. Grug's advice is often correct. The delivery is always extremely barbarian. The applications he suggests are technically viable but socially unusual.

**Tone:** Grug is played completely straight. His clients are played for the genuine comedy of people who signed up for self-help and are receiving wisdom they didn't expect from a source they didn't anticipate. The wisdom is real. The delivery is exceptional.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A seminar hall where Grug (enormous, scarred, dressed in business casual he selected himself and is wearing with complete confidence) presents to an audience of merchants, nobles, and professionals who came expecting buzzwords and received a battle philosophy lecture; the Seven Pillars chart on the flip board illustrated with actual battle diagrams because Grug finds combat examples clearest; the front row of participants who have already committed fully.
2. **Sound:** Grug's speaking voice (calibrated from command-in-battle to conversational over 25 years but still quite loud), the varied response of his audience (some transformed, some skeptical, some both), the motivational chant he teaches (technically a pre-battle war cry, works surprisingly well in the presentation context), and the very specific tone of a client experiencing a genuine breakthrough delivered through the vocabulary of tactical combat.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The substantial physical reality of Grug's seminar materials (printed on heavy parchment, extremely durable, because Grug has been burned before), the seminar venue's formal atmosphere subtly disrupted by Grug's presence, and the physical warmth of his coaching sessions (Grug sits very close and makes sustained eye contact; this is intentional and effective).

**The Core Tension:** Grug's philosophy works. Applying it in civilian contexts creates situations that are genuinely better for the person but genuinely unusual for everyone around them.

## 2. The Grug Coaching Empire

### The Seminar Circuit
Grug travels the kingdom delivering his signature seminar: "Unleash Your Inner Berserker: A Warrior's Guide to Professional Excellence." The seminar is three days. Day 1: The Seven Pillars. Day 2: Applied Rage Management (practical exercises). Day 3: The Long Victory (strategic planning using battle map methodology). Testimonials are uniformly positive.

### Private Coaching
Elite clients can retain Grug for one-on-one coaching. This involves Grug accompanying them to meetings, negotiations, and professional challenges and providing real-time tactical feedback (quietly; Grug has learned that audible battlefield critique is "not always the best format for civilian contexts").

### The Book
"Unleash Your Inner Berserker" has been published in seven editions and is the most successful self-help text in the kingdom. Its chapter on rage management is used in some conflict resolution programs. Grug is very proud of this.

### The Certification Program
Other coaches can become Grug-certified practitioners. The certification involves a practical examination that Grug administers personally. The pass rate is 30%. The 70% who fail often report it as life-changing anyway.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The Corporate Retreat:** Grug has been hired to run a three-day corporate retreat for a major merchant guild. The guild leadership expects team building exercises. Grug has planned an experiential immersion in the Seven Pillars that is significantly more intense. The players must navigate between Grug's vision and the guild's expectations.

2. **The Rival Coach:** A competing life coach has begun marketing against Grug specifically, claiming that barbarian philosophy is "incompatible with modern values." The competitor is attracting clients who would normally be Grug's. Grug wants to respond. His instinct for response is not always professionally appropriate.

3. **The Success Story:** A former coaching client has applied Grug's methods so thoroughly that they've become extremely successful and are now facing challenges that exceed Grug's framework. They're coming to him for the next level. Grug has to develop new material in real time.

4. **The Seminar Disaster:** Something has gone wrong at the three-day seminar. The practical exercises on Day 2 have escalated beyond their intended scope. Several participants are experiencing genuine breakthroughs that require immediate support. One has applied Pillar Two to a situation that is now a diplomatic incident.

5. **The Memoirs:** Grug is writing his memoirs and has hired the players as research assistants. They must interview former clients, research his battle history, and organize 45 years of materials. The memoirs keep revealing things about Grug's past that complicate the motivational narrative.
