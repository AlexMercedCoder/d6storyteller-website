> - High Keeper Solon

## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

Sol-Prima is a subterranean metropolis of stone bridges, terraced gardens, and glowing crystal towers built around a central chasm.

Suspended over the chasm is Sol-Core: a 100-foot sphere of golden plasma contained within brass magnetic rings.

**Tone:** Heroic, luminous, atmospheric fantasy. High contrast between blinding holy light and terrifying subterranean darkness.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The brilliant golden glow of Sol-Core illuminating multi-tiered stone streets. Long, dancing shadows in outer cavern districts.
2. **Sound:** The deep, warm hum of the plasma core. The ringing of shift bells signaling the artificial "dusk." The crackle of radiant crystal fuel in the furnace.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Intense heat near the furnace towers. The clean, dry smell of ionized air and burning crystal.

--

## 2. The City Districts

**The Sun Tower:** The central spire housing the crystal loading machinery and magnetic containment controls.

**The Terrace Gardens:** Bioluminescent crop terraces lit by Sol-Core's daily light cycle.

**The Shadow Outskirts:** Outer cavern neighborhoods where light is dim and shadow-barriers are maintained by local guards.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Order of Sun Keepers:** Priests and engineers who manage Sol-Core's fuel intake and magnetic containment.

**The Crystal Miners Guild:** Deep-shaft miners who extract radiant-crystals from dangerous lower caverns.

**The Cult of Eternal Night:** Outlaw zealots who believe Sol-Core is an unnatural prison for true subterranean shadow entities.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Solar Radiance

Track **Solar Radiance** from 6 down to 0.

**Radiance States:**
- **5-6 (Full Noon):** Bright illumination across all districts. Plants grow; shadow entities cannot manifest.
- **3-4 (Dusk):** Dim light. Outskirts fall into shadow; minor shadow-crawlers appear near city walls.
- **1-2 (Eclipse):** Deep twilight. City-wide panic; major shadow entities breach outer districts.
- **0 (Total Darkness):** Sol-Core extinguishes. Full shadow invasion; life support collapses.

--

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Crystal Stoker

You handle heavy tongs and heat-shield armor to feed radiant-crystals into the core furnace.

- **Drive:** *"Keep Sol-Core burning no matter the cost."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Magnetic Engineer

You adjust the giant brass containment rings that suspend and stabilize Sol-Core.

- **Drive:** *"Prevent plasma containment failure."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### The Shadow Guard

You patrol the dark outskirts armed with radiant-crystal lances to defeat shadow beasts.

- **Drive:** *"Protect the city perimeter during dusk cycles."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Crystal Shortage

The primary radiant-crystal mine suffers a collapse, cutting off fuel supplies to Sol-Core. Radiance drops to 2 (Eclipse), and shadow entities begin breaching the lower terrace gardens.

The player characters must lead a desperate expedition into the collapsed deep mines to retrieve emergency crystal reserves, fight off shadow beasts in the dark, and return to feed the furnace before Sol-Core goes completely black.
