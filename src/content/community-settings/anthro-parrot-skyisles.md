## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The **Feathered Sky** is high fantasy at its most chromatic and aerial—a civilization built on floating coral-and-stone islands in a world where the surface below is perpetual cloud. The parrot-folk who inhabit these islands are creatures of extraordinary beauty, intelligence, and social complexity. The Aerie Courts are simultaneously gorgeous (festivals of color and sound, Wind-Song ceremonies that keep islands aloft and double as musical performances) and politically vicious (chromatic rank is determined by natural plumage, which cannot be changed, which means the political hierarchy is literally biological).

The tone layers wonder (sky civilizations, Wind-Song magic, the literally colorful social hierarchy) with political drama (house alliances, the Wind-Song crisis, the question of what happens when an island falls and whose fault it is). This is a setting for players who want **beautiful high fantasy with genuine stakes** and a social system complex enough to navigate for multiple sessions.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The impossible beauty of an Aerie Court festival—hundreds of parrot-folk in full plumage performing synchronized flight patterns around a Wind-Song anchor, their collective colors shifting like a living kaleidoscope; the terrifying beauty of a falling island, tons of ancient coral and centuries of civilization dropping through cloud in slow, disintegrating silence.
2. **Sound:** The Wind-Song itself—a harmonic resonance built from contributions of thousands of Attuned individuals over centuries, audible as a low chord that is more felt than heard, like standing inside a cathedral bell; the sudden, wrongness of a dissonance note appearing in the Song.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The clean high-altitude cold of life above the clouds; the complex tropical fragrance of living coral platforms in bloom; the electric tingle of high Wind-Song Attunement, which parrot-folk describe as their feathers trying to vibrate at the Song's frequency.

## 2. The Three Kindreds

**Arakin (Macaw-folk):** Large, spectacular, and politically dominant. Their chromatic diversity (scarlet macaws, blue-and-gold, military, hyacinth) means multiple noble houses are defined by plumage color, creating a complex chromatic hierarchy. They govern the Great Perches—the largest floating islands—and consider themselves the natural aristocracy of the sky.

**Katura (Cockatoo-folk):** Scholarly, expressive, and slightly contrarian. Their predominantly white or pale plumage with vivid crests marks them as mid-tier in chromatic rank, which they have compensated for by making intellectual achievement the Katura's primary status currency. The Feathered Libraries are their greatest achievement. Their expressive crests rise and fall with emotion in ways impossible to fully control—making Katura the most readable and simultaneously most honest people in the Courts.

**Graylore (African Grey-folk):** The smallest and most diplomatically significant kindred. Their subdued grey plumage makes them low-chromatic-rank in formal settings, but their perfect auditory memory makes them indispensable as living contracts, diplomatic archives, and witnesses. Every significant agreement in the Aerie Courts requires a Graylore witness—which gives Graylore enormous unofficial power despite nominal low status.

---

## 3. Factions & Conflict

**House Scarlaine (Scarlet Macaw):** The highest-chromatic-rank Arakin house. Currently accused by rivals of deliberately disrupting Wind-Song harmonics in a competitor island's anchor region.

**The Feathered Library Conclave:** A Katura scholarly body that has identified the Wind-Song dissonance pattern as artificial—someone is intentionally introducing off-frequency contributions to the Song.

**The Graylore Memory Council:** Possess, collectively, every word spoken in formal diplomatic context for the past 200 years. Have something they haven't shared yet: a conversation they witnessed 40 years ago that is directly relevant to the current crisis.

---

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Wind-Song & Chromatic Rank

**Plumage Rating social modifier:**
- Rating 5-6: +1d6 to Presence in formal Aerie settings
- Rating 1-2: +1d6 to Finesse in informal/criminal/underground contexts (underestimation advantage)

**Wind-Song Attunement actions:**
- Attunement 1-3: Can sense the Song's health in the immediate area
- Attunement 4-5: Can contribute a stabilizing harmonic (requires sustained concentration, 1 scene)
- Attunement 6: Can identify the source of a dissonance note within the Song

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Graylore Witness
A senior Graylore diplomat who witnessed the conversation 40 years ago. Has been waiting for precisely the right moment to introduce that memory into the political record. That moment is now—they just need the right audience.
- **Quote:** *"I remember everything I have ever heard. This is a gift and a burden in equal measure. What I remember about that conversation forty years ago is both of those things, and I am done waiting to be asked."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### Archetype 2: The Arakin Wind-Singer
A young scarlet macaw whose Wind-Song Attunement is extraordinarily high—high enough to hear the dissonance clearly and identify its source. This is politically dangerous information given their house's involvement.
- **Quote:** *"The Song is being poisoned from inside. I know the frequency signature. I know whose harmonic style it matches. I would really prefer that it matched someone else's."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### Archetype 3: The Katura Fall Investigator
A cockatoo scholar who was studying the fallen island's Wind-Song anchor academically when the dissonance began. Her research notes contain the earliest recorded evidence of the artificial disturbance.
- **Quote:** *"The data says someone has been doing this for eleven years. Eleven years of tiny adjustments to the Song, each below the threshold of detection. This was planned. This was patient. This was professional."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario & Why It Matters

**The Resonance Parliament:** Three days after a secondary island (inhabited, 6,000 residents evacuated before the fall) has dropped through the cloud-sea, the three courts convene a Resonance Parliament at the Neutral Anchor—an island controlled by no single house. The Graylore Memory Council announces it has relevant testimony it has been withholding for 40 years and will introduce it now. Every major house has sent representatives. The Wind-Song dissonance is audible for the first time without specialized Attunement. The next anchor to fail will be a Great Perch. The Parliament has to decide in 24 hours whether the fall was accident, negligence, or deliberate act—and each answer leads to a completely different political resolution.

**Why It Matters:** Parrot cognition is genuinely one of the most sophisticated in the animal kingdom—grey parrots can solve abstract logical problems that stump many primates, macaws form long-term social relationships of genuine complexity. Giving this intelligence a civilization to build asks what a society would look like if every member had perfect memory, sophisticated social awareness, and communication at the heart of their culture. The chromatic hierarchy is an uncomfortable metaphor for beauty-based social discrimination. The Wind-Song is civilization as collective art project. Both are worth exploring.
