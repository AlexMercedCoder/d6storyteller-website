## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Circuit spans from the Mercury slingshot gates out to the Jovian Relay. Races are broadcast to billions of viewers across the settled system.

Craft are minimal: ultra-thin carbon-nanotube rigging, mirror-finish reflective sails covering thousands of square meters, and a tiny pressurized pod for the pilot and navigator.

**Tone:** High-octane, competitive, technical, and adrenaline-fueled. Blends extreme sports culture with hard orbital mechanics.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The blinding golden glare of the sun filling the viewport. The vast, shimmering expanse of reflective sail material stretching out like silver wings against dark space.
2. **Sound:** The whisper-quiet silence of vacuum broken only by the hum of internal life support and the sharp *snap* of tension cables under solar wind pressure.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The intense heat radiating through thermal shielding. Sweat inside a flight suit. The G-force pressure during tight planetary slingshot maneuvers.

--

## 2. The Circuit

**The Mercury Slingshot Gate:** The starting point for the Helios Grand Prix, where ships dive close to Mercury's gravity well.

**The Flare Zone:** A dangerous sector near solar equator where pilots attempt to catch coronal mass ejections for massive acceleration.

**The Finish Bar (Pallas Station):** Where teams celebrate, trade technical secrets, and settle track grudges after the race.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Racing Federation:** Enforces track regulations, sail dimensions, and safety protocols.

**The Corporate Teams:** Multi-million-credit syndicates with cutting-edge sail materials, telemetry AI, and dedicated sabotage units.

**The Privateer League:** Independent pilots flying custom-built rigs, competing on skill and nerve rather than corporate budgets.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Solar Velocity

Track **Solar Velocity** from 0 to 6.

**Velocity Controls:**
- Sail angle adjustment (Finesse roll): +1 Velocity
- Coronal flare catch (Mind roll): +2 Velocity (Risk: Sail Damage)
- Tactical braking maneuver: -1 Velocity

**Velocity States:**
- **0-1:** Slow tacking speed. Falling behind pack.
- **2-4:** Racing velocity. Optimal maneuvering envelope.
- **5-6:** Extreme speed. Maximum race lead, high risk of thermal shield failure or sail tear.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Sail Pilot

You have the instincts and reflexes to read solar wind currents and trim sail lines in real-time.

- **Drive:** *"Win the Helios Cup on personal skill, not corporate tech."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Telemetry Navigator

You calculate real-time solar flare trajectories and orbital vectors on high-speed flight computers.

- **Drive:** *"Plot the fastest track line through the solar flare zone."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Rig Master

You maintain the ultralight carbon hull, thermal coatings, and sail deployment tensioners.

- **Drive:** *"Keep the ship from burning up during close solar passes."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Corona Run

During the final leg of the Mercury Slingshot, a massive Class-X solar flare erupts directly ahead of the lead pack. The Racing Federation signals a yellow flag to abort, but the corporate leader ignores the warning and dives into the flare wave.

The player team must decide whether to follow into the flare to claim victory, pull a dangerous gravity-assist maneuver around Mercury to intercept, or deal with a rival team attempting to sabotage their sail rigging mid-race.
