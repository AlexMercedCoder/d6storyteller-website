## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Orbital Ring sits sixty thousand kilometers above the planetary surface: a monument to an era when corporate megastructures connected ground to orbit. Following the Corporate Secession Wars, vast sections of the ring were abandoned, leaving millions of tons of high-technology scrap suspended in low-to-medium orbits.

The League consists of roughly forty independent salvage tugs. They are not corporate employees; they are independent operators who pay license fees to the League Registry in exchange for claim arbitration and beacon tracking.

**Tone:** Gritty, blue-collar space industrialism. The setting emphasizes physical hazards, worn-out equipment, resource scarcity, and the camaraderie of crews living on the razor edge of vacuum survival.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The view through a salvage tug's reinforced viewport: the massive silhouette of the Orbital Ring cutting across the planetary horizon, lit by stark sunlight and peppered with flashing beacon lights. Inside the tug: cramped corridors, exposed wiring bundles, magnetic boot racks, and the blue glow of welding torches.
2. **Sound:** The dull *thunk* of magnetic tethers latching onto airlock frames. The hiss of equalization valves. The terrifying, metallic groan of buckling bulkheads under gravitational shear. The crackle of helmet comms filled with static and heavy breathing.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The biting chill of unheated airlocks. The pervasive smell of recycled ozone, burnt circuit boards, and stale ration packs. The vibration of thruster pulses echoing through hull plating directly into a spacer's magnetic boots.

## 2. The League

**The Registry Station** is an old orbital tug dock repurposed as the League's central hub. It contains the Claim Registry, a bar named *The Broken Seal*, a dry-dock repair yard, and the office of Administrator Dax.

**The Claim Registry** tracks active salvage permits. When a salvage crew tags a piece of orbital debris with a homing beacon, they register the signature with the Registry. The League enforces a thirty-day exclusive reclamation window. Disputing a claim requires filing a challenge before the Arbitrators.

**The Scrapper Bar (*The Broken Seal*)** is where crews gather between runs to exchange orbital telemetry, trade spare parts, and hire specialized EVA technicians. It is also where corporate spotters quietly recruit desperate crews for unregistered black-budget retrieval ops.

## 3. Factions

**The Independent Salvage League** represents the blue-collar operators. They value claim integrity and mutual aid during vacuum emergencies. Their unofficial motto is "Vacuum takes no sides," meaning any crew in distress receives assistance regardless of personal grudges.

**Aegis Orbital Security** is the privatized security contractor hired by Earth-based corporations to protect "legacy assets." Aegis forces operate armed interceptor skiffs and treat any salvage crew approaching corporate-flagged debris as pirates.

**The Free Orbit Guild** is a radical faction of spacers who believe all orbital debris belongs to those who live in vacuum. They refuse to pay League Registry fees and regularly jump registered claims, creating violent clashes with League veterans.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Structural Drift

Track the target wreckage's **Stability** from 6 down to 0.

**Stability Triggers:**
- Heavy cutting torch operation: -1 Stability
- Explosive decompression or thruster impact: -2 Stability
- Failing a physical Mind or Force check while moving heavy gear: -1 Stability

**Stability Conditions:**
| Stability | Wreckage State |
|-----------|----------------|
| 5-6 | Stable hull. Internal atmosphere or vacuum contained. |
| 3-4 | Minor structural shifts. Creaking bulkheads, power surges, localized decompression hazards. |
| 1-2 | Severe structural failure. Hull breaches, falling debris, orbital decay accelerating. |
| 0 | Complete disintegration or atmospheric re-entry. Immediate evacuation required. |

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The EVA Lead

You are the first person into the dark hull and the last person back into the airlock. You know thruster physics, cutting tools, and vacuum survival by heart. You can read structural stress lines in metal that others miss.

- **Drive:** *"Keep the crew alive and secure the payload before the hull bucklers."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

### The Tech Specialist

You handle computer cores, power cell stabilization, and beacon decryption. If an ancient corporate vault has an active bio-lock or encrypted data node, you are the one who bypasses it while wearing pressurized gloves.

- **Drive:** *"Decrypt the high-value data cores before Aegis security jams our comms."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### The Salvage Captain

You own the tug, manage the fuel budget, and handle League claim registration. You know which corporate patrol routes to avoid and when a claim is too hot to touch.

- **Drive:** *"Pay off the ship's mortgage and keep the Registry off our back."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Decay of Vault 88

A derelict corporate bio-lab (Vault 88) has dropped out of its stable lagrange point and is on a 48-hour orbital decay trajectory toward atmospheric entry. Sensor sweeps reveal a sealed vault compartment containing prototype cryo-storage units. The League Registry has marked it as open claim due to imminent destruction. Aegis Security has deployed a gunship to blockade the vault, while a Free Orbit Guild crew is already attempting a blind breaching operation.

The characters must navigate past the blockade, breach the failing hull without triggering total collapse, secure the cryo-units, and extract before Vault 88 burns up in the upper atmosphere.
