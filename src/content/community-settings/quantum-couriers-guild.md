## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Quantum Express Guild is an elite, independent courier network operating out of the Nexus Station, a massive hub built inside a stabilized Kerr black hole. 

Guild couriers fly light, heavily modified slip-ships equipped with chronal dampeners. They carry the most sensitive cargo in the sector: state secrets, rare gene-codes, and historical artifacts.

**Tone:** Fast-paced, mind-bending sci-fi action. High stakes, temporal puzzles, and high-octane space travel.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The blinding chromatic aberration of quantum wormhole entry. Sleek, needle-shaped slip-ships with glowing chronal rings. The bustling multi-species concourse of Nexus Station.
2. **Sound:** The piercing Doppler-shift whine of a wormhole drive spooling up. Chronal chimes warning of temporal displacement. Metallic clatter of locked cargo containers.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The feeling of hair standing on end due to static temporal charge. Cold cockpit controls. Smells of ozone, liquid nitrogen coolant, and synthetic caffeine.

--

## 2. The Infrastructure

**Nexus Station:** The chronally neutral space station at the center of the wormhole network where past, present, and future couriers meet to trade routes.

**The Slip-Lanes:** Unstable quantum tunnels cutting through non-linear space-time.

**The Chronal Vaults:** Secure storage facilities where packages destined for future centuries are kept under stasis.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Quantum Guild Directorate:** Enforces courier rules, manages slip-lane fees, and maintains neutrality in galactic conflicts.

**The Temporal Correction Bureau:** Mysterious causality enforcers who hunt down couriers creating timeline paradoxes.

**The Chrono-Pirates:** Outlaw crews who ambush slip-ships inside wormhole tunnels to steal future technology before it is invented.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Temporal Paradox

Track **Temporal Paradox** from 0 to 6.

**Paradox Events:**
- Arriving before package dispatch: +1 Paradox
- Meeting a temporal duplicate of yourself: +2 Paradox
- Changing a past event during delivery: +2 Paradox
- Resolving a paradox via stable loop: -2 Paradox

**Paradox Levels:**
- **0-2:** Stable timeline. Minor deja-vu anomalies.
- **3-4:** Chronal shadow effects (ghostly echoes of alternate choices).
- **5-6:** Reality tears. Temporal Correction agents intercept vessel.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Slip-Pilot

You fly faster than light and know how to ride wormhole turbulence without tearing your ship apart.

- **Drive:** *"Make the delivery deadline no matter what century it is."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Chrono-Nav Specialist

You calculate non-linear jump vectors and plot arrival times across temporal offsets.

- **Drive:** *"Prevent temporal paradoxes from corrupting our flight plan."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Cargo Enforcer

You protect the slip-pouch from chrono-pirates and corporate hijackers during atmospheric and vacuum transit.

- **Drive:** *"Ensure the payload reaches the recipient intact."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Yesterday Delivery

A high-priority client hands the team a sealed quantum slip-pouch addressed to... the team's pilot, timestamped three days in the past. To fulfill the contract and collect the fee, the crew must jump back through an unstable wormhole, deliver the package to their past selves without causing a timeline collapse, and avoid a Chrono-Pirate fleet tracking the pouch's unique chronal signature.
