## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The heroes of prophecy grew up, got mortgages, and settled in Maplecroft Estates. Now they have a HOA board meeting every second Tuesday.

**Maplecroft Estates** is a planned community that developed in the magical equivalent of the 1950s-1970s and has been experiencing steady property value appreciation. It is genuinely nice. The schools are good. The streets are quiet. The community pool's perpetual warmth enchantment has never failed. Most residents are happy here.

The comedy emerges from the collision of genuinely unlimited magical power with genuinely mundane suburban concerns. A wizard who could reshape reality cannot add a sunroom without a variance approval. A warlock with a patron entity from beyond the void is nonetheless subject to the noise ordinance after 10pm. A druid whose attunement to nature could turn the entire suburb into ancient forest has chosen, instead, to maintain a tasteful herb garden within the approved landscaping guidelines.

**Tone:** The characters genuinely like their neighborhood and want to be good neighbors. The HOA genuinely believes it's serving the community. Most conflicts are about mismatched expectations rather than malice. Some conflicts are about malice. The HOA treasurer has opinions.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A perfect suburban street where every third house has something subtly magical: a garden that blooms in geometric patterns that shift based on the homeowner's mood, a driveway with runes worked into the concrete that glow faintly at night, gutters that drain into a small dimensional pocket rather than onto the street, and one house that looks entirely normal because its magical nature is internal and its owners have excellent HOA compliance scores.
2. **Sound:** The suburban sounds of lawnmowers, children playing, and the distant clatter of kitchen activity, occasionally disrupted by the subsonic hum of a containment spell being refreshed, the sharp crack of a teleportation arrival on the approved landing zone, and the very audible silence that follows an HOA enforcement notice being delivered.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Freshly cut grass maintained by a growth-calibration enchantment, the warm bread smell from a neighboring druid's communal garden, the faint ozone of active wards around the Maplecroft property boundary, and the cold paper of an HOA violation notice in the mailbox.

**The Core Tension:** The players have real magical power and real suburban commitments. Every adventure balances saving the day with maintaining their Compliance Rating.

## 2. Maplecroft Estates Factions

### The HOA Board
Five elected homeowners who take this very seriously. The President is a retired combat illusionist who applies her tactical training to parking enforcement. The Treasurer controls community fund access and is not shy about this. The Secretary records everything, magically, and has files going back 40 years.

### The Neighborhood Watch
Retired wizards and enchanters with a lot of time, a deep investment in property values, and access to scrying enchantments they technically should not be using for surveillance but are.

### The New Families
Recent move-ins who didn't fully read the HOA documents before purchasing. They are learning. Their dimensional portal is affecting the Hendersons' Wi-Fi equivalent crystal resonance and this must be resolved.

### The Druid on the Corner
Has lived in Maplecroft since before it was Maplecroft. Has never violated a single HOA rule because they helped write all of them. Occasionally dispenses wisdom that seems trivial but isn't.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The Unauthorized Summoning Circle:** Unit 4B's homeowner has installed a summoning circle in their backyard. It is within the setback requirements. The entity it summons respects property boundaries. But the emanations are technically covered by the nuisance clause. Who wins?

2. **The Property Line Dispute:** Two neighboring enchanters have opposing wards that create a visible magical boundary effect exactly on their shared property line. Both claim the other started it. The effect is growing.

3. **The Emergency Board Election:** The HOA President has been polymorphed into a decorative garden gnome. (She is fine. She is annoyed.) Someone needs to run emergency community governance while this is resolved. The polymorph source is unknown.

4. **The Haunting:** Unit 19 has ghosts. Actual ghosts. The HOA's position is that this violates the community standards on uninvited guests. The ghosts were there before the HOA. Establishing squatter's rights for incorporeal entities requires research.

5. **The Annual Block Party:** The block party requires a magical entertainment permit, three separate food handling certifications for the enchanted dish contributors, and someone needs to negotiate with the druid on the corner about the inevitable nature-communion that will interrupt the schedule.
