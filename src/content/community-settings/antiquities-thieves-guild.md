> *"The Dawnspire Chalice was taken from the Veth people in 1403, during the Third Colonial Administration's systematic clearance of the Veth River settlements. It has been in Lord Carrell's family for four generations. Lord Carrell inherited it. Lord Carrell's grandfather took it from a Veth community center at gunpoint. I am aware that one of these statements makes the current Lord sympathetic and the other makes our operation straightforward. Both statements are true."*
> - Guild Master Rena, Reclamation Guild Pre-Operation Brief

## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Reclamation Guild has operated for forty years. They have completed 847 documented recoveries. Their success rate on operations is 91%. Their rate of bystander harm is zero: the Guild's operational code prohibits harm to anyone who is not directly protecting an artifact through willing participation in a criminal cover-up. This last clause is the one that Guild lawyers work with most frequently.

The Guild does not return artifacts for profit. They charge a significant fee for their services, but the fee covers operational costs, legal defense, and the Guild's archive of provenance research. The recovered artifacts go to the requesting community without charge.

**Tone:** Serious heist drama with genuine ethical weight. Every operation has a moral argument and a logistical challenge and those two things are intertwined. The Guild is not Robin Hood: they are professionals who have thought carefully about what they are doing and why, and who know that their justification does not make the risks easier.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Guild's planning room: maps, floor plans, guard schedules, and the Provenance documentation that must be complete before any operation is approved. The artifacts themselves: objects with histories that the Guild's researchers have documented in detail, so that every Guild member going into an operation knows exactly what they are recovering and why. The post-operation briefing: where recovered artifacts are documented before transfer to the requesting community.
2. **Sound:** The planning briefings: detailed, precise, and focused on contingencies. The Guild's moral framework is present in these briefings as a constant background: every operational decision is tested against the code, and if a contingency requires something the code prohibits, the contingency is rejected and a different approach is found.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The artifacts the Guild recovers: old things with history in their physical character. Guild researchers can often date an artifact's relocation based on physical evidence that contradicts the collector's claimed provenance. This physical archaeology is the Guild's most reliable evidence type and the one that produces the strongest Chain Strength.

## 2. The Guild

**The Provenance Archive** is the Guild's most valuable asset: forty years of documented recoveries, including the research files, the legal history, and the outcome records for each returned artifact. The Archive is used primarily to support new Provenance Cases but has also been cited in fourteen legal proceedings as evidence of the Guild's methodology. The legal proceedings ended in varying ways.

**The Operations Team** is structured around specific skills: researchers who build the Provenance Case, planners who design the operation, operators who execute it, and transfer specialists who ensure recovered artifacts reach their destination communities securely.

**The Legal Defense Fund** is the Guild's institutional acknowledgment that its operations will sometimes result in criminal charges. The Fund retains three lawyers and has defended 23 Guild members in criminal proceedings. Conviction rate: 14%. The Guild considers this acceptable.

**The Community Liaison Office** manages the Guild's relationships with the communities requesting recoveries. Most of these relationships are long-term: a community contacts the Guild about an artifact, the Guild's researchers build a Provenance Case, the operation is planned and executed, and then the artifact arrives. The Liaison Office manages the period between contact and arrival, which can be years.

## 3. Factions

**The Reclamation Guild** operates with the professional self-discipline of people who know their activities will be scrutinized and who have made their ethical framework robust enough to withstand scrutiny. Guild Master Rena Pell has been in the position for twelve years. She approves every Provenance Case personally, because the Chain Strength determination is the Guild's core ethical decision and she does not delegate it.

**The Collector Community** is a loose coalition of major artifact holders who are aware of the Guild and actively work to prevent recoveries. They have hired private security, improved archive documentation to complicate Provenance research, and lobbied the regional government for legislation that would classify Guild operations as cultural sabotage rather than theft. Their spokesperson, Lord Carrell, has publicly offered to "negotiate" on two Guild targets and privately offered a substantially larger sum to have the Guild's Archive destroyed.

**The Cultural Recovery Advocates** are the legal activists who support the Guild's goals through legitimate channels. They have had limited success: three diplomatic agreements in forty years have resulted in voluntary artifact returns. They find the Guild's approach effective but legally complicated, and their relationship with the Guild is one of mutual support and mutual discomfort.

**The Regional Constabulary** investigates Guild operations and has had eleven sustained investigations in forty years. None have produced convictions that stuck. The lead investigator, a careful detective named Bren, has developed a sophisticated understanding of the Guild's methodology and a complicated personal relationship with the Guild's goals that she manages by focusing rigorously on her professional obligations.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Provenance Chain

Before any operation, research produces a **Provenance Chain** with Strength from 1 to 5.

| Strength | Documentation Status |
|----------|---------------------|
| 5 | Complete chain. Original ownership proven, transfer by clearly illegal means documented. Community support certain. |
| 4 | Strong case. Primary documentation complete, minor gaps. Defensible in any hearing. |
| 3 | Credible case. Circumstantial evidence strong but direct documentation incomplete. |
| 2 | Disputed case. Significant gaps or contested documentation. Multiple parties have claims. |
| 1 | Weak case. Evidence primarily testimonial. Community claim credible but legally fragile. |

**Operational consequences by Chain Strength:**
- 4-5: Operation proceeds with full Guild support. Community confirmation secured. Legal defense team briefed.
- 3: Operation proceeds conditionally. Additional documentation attempts during operation. Transfer more cautious.
- 1-2: Guild Master review required. Operation may be declined. If approved, must address documentation gap before transfer.

**Strengthening the Chain during operations:** Recovering documents found at the target location can upgrade Chain Strength mid-operation. This is a high-value secondary objective on most Guild jobs.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Research Specialist

You build the Provenance Cases. You have documented recoveries for thirty-one artifacts in six years with the Guild. You are currently working on a case that is at Strength 3 because the primary colonial records that would document the original taking were destroyed in a fire that you believe was deliberate. You have found three secondary sources that support the community's claim. You need one more document, which appears to exist in a private archive held by the family that currently holds the artifact. You are about to propose to Guild Master Rena that the operation include a secondary objective to access and copy that archive.

- **Drive:** *"Strengthen the Dawnspire Case from 3 to 4 before the operation, using the documentary evidence in the Carrell family's private archive."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

### The Operations Planner

You plan the operations. You are the person who reads the research file, visits the location, watches the guard schedules, and designs the approach that gets the team in and out with the artifact and without harming anyone. You have planned 211 operations in fourteen years. Your success rate is 94%. Your failure rate of 6% is all documented, all analyzed, and all incorporated into the methodology updates you write after each one. You are currently planning an operation at a private estate with a security system that is genuinely new to you and a secondary objective (the archive) that complicates the extraction timeline.

- **Drive:** *"Design an operation that achieves the primary recovery and the secondary archive access within a timeline that doesn't compromise either objective."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 4 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Community Liaison

You represent the Veth community that has been waiting for the Dawnspire Chalice for four years. You came to the Guild originally because the legal channels had produced nothing in twenty years of advocacy. You trust the Guild's process. You also carry the weight of your community's expectations, and you are the person who will have to explain any delay, any complication, or any outcome other than successful recovery. You have read the Provenance research. You believe it is correct. You are aware that the Guild Master has concerns about the Chain Strength that you cannot simply override with your certainty.

- **Drive:** *"Support the operation without overstepping your role, provide the community context that might strengthen the Chain Strength assessment, and manage your community's expectations through an operation timeline that is longer than anyone hoped."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Archive Problem:** The Dawnspire Chalice operation is approved at Chain Strength 3, conditional on a secondary archive access objective that the Research Specialist has identified as the key to Strength 4. The archive is in the same building as the Chalice, in a different wing. The guard schedule accommodates both objectives if the team moves efficiently. The complication is that Lord Carrell's legal team has filed a civil injunction naming the Guild as the anticipated party in an anticipated theft, which is an unusual legal move that the Legal Defense Fund lawyer describes as "creative" and "unfortunately not without some legal basis." The injunction does not prevent the operation. It does mean that any evidence the team finds and removes will be challenged in any subsequent legal proceeding. The Research Specialist needs physical access to the archive even if the documents cannot leave the building, which means the operation now includes a documentation photography element that adds time. The characters must execute an operation that is more complex than planned, under a legal framework that is changing around them, for a Provenance Case that is genuinely important to a community that has been waiting too long.

**Why It Matters:** The Antiquities Thieves Guild is about the relationship between law and ethics in situations where the law has historically protected the outcomes of illegal acts. The Guild has answered the question of what to do about this in a specific way: direct action, bounded by a code that attempts to prevent the action from becoming the same kind of harm it opposes. Whether that answer is adequate is a question the setting leaves open.
