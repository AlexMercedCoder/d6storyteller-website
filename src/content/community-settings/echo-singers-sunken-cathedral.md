## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Soundless Cathedral is a place of sacred acoustics. Every pillar, arch, and stained-glass window is tuned to specific musical pitches.

The order uses no weapons or hammers; all maintenance, defense, and architecture is performed through choral singing and pitch-tuning forks.

**Tone:** Eerie, musical, majestic, and gothic. Focuses on sound, resonance, discipline, and architectural beauty.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Soaring gothic arches made of glowing purple and blue quartz crystal reflecting off calm subterranean water. Robed singers standing in semi-circles.
2. **Sound:** Pure, reverberating vocal harmonies echoing through massive vaults. The chime of giant silver tuning forks. The terrifying crackle of quartz under acoustic stress.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Vibrations felt in the chest from low bass notes. Cold, smooth crystal surfaces. The clean smell of ozone and underwater mineral dust.

--

## 2. The Cathedral Vaults

**The Choir Spire:** The highest balcony where the Lead Cantors perform perpetual harmonic maintenance.

**The Resonating Chamber:** A spherical room where sound waves are amplified to shape raw crystal stone.

**The Abyssal Crypt:** The flooded lowest level where the discordant subterranean frequency originates.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Order of Echoes:** The singers who maintain the cathedral's acoustic structural integrity through perpetual song.

**The Silence Seekers:** Heretics who believe absolute silence is the true cosmic state and seek to shatter the cathedral crystals.

**The Abyssal Dissonance:** A sentient acoustic anomaly dwelling in the deep abyss that feeds on vocal harmony.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Harmonic Purity

Track **Harmonic Purity** from 0 to 6.

**Purity Effects:**
- **5-6 (Perfect Resonance):** Crystal walls glow bright. Vocal checks grant bonus success; physical structure self-repairs.
- **3-4 (Minor Dissonance):** Cracks appear in non-structural glass. Echoes become distorted.
- **1-2 (Severe Structural Stress):** Vault arches crumble. Sonic attacks deal double damage to all characters.
- **0 (Acoustic Shatter):** Crystal collapse. High-volume shockwave deafens characters and floods the cathedral.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Lead Cantor

You lead the choral chants and direct acoustic energy to mend broken structures.

- **Drive:** *"Maintain the perpetual song to keep the cathedral vaults intact."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 3

### The Tuning Specialist

You carry massive silver tuning forks to isolate and neutralize discordant frequencies.

- **Drive:** *"Identify and tune out the abyssal dissonance."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Sonic Sentinel

You use targeted vocal resonance blasts to repel intruders and silence heretics.

- **Drive:** *"Defend the choir spire from Silence Seekers."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Fractured Arch

During the High Solstice Choral Service, a massive wave of discordant frequency erupts from the Abyssal Crypt. The central vault arch cracks, Harmonic Purity drops to 2, and several Silence Seekers infiltrate the balcony with sound-dampening shrouds to kill the Lead Cantors.

The player characters must eliminate the heretics, use tuning forks to isolate the discordant frequency, and sing the restorative Master Chord before the main vault shatters into the abyssal lake.
