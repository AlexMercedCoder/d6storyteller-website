## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The investigation began as a straightforward location commission: find the missing pieces, establish their condition, arrange return. Six weeks in, the investigation has established that the pieces' disappearance was not a theft but a placement: they were moved from the vault by someone with authorized access, using authorized procedure, without any of the usual theft indicators. This is either an authorized action by someone with the authority to take it or an unauthorized action by someone with unauthorized access to authorized procedures. Both possibilities are politically significant in ways that the clients have not disclosed.

**Tone:** Political thriller with a conspiracy core. The investigation is real: the pieces are genuinely missing. The complication is that the people who hired the investigators may not want the investigation to succeed, or may want it to succeed in a specific direction that serves their interests.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The royal vault: examined with permission granted by the current government, which is the source of the first complication. The documentation room: the administrative records of every vault access in the past ten years, which requires permission to examine and which the investigators have been given access to in a form that appears to have gaps. The investigators' temporary office: established in a government building, which means it is certainly monitored.
2. **Sound:** The formal language of diplomatic investigation: every statement carefully qualified, every meeting conducted with the awareness that it is being documented by multiple parties. The specific cadence of political communication when a speaker is managing what they reveal: the gaps and redirections that experienced investigators read as substance rather than silence.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The royal vault itself: enormous, well-maintained, and currently containing five of seven pieces in their documented positions. The two display cases that are empty have been cleaned recently, which is either standard maintenance and timing coincidence or evidence that they were cleaned after the pieces were removed.

## 2. The Investigation

**The Official Investigation** is the public-facing commission: hired by a government ministry, given access to specified records and locations, and expected to produce a report. The investigators' actual assessment diverges significantly from what the commission appeared to expect.

**The Unofficial Investigation** is what the official investigation has become: the pursuit of the actual answer, which requires methods and access that exceed the official commission. The investigators are managing the gap between what they have been authorized to do and what they need to do.

**The Documentation Gap** is the central evidence problem: the vault access records for a specific forty-day period have been reviewed by two investigators and produced different counts of access events. The difference of three entries, each corresponding to a date when an authorized official had documented access, suggests that the records have been altered. This finding has not been shared with the clients.

**The Three Suspects** are the three officials with authorized access during the relevant period. One is a current cabinet minister. One is the senior royal house administrator. One is an official whose position has since been abolished and who currently works in a different ministry.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Government Ministry** that commissioned the investigation is internally divided about what outcome they want. The minister who signed the commission wanted genuine answers. At least two senior officials in the same ministry would prefer the investigation to reach a specific conclusion that protects a specific person, or alternatively to not reach any conclusion.

**The Royal House** has its own interests, which are constitutionally distinct from the government's. The royal house administrator's position in the access records makes the royal house a potentially compromised party in its own right, which affects who the investigation can report to with confidence.

**The Opposition Party** has heard rumors. They have not yet acted on them. If they receive confirmation that constitutional instruments are missing and the government has been covering this for six months, the political consequences would be severe and rapid. The investigators are aware that their findings, if released to the wrong party at the wrong time, become a political weapon rather than a factual record.

**The Former Official** whose position was abolished is the investigation's most accessible suspect and the one with the least current institutional protection. They are also the one who, when interviewed, provided information that was more forthcoming than the investigators expected. The question of whether this is genuine cooperation or strategic misdirection is unresolved.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Political Exposure

Track **Political Exposure Rating** from 0 to 6.

| Rating | Operational Status |
|--------|-------------------|
| 0-1 | Quiet investigation. No political actors aware of findings. |
| 2-3 | Some awareness. One or two political actors know the investigation is finding something. Careful interference. |
| 4-5 | Active interference. Multiple actors attempting to redirect or slow the investigation. |
| 6 | Maximum exposure. Formal intervention. Commission revoked or investigation officially concluded with directed findings. |

**Raising Exposure (+1 each):**
- An interview subject reports the conversation to a political superior
- The investigation accesses records or locations beyond the specified commission
- A finding is shared with a party outside the immediate investigation team
- A political actor learns the investigation's current direction

**Lowering Exposure (-1 each, minimum 0):**
- A specific political actor is satisfied with a controlled partial disclosure
- The investigation produces a finding that serves a political actor's interests (creating alignment)
- The investigation makes a visible public move that misdirects attention from its actual direction
- Time passes without significant new findings becoming known

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Lead Investigator

You have been hired to find the Crown Jewels. You have found, instead, a political situation that is significantly more complicated than the commission described. You are managing four specific pieces of information that you have not shared with your clients: the documentation gap, the three suspect identifications, the former official's forthcoming interview, and your assessment that at least one of your clients would prefer you not find the actual answer. You are continuing the investigation because that is what you were hired to do, and because the constitutional implications of missing crown jewels are significant enough that "the clients would prefer this didn't succeed" is not a sufficient reason to stop.

- **Drive:** *"Complete the investigation with enough factual grounding to produce a report that can survive political challenge, while keeping Exposure low enough that the investigation is not shut down before you reach that point."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

### The Political Liaison

You were assigned by the government ministry to facilitate the investigation's access. This is a standard oversight role: you ensure investigators get what the commission authorizes and nothing beyond it. You have since learned that the commission's actual purpose was more complicated than the role description suggested, and that you are in a position that is either a liability or an asset depending on decisions you are about to make. You have access to information that the investigators do not, and you have obligations to parties whose interests are not aligned.

- **Drive:** *"Determine what your actual obligation is in a situation where your professional duty to the commission and your knowledge of what it is actually for are in conflict, and act on that determination before the investigation reaches a finding that forces the decision for you."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### The Former Official

You are the third named suspect: the official whose position was abolished. You spoke more freely in your interview than the investigators expected because you have been waiting for two years for someone to ask the right questions. You know what happened to the Crown Jewels. You did not take them. You know who did, and you know why, and you have been unable to report it through official channels because the person who took them is the person who controls the official channels you would use to report it. You are considering giving the investigators what you know, and you are weighing that consideration against what you believe will happen to you if you do.

- **Drive:** *"Give the investigators what they need to complete the investigation accurately, in a way that provides you with enough protection that the person who controls the channels cannot prevent what comes next."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Former Official's Decision:** The former official has contacted the lead investigator for a second interview. The content of this interview, based on the informal signal the former official provided, will include the information that resolves the investigation. The Political Exposure Rating is currently at 4, which means at least two political actors are aware that the investigation is finding something significant. The second interview, if it produces the expected information, will need to be documented in a way that is legally durable and politically survivable. The investigators must conduct the interview, document the information appropriately, and assess how to present the findings in a way that produces constitutional accountability rather than simply becoming another political tool in a conflict that was already underway before the Crown Jewels disappeared.

**Why It Matters:** The Crown Jewels Affair is about the relationship between investigation and truth in political contexts where the truth is inconvenient to powerful parties. The investigators cannot control what happens with their findings. They can control the quality and grounding of those findings: whether they are accurate enough to survive the political challenge that will inevitably come. The setting is about the specific discipline of factual investigation in contexts designed to discourage it.
