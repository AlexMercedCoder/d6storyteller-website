I don't ask questions that clients don't answer. ## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

Marta's operation occupies a specific niche in the city's criminal economy: she handles goods that no one else will touch, processes transactions that no one else can manage, and maintains a client list that represents twenty-two years of professional discretion. She does not steal. She does not hire thieves. She receives what people bring her, pays fairly, and moves it to where it goes. The specific professionalism of this work is what has kept her safe: she is not a threat to anyone who is not a direct client, and she is useful to nearly everyone.

**Tone:** Low-key crime drama with a building threat. The setting is quiet, deliberate, and focused on the specific competence of someone who has survived two decades in a dangerous industry by being very good at what she does and very careful about what she takes on.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Fence's receiving room: a back space behind a legitimate import business that serves as operational cover. Clean, organized, with specific storage solutions for different item categories. The inventory log: a physical record kept in Marta's personal shorthand that no one else can fully read. The front of the import business: maintained to the standard of the neighborhood, staffed by two legitimate employees who have no knowledge of the back operation.
2. **Sound:** The Fence's operational sound: quiet and careful. Clients are admitted one at a time. Transactions happen without excess discussion. The specific vocabulary Marta uses with long-term clients is minimal because they understand each other. New clients speak more because they are nervous, which Marta reads and uses.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The goods themselves: Marta handles everything herself, because her ability to assess value and authenticity is her primary professional skill. The specific smell of the back room: items of various ages and origins, which experienced fences learn to parse. The package that arrived without explanation: an ordinary wooden crate of medium size, sealed with a specific type of resin that Marta has not seen before and that is not, as far as she can determine, primarily designed to preserve the contents.

## 2. The Operation

**The Receiving Room** is where Marta evaluates incoming goods and meets with clients. The room has one entrance, one exit, a specific light arrangement that allows Marta to assess items without clients reading her reaction, and no chairs for clients, because Marta has found that clients who are comfortable sit longer than clients who are standing.

**The Storage System** is organized by category, time of receipt, and the specific clients associated with each item. Marta knows exactly where every item is and exactly whose it is. Her inventory knowledge is kept in her head and in the shorthand log. The log is the only physical record of the operation's business.

**The Network** is the infrastructure of buyers, intermediaries, and specialist contacts that allows items to move out. Marta has twenty-two years of relationships. She knows which buyers will take specific categories, what the fair market rate is for goods that do not have a standard market, and which intermediaries are reliable enough to use for sensitive transactions.

**The Legitimate Cover** is the import business, which is real and operational. It provides income, premises, and an explanation for Marta's business meetings. The two employees know their employer runs a second business from the back and have concluded that it is preferable not to know more than that.

## 3. Factions

**Marta** is the central figure rather than a faction, but her institutional relationships define the setting's dynamics. She has a long-term professional relationship with the city's Watch's organized crime division, specifically with the detective who runs the unit: a relationship that is not cooperation but not hostility, a professional acknowledgment that each party has constraints and interests that the other respects.

**The Criminal Economy** is the ecosystem Marta operates within. Major operators need fencing services. Marta's independence from any single organization is valuable: she serves multiple operators without being controlled by any of them. This independence is also the reason two different operators have, at different times, attempted to bring her operation under their control. Both attempts failed in ways that discourage further attempts.

**The Watch** has three separate open investigations connected to Marta's operation, each at a different stage. She knows about two of them. The third is the new one, opened in response to a missing persons report that is adjacent to the anonymous package.

**The Two Interested Parties** are the unknown factors. One contacted Marta through a specific communication channel that only twelve current clients know. One contacted her through a method that is completely unfamiliar and suggests either a new client or a party that has obtained information about her operation from somewhere. Neither will explain what the package contains.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Heat Level

Track **Heat Level** from 0 to 6.

| Level | Operational Status |
|-------|-------------------|
| 0-1 | Clean. No active investigation. Normal operations. |
| 2-3 | Background attention. Watch is aware but not actively pursuing. Standard caution. |
| 4-5 | Active investigation. Specific Watch attention on the operation. Operational pause recommended. |
| 6 | Imminent action. Raid in hours to days. Emergency protocol required. |

**Raising Heat (+1 each):**
- A client is arrested and questioned (regardless of whether they talk)
- A transaction is witnessed by an uninvolved party
- A stolen item is publicly reported and its description matches current inventory
- Watch surveillance is identified near the operation
- The anonymous package situation attracts external attention

**Lowering Heat (-1 each, minimum 0):**
- Move a significant item out of inventory quickly and cleanly
- A Watch investigation closes or is redirected
- Provide the Watch contact with something useful (creates a complex debt relationship)
- A period of operational quiet (no incoming or outgoing for 30 days, maximum one per story arc)

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Fence

You are Marta. You have been doing this for twenty-two years. You have handled approximately 4,000 transactions. You have had three clients arrested: none of them talked about you, because talking about Marta is worse for your career prospects than whatever the Watch had on you. The current situation is the first in twenty-two years where you genuinely do not know what you are holding, cannot identify who sent it, and cannot determine what either interested party actually wants. You are handling this the way you handle everything: methodically, carefully, and with the specific patience of someone who has learned that the most dangerous situations are the ones that feel like they require immediate action.

- **Drive:** *"Identify what the package is, determine which of the two interested parties is safer to deal with, and resolve the situation before Heat Level increases to the point where the Watch's attention includes the package."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 4 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Watch Detective

You are Bren. You run the Watch's organized crime division. You have an ongoing professional accommodation with Marta that you have never committed to paper because committing it to paper would require you to explain it. The accommodation is simple: Marta does not handle goods connected to violent crimes, and in exchange you have not moved against her operation with the evidence you have accumulated. You are currently managing a missing persons case that is adjacent to Marta's operation in ways you cannot yet specify, and you are trying to determine whether the adjacency is coincidental or causal before you decide whether the accommodation still applies.

- **Drive:** *"Resolve the missing persons case, determine whether Marta's operation is involved, and decide what the answer means for the professional accommodation before your supervisor asks about the case's status."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### The Interested Third Party

You represent the second interested party: not the one who used the established communication channel, but the one whose contact method Marta found unfamiliar. You obtained information about Marta's operation through a source you are not going to disclose. The package is important to your employer. You are authorized to offer a significant fee for its return and to use other means if the fee is declined. You have, so far, only offered the fee, because you are not certain Marta knows who your employer is and you are not ready to establish that connection.

- **Drive:** *"Recover the package for your employer, without confirming who your employer is, without using means that would raise Marta's Heat Level to the point where the Watch becomes involved."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Anonymous Package:** The package has been in Marta's storage for six days. The first interested party contacted her on day two. The second on day four. On day five, a Watch detective she has a professional relationship with mentioned, casually, that a missing persons case had produced a lead in her neighborhood. On day six, she noticed that the resin sealing the package has a very slight chemical property she recognized: it is the same composition as a preservation resin used on a specific category of religious artifacts that the city's largest cultural institution reported stolen eight months ago. She has not opened the package. She now has a hypothesis about what it is, two parties who want it, an adjacent Watch investigation, and a professional reputation that depends on resolving this correctly. The characters are her most trusted current associates, and she is bringing them into the situation because she needs help that requires more than one person.

**Why It Matters:** The Last Fence is about the specific ethics and intelligence of professional criminal work as distinct from criminal violence. Marta operates by a code that is genuinely different from the broader criminal economy around her, and that code has kept her safe and functional for twenty-two years. The anonymous package is the test of what that code means when circumstances don't fit its normal framework.
