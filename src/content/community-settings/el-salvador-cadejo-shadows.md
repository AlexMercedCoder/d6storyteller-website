## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** In 17th-century El Salvador (the historic realm of Cuscatlán), the eruption of the Izalco volcano awakens the legendary **Cadejo**—twin phantom hounds woven into Pipil and Salvadoran mythology. As Spanish landlords expand indigo plantations and force Indigenous villagers into hardship, the White Cadejo appears to guard night travelers, while the monstrous Black Cadejo hunts oppressors and drunks. Players embody a secret brotherhood of Pipil spirit-keepers who channel these dual entities to defend their communities.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The ominous red glow of Izalco's lava rivers against a pitch-black midnight sky; brilliant indigo dye vats glowing deep violet in village courtyards; the spectral white luminescence of the guardian Cadejo's eyes.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic clicking of ghost claws on dirt paths; the deep, rumble of volcanic earth tremors; the haunting whistling of the Siguanaba along riverbanks.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The choking stench of sulfur and burning volcanic ash; the sweet perfume of night-blooming jasmine along mountain roads; the cold, prickling chill of a spirit hound passing through your shadow.

**The Core Tension:** Justice vs. Vengeance. Protecting your people with the White Cadejo requires mercy and restraint, but unleashing the Black Cadejo delivers devastating revenge at the cost of your own humanity.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

El Salvador's cultural identity is deeply rooted in Pipil (Nahuatl-speaking) heritage and rich oral folklore. The legend of *El Cadejo* is one of the most enduring myths in Central America, representing the eternal duality of protection and temptation.

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** The Cadejo and Siguanaba legends are living folklore in El Salvador. This setting treats these traditional tales as expressions of community resilience and moral storytelling against colonial exploitation.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Guardians of the White Chain (Iztac Cadejo)
Protectors who use spiritual discipline to summon the White Cadejo for defense, healing, and guidance.
- **Cultural Association:** Pipil healers, village midwives, and night navigators.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"The White Cadejo walks beside me whenever I carry medicine through the dark"*
  - *"My voice can calm a panic-stricken crowd before the tremors begin"*

### The Avenging Pack (Tliltic Cadejo)
Rebels who invoke the Black Cadejo's wrath against abusive plantation masters.
- **Cultural Association:** Mountain guerrillas, indigo workers, and nocturnal vigilantes.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"The smell of sulfur follows my footsteps when justice is denied"*
  - *"I have faced Spanish steel without flinching because my shadow carries teeth"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Cadejo Alignment

Characters track a **Dual Spirit Meter** (-3 White / +3 Black):
- **White Alignment (-3 to -1):** +1d6 to Presence and Mind. Can project protective aura shields.
- **Balanced (0):** Normal rules. Access to both minor spirit abilities at the cost of 1 Momentum.
- **Black Alignment (+1 to +3):** +1d6 to Force and Finesse. Terrifying presence, but failure summons the Black Cadejo to attack friend and foe alike.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Pipil Healer (Iztac Initiate)
- **Concept:** A village apothecary protected by the White Cadejo.
- **Descriptors:** *Master of highland medicinal herbs*, *Protected by the White Hound*, *Respected across three villages*.
- **Drive:** *"No innocent person shall perish on these roads tonight."*
- **Flaw:** *"Reluctant to harm even those who threaten us."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### Archetype 2: The Indigo Rebel (Tliltic Champion)
- **Concept:** An escaped plantation laborer who wields the Black Cadejo's vengeance.
- **Descriptors:** *Stained indigo palms that strike like iron*, *Fears no night terror*, *Knows every mountain hideout in Izalco*.
- **Drive:** *"Break every chain on the indigo vats."*
- **Flaw:** *"Quick to anger when authority speaks."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

### Archetype 3: The Mountain Priest
- **Concept:** An elder Pipil astronomer who reads volcanic signs.
- **Descriptors:** *Speaks fluent Pipil-Nahuatl*, *Read the smoke omens of Izalco*, *Keeper of sacred jade relics*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the balance between fire and earth."*
- **Flaw:** *"Detached from immediate human troubles."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 4 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Burning of Izalco

- **Framing:** The Izalco volcano erupts as a greedy encomendero attempts to seize village farmland.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Escorting villagers past a lava flow under Black Cadejo attack.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Ash cloud engulfing the valley in 5 beats.
  3. *Opportunity:* Calling the White Cadejo to guide the caravan through a hidden lava tube.
- **Antagonist:** *Don Diego de Alvarado* (Cruel Indigo Landlord).
  - *Traits:* Well-armed guards, ruthless greed.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Overconfident / ☐ Guard Force Broken / ☐ Driven Away.
