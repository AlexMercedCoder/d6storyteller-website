## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The *Vanguard* is a closed ecosystem. Everything is recycled: air, water, organic matter, and even memories. The ship's culture is obsessed with conservation, duty, and lineage. Because resources are finite, strict birth quotas are enforced by the Population Council, and every citizen is assigned a career role at age sixteen based on genetic aptitude and deck needs.

**Tone:** Claustrophobic, sociological, and morally gray. The setting focuses on human nature under extreme isolation, the weight of inherited destiny, and the ethics of institutional secrets.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The endless artificial curve of the hull ceiling above, lined with glowing LED strips that simulate a 24-hour day-night cycle. The rust-stained metal bulkheads of the Low Decks contrasted with the polished brass and clean glass of Command.
2. **Sound:** The omnipresent, low-frequency thrum of the ion drive array echoing through every bulkhead. The hissing of atmospheric scrubbers. The ringing of shift-change bells.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The warm, damp humidity of the Hydroponics decks smelling of wet soil and spirulina. The metallic taste of thrice-recycled water. The constant faint vibration underfoot.

--

## 2. The Decks

**Command Ring:** Located at the spinning hub of the ship. Home to the Officer Corps, sensor analysts, and the Executive Council. Enforces laws through the Enforcer Corps.

**Mid-Decks (Hydroponics & Manufacturing):** The economic backbone. Millions of vertical farming racks provide food and oxygen, while manufacturing shops process recycled metal.

**Low-Decks (Drive Engineering & Waste Management):** Warm, noisy, and hazardous. Home to the maintenance crews who keep the reactor and reaction mass drives operating.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Executive Council:** Dedicated to maintaining order and completing the mission at all costs. They believe revealing the truth about Epsilon Eridani would cause irreversible societal collapse.

**The Truth Seekers:** An underground network of engineers and archivists who suspect Command is hiding navigation logs. They seek to breach the encrypted Central Archive and expose the truth to all decks.

**The Earth Return League:** A religious-political cult in the Low-Decks who believe the mission was a mistake. They advocate flipping the ship and burning back toward Earth, despite the physical impossibility of such a maneuver.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Social Tension

Track **Social Tension** from 0 to 6.

**Tension Modifiers:**
- Public arrest of a popular deck leader: +1 Tension
- Ration cuts or environmental system failure: +1 Tension
- De-escalation through successful mediation or compromise: -1 Tension

**Tension Thresholds:**
- **0-2:** Orderly ship operation. Routine police patrols.
- **3-4:** Strikes, unauthorized deck blockades, widespread curfew violations.
- **5-6:** Active sabotage, armed clashes between Enforcers and Deck Factions, life support compromised.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Deck Enforcer

You were born in Command and trained to keep order across all decks. You carry a shock-baton and an iron sense of duty, but you are beginning to notice inconsistencies in Command's official bulletins.

- **Drive:** *"Maintain order without destroying the fragile peace between decks."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Hydro Archivist

You tend the seed vaults and historical records in Mid-Decks. You discovered a discrepancies in the star-mapping telemetry logs from seventy years ago.

- **Drive:** *"Uncover the true destination coordinates without being recycled for treason."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### The Low-Deck Mechanic

You grew up next to the main drive coils. You can fix any pump or relay with scrap wire, and you have the trust of the workers who keep the ship alive.

- **Drive:** *"Protect your deck from unfair rationing and Enforcer overreach."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Encrypted Telemetry

A rogue sensor technician from Command has fled down to Hydroponics carrying a hard-drive spindle containing unedited optical scans of the Epsilon Eridani system. Enforcer squads have sealed off the sector, while Truth Seeker cells move to extract the technician.

The players must locate the technician first, decide whether to release or suppress the data, and prevent the standoff from escalating into a ship-wide riot.
