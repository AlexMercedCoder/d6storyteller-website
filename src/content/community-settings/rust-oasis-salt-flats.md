## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Great Salt Flats were once a thriving inland sea, now dried into a blinding, white desert under a merciless sun. Iron-Well was constructed out of rusted shipping containers, scavenged tanker trucks, and solar stills anchored to a central geothermal deep-well pump.

**Tone:** Gritty, brutal survival, mad-max wasteland aesthetic. High tension around resource scarcity, harsh environmental hazards, and desperate moral choices.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Blinding white salt reflecting harsh sunlight. Rusted iron towers against a cloudless cyan sky. Dust-rig vehicles with spiked tires.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic, groaning thump of the geothermal water pump. The howling desert wind whipping salt dust against metal plating. The crackle of desert radios.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Salt-caked skin. The burning heat of exposed metal. The sweet, metallic taste of purified well water.

--

## 2. The Wasteland Landmarks

**The Wellhead:** The central geothermal pump room, guarded 24/7 by heavy turrets.

**The Scrap Bazaar:** A chaotic market under stretched canvas sails where scavengers trade pre-collapse tech for water chits.

**The Salt Wastes:** Miles of cracked white crust hiding sinkholes, unexploded warheads, and marauder ambush posts.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Wellkeepers:** The ruling junta of Iron-Well who control the water supply and enforce strict rationing laws.

**The Sun-Warriors:** A violent warlord faction driving motorized scrap rigs, seeking to seize the well for themselves.

**The Dune Crawlers:** Nomadic scavengers who harvest pre-collapse ruins buried deep in the salt crust.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Hydration Depletion

Track **Hydration Depletion** from 0 to 6.

**Depletion Triggers:**
- Travel through Salt Wastes without shade: +1 Depletion per hour
- Intense combat or physical exertion in heat: +1 Depletion
- Consuming a ration of purified water: -2 Depletion

**Depletion Levels:**
- **0-1:** Fully hydrated. Optimal physical condition.
- **2-3:** Mild thirst. Chapped lips, slight fatigue.
- **4-5:** Severe dehydration. Disadvantage on physical Force and Finesse checks.
- **6:** Heatstroke collapse. Incapacitated until rehydrated.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Rig Driver

You pilot a heavily armored scrap vehicle equipped with harpoons and reinforced bumpers.

- **Drive:** *"Keep the rig running and convoy routes open."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Salt Scavenger

You know how to read the salt crust, avoid sinkholes, and unearth pre-collapse technology.

- **Drive:** *"Find the motherlode pre-war tech cache buried beneath the salt."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Well Technician

You maintain the ancient geothermal pumps and water purification filters.

- **Drive:** *"Keep the water flowing and prevent pump filter contamination."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Filter Contamination

The main purification filter at Iron-Well begins failing, tainting the water supply with sulfur and toxic minerals. The only replacement filter unit is located inside a submerged pre-collapse research bunker fifteen miles into Sun-Warrior territory.

The players must outfit a scrap rig, navigate the treacherous Salt Wastes, breach the bunker, and return with the replacement filter before Iron-Well's stored water runs out in forty-eight hours.
