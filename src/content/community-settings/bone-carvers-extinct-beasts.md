## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Frost-Spire Valleys are a stark, snow-covered realm where human settlements are built literally inside the ribcages of ancient leviathans. 

Bone carving is both an art and a sacred magic. Carvers use obsidian chisels and diamond-tipped hand drills to unlock the spiritual energy trapped in fossilized marrow.

**Tone:** Tribal, mythic, visceral, and primal. Blends ice-age survival with runic magic and beast lore.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Towering white ribcages jutting out of snow drifts like gothic arches. Fires reflecting off polished ivory blades covered in glowing blue runes.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic *tap-tap-tap* of obsidian chisels on hard bone. The howling glacial wind through rib-canyon formations. The deep hum of activated bone runes.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Cold smooth ivory, fine bone dust coating hands and lungs, the smell of burning pine pitch and warm bone grease.

--

## 2. The Fossil Valleys

**The Great Rib-Hall:** The central tribal village built inside the ribcage of an ancient 300-foot mega-mammoth.

**The Marrow Mines:** Deep glacial shafts where carvers extract rare, un-fossilized marrow-stone.

**The Tooth Quarry:** A jagged cliffside littered with ten-foot dire-serpent fangs used for heavy weapon forging.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Carvers Guild:** Traditional artisans who follow sacred hunting and carving rites to honor the extinct beasts.

**The Frost-Tribe Hunters:** Warriors who wield runic bone weapons to defend the valley from glacier trolls and dire wolves.

**The Marrow Breakers:** Outlaw scrap-scavengers who use explosives to shatter ancient skeletons for quick bone harvests, destroying the spirit resonance.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Marrow Resonance

Track **Marrow Resonance** from 0 to 6.

**Resonance Modifiers:**
- Carving new runes into bone gear: +1 Resonance
- Channeling beast power during combat check: +1 Resonance
- Quenching bone in sacred herbal oil: -1 Resonance

**Resonance States:**
- **0-1:** Inert bone item. Standard durability and damage.
- **2-3:** Awakened item. Bonus damage; faint animalistic growls heard by wielder.
- **4-5:** High Resonance. Beast ability unlocked (e.g., mammoth strength, serpent venom immunity); user takes on physical traits (tusk growth, slitted eyes).
- **6:** Beast Possession. Wielder temporarily loses control, acting with pure animal predatory instinct.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Master Carver

You use obsidian chisels to shape raw fossil bone into runic weapons and protective amulets.

- **Drive:** *"Craft a legendary runic blade from the warm mammoth marrow core."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Bone Hunter

You track down un-excavated beast skeletons buried beneath shifting glaciers.

- **Drive:** *"Locate the legendary dire-serpent graveyard in the upper peaks."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Spirit Keeper

You conduct rituals to calm the restless spirits of extinct megafauna before bone harvesting.

- **Drive:** *"Appease the spirit of the Great Mammoth before the Marrow Breakers anger it."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Warm Marrow

During an excavation deep in the Glacier Shaft, carvers unearth the heart-bone of a mega-mammoth that is still warm and throbbing with vital energy. The Marrow Breakers launch a surprise raid on the shaft with black-powder explosives to steal the heart-bone.

The players must defend the excavation site, prevent the explosives from shattering the fossilized skeleton, and resolve the sudden spiritual awakening of the mammoth spirit.
