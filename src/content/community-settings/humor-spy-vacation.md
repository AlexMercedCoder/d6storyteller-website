## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The best spy in the world cannot turn it off. Also, the resort has a genuine intelligence operation running in its conference facilities and the spy is technically on vacation.

The world of **Recon & Relaxation** follows Bureau of Covert Intelligence operatives who have been ordered to take mandatory vacation after accumulated operational stress. The order is genuine, the resort is real, and the crisis the operatives find is also real -- which creates the central dilemma: is solving it a recovery failure or a professional triumph?

The comedy emerges from the gap between what vacation looks like (leisure, restoration, genuine rest) and what an elite spy experiences as they try to achieve it (pattern recognition running continuously, threat assessment automated to the point of autonomy, social interactions defaulting to interrogation technique).

**Tone:** Equal parts spy thriller and recovery comedy. The missions are real and well-designed. The vacation failure is also real and well-observed. The question of whether these people are okay is genuine and doesn't fully resolve.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A luxury beach resort that is objectively beautiful and is being experienced by the players through a layer of professional threat assessment: the sightlines from each lounge chair, the communication pattern of the resort staff, the guests who make arrival and departure times that don't match the shuttle schedule, and one poolside encounter that is genuinely just two strangers talking, which the player won't stop monitoring.
2. **Sound:** The genuinely peaceful sound of a beach vacation interrupted by the internal pattern-matching of a spy's brain: every conversation near the pool assessed for keywords, the room service order heard through the wall deconstructed for information content, and the moment of genuine quiet at 4am when the operative has been awake and monitoring for six hours and realizes they've missed another night of sleep.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Salt air and sunscreen, genuinely pleasant, experienced while scanning for threats. The soft texture of a beach towel. The weight of a cocktail that is being consumed as directed but not fully enjoyed. The unexpected warmth of genuine connection with another guest before the professional brain takes over and starts running a background assessment.

**The Core Tension:** The genuine need for rest conflicts with the genuine crisis. Treating the crisis as an excuse to avoid the recovery is also a real possibility. Both can be true simultaneously.

## 2. The Resort

### The Legitimate Operation
The Crystal Cove Resort is genuinely a luxury vacation destination. Most of its guests are genuinely on vacation. The staff are genuinely hospitality professionals. The beach is genuinely nice.

### The Intelligence Operation
The resort's conference facilities are being used by a group of very interesting guests who have not disclosed their actual purpose. Their communication patterns, arrival timing, and security posture indicate something significant. The operative's professional assessment is that this requires action. The therapist's assessment is that this is the pattern reasserting itself.

### The Other Guests
A varied collection of genuinely vacationing people who keep having conversations with the operative that the operative cannot fully allow themselves to experience because their pattern-recognition is constantly running. Some of these people are interesting and the connections available to them are real, if the operative could access them.

### The Resort Staff
Good at their jobs, coordinated in the way that good hospitality staff are coordinated, and absolutely not running surveillance of any kind. The operatives monitor them anyway.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **Day Three:** The operatives have identified the intelligence operation. Protocol requires they report it and await direction. Reporting means disclosure of their location and the end of anonymity. It also means Bureau involvement and probably the end of the vacation. They have 24 hours to decide whether this meets the threshold for active reporting.

2. **The Fellow Traveler:** Another guest is clearly also a spy. They're from a different agency. They're also on mandatory vacation. Both are monitoring each other, both recognize that the other recognizes them, and both are simultaneously failing at vacation. Can these people help each other? Can they trust each other? Should they?

3. **The Genuine Connection:** An operative has accidentally formed a genuine friendship with another guest, who has no intelligence background and no connection to the resort operation. The friendship is real. The operation is real. The guest is in a location that makes them vulnerable if the situation escalates. Protecting them while maintaining cover while technically on vacation is complicated.

4. **The Extraction:** The intelligence operation at the resort has an asset who needs to get out and has targeted this vacation location specifically because they knew an operative would be here. This was not an accident. This vacation was planned. The therapy referral may have been compromised. Everything is more complicated than it appeared.

5. **The Last Day:** The vacation ends tomorrow. The resort operation has not been resolved. The operatives must decide: report and return to duty, complete the vacation with the operation unresolved, or act independently on the final day and reframe whatever happens as the conclusion of a vacation.
