## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Dragons are the most powerful beings in the known world. They are also, it turns out, extremely interested in protecting their financial assets through comprehensive insurance products.

**Drakenvault Insurance Solutions** was founded in the Second Age by an actuary who realized that the biggest underserved market in the world was the beings creating the most insurable events. Three hundred years later, Drakenvault insures 40% of dragons in the northern realms, handles claims for the communities those dragons affect, and maintains the world's most complete database of draconic behavior patterns.

The players work for Drakenvault: as claims adjusters (visiting villages that have been incinerated), underwriters (assessing new dragon clients), field agents (the people who actually go talk to the dragons), or analysts (the people who calculate premiums from a safe distance).

**Tone:** This is played entirely straight. The dragons are real and dangerous. The insurance is real and valuable. The comedy emerges from the completely professional application of actuarial science to catastrophic magical creatures.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A claims site where a village is still smoldering, a Drakenvault adjuster in professional attire carefully measuring scorch radius and damage depth with official instruments, the dragon watching from a nearby hill with the expression of an entity that knows exactly what percentage of this is covered, and a property owner watching both of them trying to figure out who to complain to first.
2. **Sound:** The professional tone of a claims conversation between an adjuster and a dragon client (the dragon speaks; the adjuster takes notes; the notes are fire-resistant), the specific language of policy terms being read aloud, and the very particular sound of an actuary doing mental calculations when confronted with a damage scenario they had placed in the "theoretically possible" column.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Fire damage everywhere, professionally assessed and documented, the company's standard flame-resistant field equipment (Drakenvault invests in good equipment), policy documents on absolutely premium parchment that has been enchanted against fire (there was a learning period early in the company's history), and the cold professional comfort of a claims number being assigned to your disaster.

**The Core Tension:** The players serve both sides of the policy -- the communities affected by dragons and the dragons themselves. Both have legitimate claims. Both can be difficult clients. Both are dangerous to cross.

## 2. Drakenvault Products

### Community Coverage Lines
- **Fire Damage Standard:** Covers damage from territorial dragon events up to 5 miles from a declared lair
- **Treasure Recovery Assistance:** Covers costs of recovering items taken into hoard during territorial events
- **Evacuation Reimbursement:** Covers documented displacement from active dragon zones
- **Being-Eaten Liability:** Life insurance for the statistically small but non-negligible risk of direct draconic consumption

### Dragon Client Coverage Lines
- **Hoard Theft Protection:** Comprehensive coverage for adventurer-initiated hoard reduction events
- **Rider Liability:** Covers damages caused by unauthorized individuals using the dragon as transportation
- **Territorial Dispute Coverage:** Legal defense costs for kingdom disputes over claimed territories
- **Princess Acquisition:** Complex product; significant underwriting required; available in select markets

### The Actuarial Department
The analysts who make Drakenvault function. They have developed the world's most comprehensive understanding of draconic behavior, patterns, and risk factors. They have never, to Drakenvault's knowledge, met an actual dragon. They prefer it this way.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The New Client:** Drakenvault has an opportunity to underwrite the most powerful dragon in the southern range. The field assessment requires an actual site visit. The dragon is cooperative but the cooperative visit of a dragon is still a visit of a dragon. Underwriting requires 14 specific data points. Gather all 14.

2. **The Disputed Claim:** A community is claiming damages from a dragon event. The dragon disputes the claim, insisting the event was in response to community provocation. Both have documentation. Determining the truth requires investigation in an area both sides consider their territory.

3. **The Policy Review:** A dragon client is up for their annual policy review and the actuarial team has flagged "concerning behavior changes" in the pattern data. The field agent must conduct the review, deliver the premium increase news, and navigate the dragon's response.

4. **The Competitor:** A rival insurance firm has begun offering dragon coverage at below-market rates with extremely aggressive claims handling (they simply don't pay). Drakenvault's clients are switching. The competitor's model is unsustainable but the damage to Drakenvault's market position is real and immediate.

5. **The Catastrophic Loss:** The largest single dragon event in recorded Drakenvault history just occurred. The claim would exceed the company's current reserves. Investigation of the event reveals it may have been preventable -- by action that Drakenvault had information about and did not share because it would have violated another client's policy terms.
