> *"Current situation update: we have recovered 2,847 of the 4,000 affected doses. Of the remaining 1,153, we have identified 89 doses that have already been consumed. The 89 affected individuals are all experiencing the side effect without known health risk. We are managing three situations that have progressed beyond routine: a judge who consumed the elixir yesterday and conducted an entire afternoon session in warbler, which the court stenographer apparently transcribed faithfully but cannot read back; a diplomatic reception at which two of the affected individuals were translators, which has created an international incident the Foreign Office is handling; and a bard who has taken the side effect and released a song in the new format that is, according to our cultural impact consultant, 'objectively beautiful and completely incomprehensible to anyone who doesn't winter on the northern coast.' The bard is declining recall support and requesting a second dose."*
> - Vitalis Alchemical Corporation, Recall Response Team Situation Report, Hour 36

## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

Vitalis has been in business for eighty years. They have issued four recalls in that time, all managed cleanly. Lot 7443 is their most complex recall because the side effect, while not harmful, is extremely public. A person speaking warbler in a courtroom attracts attention. The company's Recall Response Team is experienced, well-resourced, and handling a situation that is simultaneously manageable and continuously generating new complications.

**Tone:** Crisis comedy with genuine stakes. The side effect is funny. The recall logistics are genuine work. The setting rewards characters who can manage both the absurdity and the professionalism simultaneously, because the company needs both.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Recall Response Center: a commandeered conference room at Vitalis headquarters, now functioning as a logistics hub with maps of distribution routes, dose tracking boards, and a wall-mounted Severity Rating display that the team is working very hard to keep at 2. The field recall team: employees in company uniforms with the company's recall authorization documentation, visiting apothecaries, inns, and adventuring supply shops with the systematic thoroughness of people on a time-sensitive mission. The affected individuals: normal-looking people who, when they speak, produce sounds that birds in the vicinity respond to with interest.
2. **Sound:** The specific sound of the side effect: not bird sounds, exactly, but speech that has taken on the tonal qualities of bird language, which is melodic and structured in ways that human speech is not and which is genuinely pleasant to listen to even when it makes communication impossible. The field team's coordination calls: rapid, precise, and occasionally interrupted by the birds that have been following affected individuals.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The recalled potions: standard healing elixir in standard packaging, distinguishable from safe stock only by the Lot number, which is stamped on the bottom of each vial in a font size that the recall team has been describing to apothecaries with increasing specificity as the operation progresses.

## 2. The Company

**The Recall Response Center** is the operation's headquarters. Response Director Nora manages twenty people across field retrieval, affected individual support, public communications, and the regulatory liaison that is required by law when a recall affects more than one hundred individuals. She has managed three previous recalls. She is managing this one with the specific focus of someone who knows exactly what she is doing and is being regularly surprised by new developments that fall outside the previous recalls' parameters.

**The Field Retrieval Teams** are in twelve distribution districts simultaneously, working through the distribution chain: warehouse to distributor to apothecary to end consumer. The chain is documented. Following it is logistics work of moderate complexity. The complications are the consumers who cannot be located, the apothecaries who sold doses without recording purchaser information, and the 89 known-affected individuals who are now dealing with a side effect that is attracting community attention.

**The Affected Individual Support Unit** is the team responsible for contacting all 89 known-affected individuals, explaining the situation, providing documentation, and coordinating with the company's medical advisory team to confirm that the side effect is temporary and without lasting health consequences. The Support Unit has developed a standardized communication approach for the first interaction that accounts for the fact that the person they are speaking to may respond in warbler.

**The Regulatory Liaison** is the company's interface with the Kingdom Alchemical Safety Board, which has been notified of the recall and is monitoring the situation. Board representative Dax has been on a call with the Recall Response Center every six hours and is maintaining a professional equanimity about the situation that the Vitalis team finds both reassuring and slightly unnerving.

## 3. Factions

**The Recall Response Team** is the company's internal crisis response. They are competent, stressed, and managing a situation that is getting bigger rather than smaller. Director Nora's priority is completing the recall quickly and quietly. Her secondary priority is managing the cases that have already become public before they escalate the Severity Rating to the level that triggers a mandatory regulatory production review.

**The Alchemical Safety Board** is monitoring the recall and will take action if the Severity reaches 4 or higher. Their standard action is a production audit, which takes six weeks and requires company cooperation. Board representative Dax is not adversarial; he is doing his job, which is ensuring that the recall is proceeding according to regulation and that the public is not being harmed. He is also quietly fascinated by the court stenographer situation.

**The Affected Individuals** are 89 people managing a temporary, non-harmful side effect that has complicated their professional and social situations in varying degrees. Most of them are cooperating with the Support Unit. A meaningful subset are managing complications that the Support Unit was not designed for. The bard is requesting a second dose. The diplomatic translators are the subject of an international incident. The judge is working through a backlog of decisions that are transcribed but not readable.

**The Media** is the factor the Recall Response Team is most actively managing. The court incident was reported. The diplomatic incident is being managed by the Foreign Office but is not containable. The bard's song has been reproduced in three publications. The team's public communications strategy is holding Severity at 2, but each new public incident increases the likelihood of a consolidated story that brings more attention than individual incidents.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Recall Severity

Track **Severity Rating** from 1 to 5.

| Rating | Status |
|--------|--------|
| 1 | Quiet recall. Known to industry professionals only. |
| 2 | Known incident. Reported in trade publications. Public awareness minimal. |
| 3 | Public awareness. General press coverage. Regulatory monitoring active. |
| 4 | Crisis status. Significant public attention. Regulatory audit triggered. |
| 5 | Full crisis. Production suspension. Executive statements required. Regulatory investigation. |

**Raising Severity (+1 each):**
- A public incident involving an affected individual is reported in general press
- The company's recall communications produce public confusion rather than reassurance
- An affected individual experiences a complication that the "non-harmful" classification cannot accommodate
- Regulatory board escalates from monitoring to active investigation

**Lowering Severity (-1 each, minimum 1):**
- Recall retrieval rate reaches 75%, then 90%
- A public incident is successfully managed before press reporting
- Affected individuals speak positively about the company's support
- A genuine medical expert publicly confirms the side effect is temporary and harmless

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Response Director

You are Nora. You have managed four recalls. You are managing this one. You know exactly what you are doing and you are being regularly confronted with new developments that you know exactly what to do with and that keep producing new developments that require you to know what to do with a new thing. You are currently on hour 36 of a recall that you estimated would take 48 hours and that you now think will take 96. The bard situation is the one that is keeping you awake. Not because it is harmful. Because it is beautiful, and you are a person who responds to beautiful things, and the thing you are trying to recall is responsible for it, and you are going to complete the recall anyway.

- **Drive:** *"Complete the recall with the smallest possible public footprint, manage the bard situation without creating a precedent that future Lot 7443 consumers use to refuse recall support, and get the Severity back to 1 before the Board's next check-in call."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Field Retrieval Agent

You have been tracking doses in the southern distribution district for 30 hours. You have recovered 412 doses. You have visited 23 apothecaries. You have located and spoken to (or attempted to speak with) 14 affected individuals. You are very good at your job and you are very tired. You have also been documenting the affected individuals' experiences as part of standard recall protocol, and your notes contain observations that you think the company's medical advisory team needs to see before they finalize the "temporary and non-harmful" classification.

- **Drive:** *"Complete the southern district retrieval and get your clinical observation notes to the medical team before the 48-hour company statement locks in claims about the side effect's nature that your documentation suggests are not fully accurate."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### The Regulatory Liaison

You are Dax from the Alchemical Safety Board. Your job is to ensure the recall is proceeding according to regulation and that the public is protected. You are good at your job. You are also aware that the current situation falls into a category your training described as "novel" and your supervisor described as "use your judgment." The court stenographer situation specifically: the recall covered the affected individual, but the transcribed proceedings are a legal document produced in a language that nobody can read back. That is not a public health problem. It might be a legal problem. It might be nothing. Your judgment, which you are using, is that you need to call someone who knows courtroom law, and you are going to do that immediately after this call with the Recall Response Center.

- **Drive:** *"Ensure the recall meets regulatory requirements, determine whether the court proceedings situation creates additional regulatory obligations, and maintain the Board's monitoring role without either overstepping or undersupplying oversight."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Bard's Request:** The bard who consumed Lot 7443, released a widely circulated recording of the side effect's effect on their music, and is declining recall support has now issued a public statement arguing that the side effect constitutes an artistic discovery and requesting that Vitalis provide a second dose under voluntary informed consent for "creative research purposes." The statement has Severity implications: if Vitalis refuses, the bard frames it as the company suppressing artistic innovation. If Vitalis accommodates, they have created a consent framework for intentional side effect exposure that the Safety Board will have significant opinions about. Director Nora is writing three different response drafts. Dax from the Safety Board has asked for a call. The characters must find a position for Vitalis that is legally defensible, publicly reasonable, and does not require the company to either suppress an artist or intentionally distribute a product with known side effects.

**Why It Matters:** The Potion Recall Crisis is about the comedy and genuine complexity of institutional responsibility for unintended consequences. Vitalis made a good product that developed an unexpected property. The recall is the right response. The bard's experience is a genuine complication: an unintended consequence that produced something of value, and the question of what the company owes to that complication is not straightforwardly answerable. The setting is about corporate responsibility, the ethics of unintended effects, and the specific absurdity of institutions managing situations they were designed for that keep developing features they were not.
