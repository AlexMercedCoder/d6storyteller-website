## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

The Guild of Certified Referees is the oldest and most secret order in the realm. While spectators buy tickets for athletic competition, the Guild treats every stadium as a containment ward. Tens of thousands of screaming fans produce immense psychic and magical pressure. Entities from neighboring planes sense this energy and attempt to breach reality through the stadium turf, often anchoring themselves to high-velocity players or dramatic moments.

The referees manage these threats without breaking the illusion of the game. A yellow card is a targeted warding sigil. A corner kick reset releases excess mana. A red card ejects a player whose aura has been compromised by an invading entity. The officials must balance game fairness, crowd management, and dimensional security simultaneously.

**Tone:** High-stakes procedural, tense, and darkly humorous. It blends sports officiating bureaucracy with urban fantasy containment operations.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Bright stadium floodlights illuminating pristine green turf, contrasted by subtle magical shimmers in the air, glowing sigils carved into the referee's whistle, and shadows behind the players that move half a second too late.
2. **Sound:** The deafening roar of 50,000 fans, the sharp, shrill blast of an silver whistle that vibrates on a frequency only entities and trained officials can hear, and the low, subterranean rumble of reality straining beneath the pitch.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Freshly cut grass, sweat, hot dogs, and the ozone stench of raw mana bleeding into the atmosphere. The heavy weight of the referee's leather kit, lined with hidden lead and silver threads.

## 2. The Stadiums

**The Grand Arena of Oakhaven** is a massive 60,000-seat colosseum built directly over an ancient leyline intersection. It hosts the championship matches and experiences the most frequent class-5 breaches.

**The Guild Chamber (Locker Room 4)** is hidden behind a false wall in the main referee locker room. It contains warding books, silver-cast whistles, enchanted foul flags, and dimensional monitoring equipment disguised as tactical whiteboards.

**Section 104** is a notorious section of seats at Oakhaven where the barrier between worlds is paper-thin. Fans in Section 104 are famous for being particularly rowdy; the referees know they are actually experiencing mild psychic contamination.

**The Under-Pitch Tunnels** run beneath the stadium floor. Guild technicians monitor leyline pressure plates and maintain the massive containment runes buried six feet under the grass.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Guild of Certified Referees** is the secretive organization tasked with officiating and containment. Senior Official Marcus Vance leads with cold precision, placing dimensional stability far above fan satisfaction or league ratings.

**The League Executive Board** manages the commercial side of sports. They are unaware of the magical threats and constantly pressure referees to allow more "flow to the game" and fewer disruptive penalty calls, inadvertently threatening containment.

**The Unshackled** are rogue athletes or dark cultists who deliberately use stadium energy to perform forbidden rituals during high-profile matches, hiding their spellcasting within athletic movements.

**The Fanatic Order** consists of extreme supporters whose sheer collective emotion occasionally forces an entity through the rift accidentally.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Containment Score

Track **Containment Score** from 0 to 10 (starts at 5).

**Maintaining Containment:**
- Blow a correct whistle call to interrupt spellcasting (+1)
- Issue a yellow card to ward a compromised player (+1)
- Reset field positioning through a stoppage of play (+2)
- Perform an under-pitch leyline purge during halftime (+3)

**Decaying Containment:**
- Allowing a play to continue while mana swells (-1)
- Overriding an official's call due to player/crowd protest (-2)
- An entity successfully anchors to a high-momentum play (-3)

**Thresholds:**
- **8-10:** Clean game. Wards are stable.
- **4-7:** Anomalies appear: ball physics distort, shadows misbehave, players experience sudden hallucinations.
- **1-3:** Near breach. Weather shifts inside the stadium, turf bleeds, fans enter trance states.
- **0:** Breach! A full entity manifests on live television. The game is canceled and memory-wipe protocols activate.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Veteran Head Referee
You've officiated 400 matches and banished 50 higher demons without missing a foul call. You care about safety first, the laws of the game second, and fan opinions not at all.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the portal shut and give a fair 90 minutes of sport."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Assistant Linesman / Abjurer
You run the sidelines, tracking offside violations with one eye and rift fluctuations with the other. You carry the enchanted flags that seal boundary leaks.
- **Drive:** *"Spot the violation before it tears a hole in the sideline."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### The Rookie Official
Fresh out of the Guild Academy, you thought officiating was about learning rulebooks. Now you're holding a whistle made of exorcism silver and trying not to panic.
- **Drive:** *"Survive my first major rivalry match without causing an apocalypse."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario & Why It Matters

**The Rivalry Derby:** The semifinals between Oakhaven FC and Iron City FC have reached peak intensity. In the 75th minute, Iron City's star forward begins executing footwork patterns that match an ancient summoning spiral. A class-4 entity is attempting to manifest right on the penalty spot. Meanwhile, the League President is in the stands threatening to fire the officiating crew if they stop play again. Containment is at 3 and dropping fast.

**Why It Matters:** The Referees' Conspiracy turns sports bureaucracy into a heroic sacrifice. Referees are universally hated by fans and players, but in this world, that hatred is the shield protecting humanity from cosmic horrors.
