## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Set in 1930s Honduras, *The Lost Citadel of the Monkey God* blends 1930s pulp adventure with rich Lenca and Maya archaeology. Deep within the unexplored Mosquitia rainforest lies *La Ciudad Blanca* - a mythic city of carved white stone, guarded by ancient monkey-god guardians (*Olocsing*) and Lenca protectors. As international fortune hunters and corporate mercenaries enter the jungle with dynamite and airplanes, local guardians must protect the sacred ruins from plunder.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Giant stone monkey monoliths covered in glowing green moss; blinding white limestone ruins emerging from triple-canopy jungle; the flash of scarlet macaws over the Patuca River.
2. **Sound:** The deafening roar of howler monkeys at dawn; the buzzing of relentless mosquito swarms; the distant thrumming of a biplane engine cutting through rain clouds.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Soaking jungle humidity that rusts steel in hours; the heavy scent of crushed orchid blossoms and damp loam; the sting of razor-sharp sawgrass along riverbanks.

**The Core Tension:** Discovery vs. Desecration. Unlocking ancient secrets could save local communities, but exposing the city risks destroying it.

--

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

Honduras is rich with archaeological history, from the great Maya city of Copán to the legendary *Ciudad Blanca* in Mosquitia. The Lenca people, the largest Indigenous group in Honduras, maintain profound spiritual traditions connected to mountain spirits (*Wancasco*) and sacred stone monuments.

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** While incorporating 1930s pulp aesthetics, this setting elevates Lenca indigenous sovereignty and archaeological stewardship over colonial grave-robbing.

--

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Lenca Canopy Guardians (Wancasco Keepers)
Indigenous protectors who use ancient forest paths to guard the sacred sites of Mosquitia.
- **Cultural Association:** Masters of poison darts, botanical medicine, and jungle concealment.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can walk across the jungle canopy without snapping a single twig"*
  - *"The Lenca mountain spirits speak to me through the river currents"*

### The Copán Antiquarian Society
Honduran scholars and archaeologists dedicated to preserving their nation's heritage.
- **Cultural Association:** Translators of Maya glyphs, cartographers, and museum archivists.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"Read Maya glyphs as easily as newsprint"*
  - *"Carries a tailored machete and a waterproof notebook"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Jungle Strain

Characters track a **Jungle Strain Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Fresh):** +1d6 to Mind and Presence rolls. Gear functions perfectly.
- **3–4 (Weary):** +1d6 to Finesse in jungle terrain. Costs 1 Momentum to maintain equipment.
- **5–6 (Delirious):** +1d6 to Force, but Mind rolls suffer penalties. Hallucinations of monkey-spirits manifest.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Lenca Scout
- **Concept:** A rainforest guide protecting sacred ground.
- **Descriptors:** *Silent navigator of Mosquitia*, *Expert with blowgun and herb remedies*, *Honored by river elders*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the sacred city hidden from plunderers."*
- **Flaw:** *"Suspicious of all outsiders carrying cameras or guns."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

### Archetype 2: The Honduran Archaeologist
- **Concept:** A patriotic scholar racing to save artifacts for Tegucigalpa museums.
- **Descriptors:** *Translator of Copán stelae*, *Crack shot with a service revolver*, *Fluent in Spanish, Lenca, and English*.
- **Drive:** *"Return our ancestral treasures to our own people."*
- **Flaw:** *"Takes reckless risks to preserve ancient tablets."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

### Archetype 3: The Bush Pilot
- **Concept:** A barnstorming aviator who knows the air currents over Mosquitia.
- **Descriptors:** *Flies a battered twin-engine biplane*, *Can land on sandbars in a tropical storm*, *Fears no jungle peril*.
- **Drive:** *"Fly where no map has ever drawn a line."*
- **Flaw:** *"Gambles away paychecks in port taverns."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Gates of White Stone

- **Framing:** The expedition arrives at the entrance of La Ciudad Blanca as a rival mercenary group sets dynamite charges.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Disarming dynamite charges while under howler monkey swarm attack.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Rainforest monsoon arriving in 3 beats.
  3. *Opportunity:* Triggering ancient stone counterweights to seal the inner sanctum.
- **Antagonist:** *Baron Von Krasner* (Mercenary Treasure Hunter).
  - *Traits:* Armed with submachine guns, ruthless.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Arrogant / ☐ Mercenaries Scattered / ☐ Trapped in Ruins.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

Honduras contains some of the most mysterious and ecological pristine landscapes in the Americas. This setting celebrates Honduran archaeology and Lenca heritage through high-stakes adventure.
