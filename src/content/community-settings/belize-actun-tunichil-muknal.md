## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Set during the Terminal Classic Maya period (c. 800–900 CE) in the lush river valleys of central Belize, *The Secret of Actun Tunichil Muknal* plunges players into a world of desperate ritual, underground rivers, and high-stakes diplomacy. As prolonged droughts threaten the agricultural heartlands of Caracol, Xunantunich, and Lamanai, an elite circle of cave-guardians (the *Ah K'inob of the Deep*) descends into the underworld portal of Actun Tunichil Muknal to make peace with the rain gods (Chaac) and the lords of death.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Sparkling calcite formations reflecting flickering pine-pitch torches; calcified human remains glowing iridescently in subterranean chambers; the brilliant turquoise of subterranean river pools cutting through pitch-black limestone.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic drip of mineral water echoing across cavern ceilings; distant subterranean roaring of underground rapids; low chanting in ancient Ch'olan Maya; the sudden flutter of thousands of insect-eating bats returning to the cave mouth.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The shock of ice-cold river water against chest-high wading; the sharp, metallic tang of cave minerals and old copal resin; smooth, water-carved limestone ledges slippery with damp moss.

**The Core Tension:** Devotion vs. Survival. As royal dynasties collapse and city-states fragment, the characters must decide whether to perform increasingly costly sacrifices to satisfy the underworld or find new ways for their communities to adapt above ground.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

Belize was a vital center of the Classic Maya world, home to major urban centers like Caracol, Xunantunich, Lamanai, and Altun Ha. Actun Tunichil Muknal (ATM) is a real archaeological cave site in the Cayo District of Belize, famous for sacrificial offerings, ceramics, and the calcified remains of ritual victims (such as the "Crystal Maiden").

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** The cave rituals of Actun Tunichil Muknal represent sacred Maya ceremonial traditions undertaken during times of catastrophic environmental crisis. This setting presents these traditions with deep reverence for Maya cosmology, focusing on human resilience, spiritual duty, and community preservation.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Guardians of the Deep (Ah K'inob Muknal)
Subterranean ritual specialists trained to navigate the flooded subterranean labyrinths of the Cayo District.
- **Cultural Association:** Keeper of ancient cave maps, astronomical alignment calendars, and sacrificial protocols.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can swim three leagues through pitch-black subterranean currents without losing my direction"*
  - *"The calcite crystals speak to me of droughts six seasons before they arrive"*

### The Coastal Reef Navigators (P'entik-Ha)
Traders and maritime navigators who connect the inland river networks of Belize to the barrier reef and Caribbean trade loops.
- **Cultural Association:** Expert canoe builders, salt merchants, and coastal spirit-callers.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"My canoe has weathered the outer reef storms that broke Spanish galleons"*
  - *"I trade barrier reef coral for Cayo jade without losing a single grain"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Crystal Resonance

Characters track a **Calcite Spark Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Grounded):** Human Mind and Presence rolls gain +1d6. Subterranean magic is unavailable.
- **3–4 (Resonant):** Balanced state. Characters can sense hidden cave chambers and water veins. Costs 1 Momentum per scene.
- **5–6 (Overcharged):** Subterranean Force and Finesse rolls gain +1d6. The character can manipulate rock or step through shadows, but failing a roll introduces *Subterranean Madness* (Storyteller gains narrative control of illusions).

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Crystal Priestess (Ah K'in Bloodline)
- **Concept:** A young ritualist destined to enter the deepest chamber of Actun Tunichil Muknal.
- **Descriptors:** *Master of copal incense rituals*, *Read the star alignment on subterranean pool reflections*, *Fluent in high court Ch'olan*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the rains flowing to save our valley."*
- **Flaw:** *"I trust the omens more than the people around me."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### Archetype 2: The River Scout
- **Concept:** A rugged navigator who guides ritual parties through flooded cave systems.
- **Descriptors:** *Built like mahogany wood*, *Knows every hidden subterranean breathing pocket*, *Never lost a torch in swift water*.
- **Drive:** *"Protect the priestess at all costs."*
- **Flaw:** *"Impatient with political court talk."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

### Archetype 3: The Xunantunich Scholar
- **Concept:** A royal scribe seeking ancient codices hidden in cave sanctuaries before the city falls.
- **Descriptors:** *Deciphered the oldest stelae at Caracol*, *Carries waterproof glyph tablets*, *Connected to noble trade houses*.
- **Drive:** *"Preserve our history before the drought consumes the cities."*
- **Flaw:** *"Physically frail in rough terrain."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 4 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Chamber of the Crystal Maiden

- **Framing:** The characters enter Actun Tunichil Muknal to retrieve a sacred jade stela before a rival warlord loots the cave.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Wading through a rising subterranean river flash flood.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Torchlight burning out in 4 beats.
  3. *Opportunity:* Using ancient calcite carvings to open a secret dry bypass chamber.
- **Antagonist:** *K'inich the Defiler* (Rival warlord raiding cave tombs).
  - *Traits:* Ruthless, heavy obsidian war club.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Wounded / ☐ Disarmed / ☐ Retreating.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

Actun Tunichil Muknal is one of the most sacred and culturally profound archaeological sites in Central America. This setting honors the spiritual depth and resilience of the Maya during a epochal period of environmental transition.
