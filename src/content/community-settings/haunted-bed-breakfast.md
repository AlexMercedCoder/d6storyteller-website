## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Haunted Hospitality Association was founded by three property owners who discovered that their haunted houses were attracting paying guests and decided that a professional framework would be better than improvised chaos. Forty years later, the Association has 217 member properties, a robust rating system, a quarterly newsletter, and the most unusual set of hospitality industry standards in the known world.

The Association's core challenge is that supernatural presences are not employees. They cannot be trained, contracted, or given performance reviews. The best that property managers can do is understand the property's specific manifestations well enough to position them as features rather than problems.

**Tone:** Warm hospitality comedy with genuine supernatural underpinning. The humor comes from the determined professionalism of property managers trying to provide five-star service in circumstances that are fundamentally uncontrollable. The ghosts are often sympathetic figures, sometimes comic, and occasionally genuinely unsettling.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Association's model property: a Victorian house in excellent repair, with historically accurate decor that serves double duty as both hospitality aesthetic and authentic period atmosphere for supernatural manifestations. The guest registry desk where check-in forms include a consent acknowledgment for potential supernatural encounters. The "Experience Level" indicator board in the lobby, a framed display that Property Manager Nora updates each morning with that day's expected activity level.
2. **Sound:** The property's ambient sounds: old house settling, and occasionally something else. The specific protocol Nora uses to address manifestations: a calm, professional tone, because the properties that have tried to suppress their hauntings have universally had worse outcomes than the ones that learned to communicate. The guest bell that rings from Room 4 at 3 AM with the reliability of a rooster.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The particular cold that predicts a manifestation: experienced property staff learn to recognize the temperature drop that precedes supernatural activity and use it as a scheduling signal rather than a fright trigger. The smell of the breakfast that is served regardless of what happened during the night: good food as a stabilizing force.

## 2. The Property

**The Main House** is where most guests stay and where most manifestations occur. The house has three active supernatural presences: a Victorian-era widow who appears in the dining room during evening service, a figure who manifests in Room 4 and appears to have access to personal information about guests, and something in the cellar that has not manifested clearly enough to classify but that produces temperature anomalies and a low resonance on certain nights.

**The Association Inspector's Room** is always kept available for Association compliance visits, which are unannounced and quarterly. Chief Inspector Dax has visited eleven times and has developed a professional relationship with the Victorian widow that is unprecedented in Association records.

**The Garden** is the safest area: no documented manifestations, good natural light, and the location of the Association's most-praised amenity (the breakfast service, which Property Manager Nora runs with the precision of someone who has discovered that excellent food offsets a significant amount of supernatural inconvenience).

**The Cellar** is the property's classification problem: the manifestation down there has been present for as long as the property has stood but has never resolved into a form the Association can rate. The Property is currently listed as having "2 Rated Presences + 1 Unclassified Activity" in the Association directory, which some guests interpret as a bonus feature and others find alarming.

## 3. Factions

**The Haunted Hospitality Association** is the regulatory and professional body. Chief Inspector Dax manages compliance with the professional detachment of someone who has been doing this long enough that walking through a manifesting dining room is part of the job description. The Association genuinely supports its member properties and offers practical resources: a guest complaint mediation service, a database of successful management strategies, and an annual conference where property managers share approaches.

**The Property's Supernatural Presences** are not organized as a faction but function as one in practice. The Victorian widow (named internally as Elara, based on period records) has shown evidence of preferences and opinions. The Room 4 presence has demonstrated awareness of Association compliance requirements. The Cellar presence is not classifiable. They do not coordinate, but their interactions with each other and with management have produced situations that required an emergency Association consultation.

**The Guest Community** is self-selecting: people who book certified haunted properties expect and want an experience. The Association tracks guest demographics and has found three distinct types: thrill seekers who want intensity, researchers who want documentation, and a smaller group who appear to be processing personal grief and who find the presence of the willing-to-communicate dead useful in ways the Association's counselor consultant has been documenting for a paper.

**The Neighborhood Association** objects to the property's operation on general grounds and specifically on the grounds that their property values are affected by a certified haunted house three doors down. Their legal standing is unclear. Their annual complaints are regular. Property Manager Nora has learned to predict them by the same kind of seasonal awareness she applies to the manifestations.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Haunting Level

Track the property's **Haunting Level** from 0 to 6.

| Level | Activity | Guest Experience |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| 0-1 | Minimal. Cold spots only. Nothing visible. | "We paid for haunted. This is just a cold house." Refund requests likely. |
| 2-3 | Moderate. Regular manifestations of the rated presences. Predictable patterns. | "Excellent experience, exactly as described." Association's target range. |
| 4-5 | High. Additional manifestations, longer durations, increased intensity. | "Extraordinary experience." Also produces incident reports. |
| 6 | Critical. Uncontrolled activity. | Evacuation protocol. Association emergency contact. Rating suspension. |

**Adjusting Haunting Level:** The manager can take actions to encourage or calm activity. Encouraging activity risks pushing toward 5-6. Calming activity risks pushing toward 0-1. The Cellar presence is not responsive to either approach and introduces unpredictable modifiers.

**Incidents at Level 5-6:** Roll for incident type: property damage, guest fright requiring medical attention, guest who refuses to leave (considers the experience transformative), or manifestation of a presence not previously recorded at the property.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Property Manager

You are Nora. You manage a certified haunted property with two rated presences and a third unclassified situation in the cellar that you have reached a professional accommodation with by simply not going into the cellar after 9 PM. You run the best breakfast service in your property's rating category because you have discovered that excellent food is a universal stabilizer. You are about to host an Association compliance inspection, a group booking from a paranormal research team, and a couple who are getting married in the garden and who have been assured that the supernatural activity can be managed to not interfere with the ceremony.

- **Drive:** *"Get through the compliance inspection, the research booking, and the wedding without the Cellar situation deciding that this particular weekend is the right time to fully manifest."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### The Association Inspector

You are Dax. You have inspected every certified haunted property in your district. You have developed, in the line of professional duty, a working relationship with the Victorian widow at the Merced House that the Association's ethical guidelines do not specifically address because nobody anticipated this situation. You are currently writing a supplementary framework for "Presence Relationships" that will be presented at the annual conference. You are also conducting a compliance inspection that you are approaching with the same professional rigor you apply to every inspection, while personally hoping that Elara is in the dining room this evening.

- **Drive:** *"Complete a rigorous compliance inspection that accurately assesses the property's current standards, without allowing your personal relationship with one of the presences to affect your professional judgment."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### The Paranormal Researcher

You are Dr. Sela Morrow, lead investigator for a university research team that has booked the entire property for a weekend. You have a grant, equipment, a research protocol, and three graduate students who are considerably less calm about haunted properties than their applications suggested. You are interested in the unclassified Cellar presence specifically. You believe it is not a traditional presence but something that defies the Association's existing classification framework. You have not shared this hypothesis with the Property Manager because you suspect she would prefer not to know.

- **Drive:** *"Collect sufficient data on the Cellar presence to publish a classification challenge without destabilizing the property's management situation or causing your graduate students to transfer programs."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Wedding Weekend:** A couple has booked the property's garden for a wedding ceremony. They are aware of the property's haunted status and have signed the standard consent acknowledgment. They have not been informed that one of the presences in Room 4 appears to have a strong emotional response to weddings, based on the property's incident records for the past twelve years. The paranormal research team is arriving the same weekend to investigate the Cellar. The Association's compliance inspection is scheduled for Saturday morning. Haunting Level is currently at 3, trending upward. Nora has prepared the best breakfast menu of her career and is hoping very hard that this is enough.

**Why It Matters:** The Haunted Bed and Breakfast Association is about the comedy of professionalism applied to the uncontrollable. Nora cannot manage the presences. She can only manage her response to them and provide enough stability around them that the experience becomes something guests value. The setting is quietly about the limits of institutional control and the specific grace required to provide care in circumstances that are fundamentally beyond your authority.
