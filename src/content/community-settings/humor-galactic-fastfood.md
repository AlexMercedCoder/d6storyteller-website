## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The galaxy is vast and strange. Stellar Bites is in 600 star systems of it, and they all have the same menu.

**Stellar Bites** achieved galactic dominance through a simple proposition: standardized, consistent, universally adequate food at every location. Not great. Consistent. In a universe of infinite variety, consistency is a value proposition. The franchise model works. The individual locations work less smoothly.

Location 7,743 is on the Teryx Station, a mid-sized transit hub that sees traffic from 30+ species daily. The crew is diverse, the management is earnest, and the situations are consistently unusual in ways the franchise manual does not address.

**Tone:** This is workplace comedy in space. The problems are real (broken equipment, difficult customers, supply chain issues) and also occasionally cosmic (the secret sauce, the franchise inspector's true nature, the anomaly). The crew handles both with the same level of weary professionalism.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A fast food restaurant that is simultaneously absolutely generic (same counters, same menu boards, same uniform design across 14,000 locations) and completely unusual (serving beings of dozens of species, in a space station, with a kitchen that has been modified three times for non-standard ingredients and once to accommodate the Zorgathi prep crew's environmental requirements).
2. **Sound:** The constant rush-period soundtrack of a fast food kitchen: orders coming in, equipment running, the beep of the fryer timer, a customer complaint escalating at the counter, and the very specific spatial audio of someone complaining in a language the translator can't fully render.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The standard Stellar Bites smell (designed to trigger appetite in 90% of species; works on most of the crew too after long enough), the greasy warmth of a kitchen that has been running for 14 consecutive hours, the texture of the franchise manual (good paper; corporate takes the brand seriously), and the cold of the walk-in storage.

**The Core Tension:** The franchise system provides structure that makes the work manageable. The actual work is never what the structure anticipated. Every shift requires both compliance and improvisation.

## 2. Location 7,743 Operations

### The Menu
- **Classic Asteroid Patty:** Stellar Bites' signature item; standardized protein blend calibrated for maximum cross-species palatability
- **Nebula Nuggets:** Processed protein shapes; the photosynthesis option is real but the plants do seem slightly distressed after consumption
- **Void Berry Shake:** Limited time. Nobody is sure what a Void Berry is. They're very popular. Corporate has declined to clarify.
- **The Local Adaptation:** Every location is allowed one locally-adapted menu item. 7,743's is the "Teryx Special" and nobody outside the station has heard of it but it has a cult following.

### The Corporate Relationship
Stellar Bites corporate is deeply, genuinely invested in brand consistency. The Franchise Standards Team issues weekly updates. The metrics are monitored. The Franchise Inspector visits quarterly. The Inspector's visits are noted in crew logs with a specific tension that suggests the Inspector is unusual in a way the logs do not fully articulate.

### The Supply Chain
Stellar Bites operates a galaxy-wide supply chain delivering standardized ingredients to all 14,000 locations. The chain is generally reliable. When it fails, location 7,743 is expected to improvise within franchise standards. The franchise standards do not address improvisation.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The Secret Sauce Investigation:** Someone has read the full ingredient list for the Stellar Bites signature sauce. One ingredient is listed by a designation number that doesn't correspond to any known food source. Further investigation reveals the designation is a classification code for a sentient organism. It may be classified. It may be voluntary. It may be neither.

2. **The Franchise Inspection:** The Franchise Inspector arrives. The Inspection reveals the Inspector is not human, not from any recognized species, and has been inspecting Stellar Bites locations across the galaxy since before some of those locations existed. The inspection itself is meticulous and professional. The inspectoris nature creates a parallel concern.

3. **The Supply Failure:** The supply chain has failed. 7,743 has 36 hours of standard ingredients. After that, they either close (Service Score disaster) or improvise a menu from locally available sources. The local sources include some things that are technically food and some things that are technically not food but might pass in an emergency.

4. **The Long Shift:** A galactic transit disruption has trapped 3,000 beings on Teryx Station with nowhere to go but the three restaurants, of which Stellar Bites is the only one still operational. The rush is beyond anything the location was designed for. Normal service is impossible. Extraordinary service requires extraordinary measures.

5. **The Perfect Review:** Location 7,743 has been nominated for Stellar Bites' Corporate Excellence Award. A review team is coming. The location is not, in its current state, award-ready. It has been awarded anyway, by a being who apparently visited in the future and found it perfect. Making the award legitimate requires figuring out what about the future visit was perfect and replicating it now.
