## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Inspired by ancient non-binary, trans, and Two-Spirit spiritual traditions across global cultures (such as the Scythian Enaree, the Bugis *Bissu* of Indonesia, and Indigenous American Two-Spirit shamans), *The Celestial Weavers* is a sweeping high-fantasy setting celebrating gender variance as a sacred cosmic power.

In the high mountain sanctuaries along the **Astral Silk Road**, the **Weavers of the Third Sun** are revered as sacred guardians. Because they embody both or neither traditional binary gender roles, they possess **Limen** - the innate ability to stand at the threshold of realities. Using loom-staves and enchanted silk thread spun from starlight, they repair broken souls, heal dimensional rifts, and maintain harmony across worlds.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** High mountain peaks under a swirling galaxy of nebulae; translucent looms made of carved bone and starlight; glowing threads of shimmering violet, gold, and cyan humming with energy.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic click of loom shuttles weaving cosmic threads; the resonant hum of singing bowls; the quiet whisper of mountain winds carrying starlight.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The tactile smoothness of astral silk that feels like liquid light; the crisp, thin air of high mountain passes; the scent of burning cedar and sweetgrass.

## 2. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Weavers of the Third Sun
Non-binary and trans shamans who maintain the looms of reality.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I spin threads of starlight that bind physical matter to spirit"*
  - *"My gender is a constellation, constantly shifting and shining"*

### The Iron Binary Legion (The Antagonists)
Extremist astral conquerors who seek to flatten all complexity into rigid, opposing binaries.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"They fear what they cannot box into simple categories"*

--

## 3. Unique Mechanic: Limen (Boundary Walking)

- **1-2 (Constrained):** Forced into rigid expectations. Magic is limited.
- **3-4 (Threshold Walker):** Embracing liminal power. +1d6 to Mind and Finesse. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5-6 (Cosmic Weaver):** Fully realized. +1d6 to all rolls. Can step between dimensions, weave forcefields of starlight, and heal trauma.
