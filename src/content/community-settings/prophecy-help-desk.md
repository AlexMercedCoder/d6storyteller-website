## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Division was established when it became clear that divine prophecies, left without administrative support, were producing an unacceptable level of collateral damage: heroes taking the wrong quests because they misunderstood their destiny, villains fulfilling prophecies they thought they were preventing, and at least one case where two different heroes both attempted to fulfill the same prophecy and created a diplomatic incident.

The Division does not control prophecies. It clarifies them, tracks their resolution status, and provides customer support to the people whose fates are described in language that made perfect sense to the divine source and is deeply ambiguous to the mortal recipient.

**Tone:** Procedural comedy with genuine existential stakes. The humor is in the gap between the profundity of fate and the mundanity of the ticketing system. The Division staff are not cynical about prophecy; they take it very seriously. They are also very serious about queue management, which produces some absurd prioritization decisions.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Division's main floor: rows of interpretation desks, each with a glowing prophecy display and a stack of case files. The Queue board: a large display showing current ticket numbers, estimated wait times, and the occasional status flag (Contradiction Pending, Oracle Unavailable, Celestial Event Delay). The oracle confirmation room: a sealed chamber where the Division's senior specialists interface with the licensed oracle network, visible through a small window that staff pass without looking because looking gives most people a migraine.
2. **Sound:** The customer communication language: formal, reassuring, and designed to prevent heroes from abandoning their prophesied paths because the paperwork was frustrating. The interpretive debate between two Division specialists who have looked at the same three-line prophecy for four days and reached different conclusions about what "the last heir of the fallen house" means in a kingdom that has had seventeen successive royal family line extinctions.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The prophecy scrolls themselves: divine documents that are warm to the touch and smell faintly of the specific oracle who received them. Copies made by Division scribes lose both qualities, which is how you can tell the difference between an official prophecy and a forgery. The Division's filing system: an extensive physical archive organized by prophecy date, recipient name, and a thematic index that the current archivist has spent fifteen years building and that is the most complete reference on prophetic patterns in existence.

## 2. The Division

**The Customer Service Floor** handles new ticket intakes, initial interpretations, and the ongoing communication with prophecy recipients who have questions. Most specialists develop areas of expertise: celestial events, lineage prophecies, object-centered destinies, and the Division's least popular specialization, Contradiction Management.

**The Senior Interpretation Unit** handles the difficult cases: contradictions, multi-party prophecies, and the increasingly common category of prophecies that appear to have been issued about the same event from different divine sources using incompatible symbolic vocabularies. Unit Director Nora Pell has been managing this for fourteen years and has the specific combination of deep expertise and resignation that the job produces.

**The Oracle Liaison Office** maintains the working relationship with the licensed oracle network. Oracle Liaison Dax has the most unusual job in the Division: he communicates with entities whose answers to questions are never straightforward and who are contractually required to provide clarification but have a very loose interpretation of what "clarification" means.

**The Archive** contains complete records of every officially registered prophecy for the past six hundred years, along with their resolution records: whether they were fulfilled, contradicted, expired, or resolved as "Ambiguous Outcome." The resolution records are the most instructive database in the kingdom for understanding how prophecies actually work, and the Division uses them primarily for precedent research and secondarily for a deeply uncomfortable quarterly statistic: the percentage of active prophecies that resolve as "Ambiguous Outcome" rather than clear fulfillment. It has been rising for twenty years.

## 3. Factions

**The Division Staff** are the specialists, directors, and support personnel who run the prophetic support system. They believe in what they do. The Division prevents harm by ensuring that prophecies are understood correctly before heroes act on them. The alternative is documented: the Archive has a whole section on what happens when prophecies are acted on without interpretation support.

**The Oracle Network** is the licensed group of divine conduits through which official prophecies are issued. The Network is not an organization the Division controls; it is an entity the Division has a working relationship with. Oracle Network Representative Tam attends Division meetings, answers questions in the specific non-linear way that oracle representatives answer questions, and is technically the Division's most important partner and practically its most difficult one.

**The Heroes' Advocacy Group** represents the recipients of prophecies in their dealings with the Division. Most of their complaints are about wait times. Some are about interpretation quality. A growing subset are filing formal objections to the prophecies themselves, arguing that a destiny they did not choose and cannot opt out of requires more robust due process than a ticket number.

**The Fulfillment Verification Team** is the Division unit that confirms when a prophecy has been fulfilled. Their job is to review the claimed fulfillment, compare it against the original prophecy text, and issue a Fulfillment Certificate or an Incomplete Fulfillment notice. They are the most unpopular unit in the Division because heroes generally believe their prophecy is fulfilled the moment the dramatic climax occurs and are not prepared for a three-week review period.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Interpretation Queue

Track each case's **Queue Position** from 12 (just filed) to 1 (final resolution pending).

**Advancing in Queue (Position -1 each):**
- Gather corroborating evidence that supports a specific interpretation
- Secure oracle confirmation of an ambiguous clause
- Resolve a subsidiary contradiction that was blocking the main interpretation
- Demonstrate partial fulfillment that narrows the remaining interpretation

**Regressing in Queue (Position +1 each):**
- New contradictory evidence arrives
- Oracle issues an addendum that changes the interpretation
- A second prophecy about the same subject arrives from a different source
- The Division's gods issue a clarifying addendum that makes things worse

**At Position 1:** The case is submitted for Final Resolution Review. This can result in Certified Fulfillment, Incomplete Fulfillment, Expired Prophecy, or Ambiguous Outcome. Ambiguous Outcome means the case is archived without resolution, which neither the Division nor the recipient finds satisfying but which the Archive statistics suggest is increasingly common.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Interpretation Specialist

You have been interpreting prophecies for nine years. You are excellent at it. You are also very aware that the "Ambiguous Outcome" rate in your case history is 12%, which is slightly above the Division average but which represents four dozen prophecies that you were unable to resolve satisfactorily. You have started keeping a private file on cases you believe represent patterns that the Division's interpretation framework does not currently address, because you think the rising Ambiguous Outcome rate means something, and you think what it means is that the divine sources are becoming less specific, not that the Division's interpretation quality is declining.

- **Drive:** *"Present your pattern analysis to the Director before the next quarterly statistics report makes the rising Ambiguous Outcome rate a political problem rather than a methodological question."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Hero in the Queue

Your prophecy was issued seven years ago. It said you would "restore the balance of the Third Kingdom." The Third Kingdom has not existed for forty years. You have filed four interpretation requests. Your case is currently at Queue Position 7. You have been living your life in a state of prophetic incompleteness for seven years, unable to commit fully to anything because your destiny is pending Division review, and you are at the point where you have come to the Division in person to ask someone to look you in the eye and tell you whether your life has a purpose or whether you are waiting for resolution on a prophecy that is going to come back as Ambiguous Outcome.

- **Drive:** *"Get a final resolution on your prophecy, one way or another, so that you can either fulfill it or mourn it and start building an un-prophesied life."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 1 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### The Oracle Liaison

You are Dax. You speak to oracles for a living. You have developed a working methodology for translating oracle communication into Division-compatible documentation. You have also been having an ongoing background conversation with the Oracle Network's senior representative for four years that the Network considers a casual exchange and that you consider the most important prophetic research being conducted anywhere. The Network has been hinting at something about the rising Ambiguous Outcome rate that it will not state directly, in the specific way that oracle communication is never direct, and you are assembling the hints into a theory that, if correct, will require the Division to fundamentally rethink its operating framework.

- **Drive:** *"Assemble the oracle hints into a coherent theory and determine whether the Division needs to change its approach before the Ambiguous Outcome rate reaches a threshold that the gods apparently have an opinion about."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Contradiction:** Two active prophecies, filed by different recipients from different oracles, appear to describe the same event with mutually exclusive outcomes. Prophecy A says the heir of the Velden bloodline will prevent the Breaking of the Seventh Seal. Prophecy B says the Breaking of the Seventh Seal will be caused by the last act of the Velden bloodline. There is one living member of the Velden bloodline, who is currently at Queue Position 3 on Prophecy A and has not yet been informed that Prophecy B exists. Senior Unit Director Nora Pell has called an emergency case conference. The Oracle Liaison is trying to reach the Network for clarification. The Heroes' Advocacy Group has been notified that their member's case has a "complexity flag" and is asking for details. The characters must resolve the contradiction before the Velden heir takes any action that could fulfill either prophecy or both at once.

**Why It Matters:** The Prophecy Help Desk is about the specific comedy and genuine tragedy of fate administered through procedure. The Division exists because prophecy without interpretation support causes harm. The rising Ambiguous Outcome rate suggests that something about the relationship between fate and the people it is assigned to is changing. The setting asks whether destiny is something you support or something you question, and whether those two things are actually different.
