## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** South Africa's cultural landscape is defined by its deep spiritual connection to the **Amadlozi** (ancestral spirits). Traditional healers (**Sangomas**, who divine through bone-throwing and herbs, and **Inyangas**, who focus on medicine) operate comfortably alongside modern hospitals and universities. South African folklore features terrifyingly vivid entities: the **Impundulu** (a vampiric bird of lightning that can take human form), the **Tokoloshe** (a dwarfish water sprite summoned by malicious sorcerers to cause illness and misfortune), and the **Inkanyamba** (a giant winged serpent that causes severe storms when enraged).

This setting bridges the vibrant, fast-paced modern world of Johannesburg and Cape Town with the timeless power of the Drakensberg mountains and the Highveld. When modern greed disturbs ancient sacred sites, the ancestral realm fights back with thunder and lightning.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The iconic flat silhouette of Table Mountain shrouded in its white "tablecloth" cloud layer; the neon-and-concrete cityscape of Johannesburg under a sudden violet thunderstorm; the delicate ivory and animal bones of a Sangoma's *muthu* bag scattered across a ceremonial mat.
2. **Sound:** The crisp clatter of thrown bones striking a woven mat; the deafening crack of Highveld thunder echoing across open grassland; the hypnotic rhythm of traditional drumming and ancestral chanting in a township shrine.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The sharp smell of ozone right before a lightning strike; the earthy aroma of burning *imphepho* (incense herb) purifying a room; the cold metal of modern city structures against the warm, organic texture of beaded sacred regalia.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

South Africa's rich mythology draws from Bantu-speaking nations (Zulu, Xhosa, Sotho, Tswana) as well as the indigenous San and Khoikhoi peoples whose rock art stretches back tens of thousands of years.

Key elements include **Sangoma** spiritual calling (*ukuthwasa*), **Impundulu** (lightning bird mythology), and **San star-lore**, which views the night sky as a living canvas of ancient hunters and cosmic spirits.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Sangoma Order (Initiates of the Bones)
Healers called by the ancestors to divine truth, cure spiritual illness, and protect communities.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I threw the bones on the mat and read three generations of family history in their position"*
  - *"I burn imphepho to clear bad energy before entering any room"*

### The San Bushman Star-Walkers
Keepers of the world's oldest continuous artistic and spiritual tradition, capable of trance-dancing into the spirit world.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"My ancestors painted these rocks ten thousand years ago; the paintings are still warm"*
  - *"In the trance dance, my spirit leaves my body and flies with the eland"*

### The Impundulu Cult (Storm Brokers)
Renegade sorcerers who make bargains with the Lightning Bird for wealth and electrical power.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I carry a feather from the Impundulu; it charges my phone and electrocutes my enemies"*
  - *"We trade blood for high-voltage dominance over the city's power grid"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Amadlozi (Ancestral Favor)

- **1-2 (Distant):** Cut off from ancestors. Standard physical play. Vulnerable to dark magic.
- **3-4 (Guided):** Ancestors offer subtle signs and intuition. +1d6 to Mind when reading people or situations. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5-6 (Possessed by Grace):** Ancestral power flows directly through the character. +1d6 to all rolls. Can cast out evil spirits and command elemental lightning. **Risk:** The ancestors demand immediate restitution for any ethical breach.

## 6-7. Conditions & Starter Scenario

**Conditions:** *Lightning Singed*, *Tokoloshe Torment*, *Ancestral Disfavor*, *Imphepho Smudge*, *Bone-Reading Shock*.

**Starter Scenario: The Blackout Bird.** A massive blackout plunges Gauteng province into darkness. While engineers panic, Sangomas across the country receive the same vision: an Impundulu has roosted in the central power station, feeding on the high-voltage current to prepare for its egg-laying season. The characters must infiltrate the station, placate or battle the bird, and restore balance without frying the city's infrastructure.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

South Africa offers a brilliant synthesis of cutting-edge modern life and deeply rooted ancestral spirituality. It treats traditional indigenous medicine and spiritual practice not as quaint history, but as vital, living forces capable of addressing modern challenges.
