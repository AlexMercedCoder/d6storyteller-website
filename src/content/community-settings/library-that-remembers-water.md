## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Archive of Hydros is an architectural wonder: five flooded tiers of carved white marble submerged under crystal-clear subterranean spring water. 

Knowledge seekers wear silver diving helmets and lead-weighted boots to traverse the submerged reading rooms.

**Tone:** Dreamlike, poetic, mythic, and scholarly. Emphasizes atmosphere, memory ethics, and mystical exploration.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Sunlight filtering down through fifty feet of azure water onto sunken marble statues and brass bookshelves filled with glowing glass vials.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic, hollow echo of brass diving boots on marble stairs. Bubbles rising from copper diving helmets. The muffled ring of underwater signaling bells.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Weightlessness while swimming between stacks. The cool, crisp metallic taste of a centuries-old liquid memory.

--

## 2. The Sunken Halls

**The Atrium of Liquid Songs:** The main entrance hall where common histories and poetry vials are cataloged.

**The Vault of Tears:** A deep, cold trench housing sorrowful memories and historical tragedy vials.

**The Crystallized Gallery:** Upper tiers currently exposed to air due to receding water, where dry memories take physical form as sharp glass-like spires.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Order of Hydros:** The resident order of diving archivists who maintain the vials and guide visiting scholars.

**The Memory Merchants:** Outlaw collectors who steal rare liquid memories to sell to surface nobles seeking artificial experiences.

**The Dry Seekers:** Scholars who believe the library should be drained completely to convert the liquid memories into standard printed books.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Memory Saturation

Track **Memory Saturation** from 0 to 6.

**Saturation Modifiers:**
- Ingesting a standard memory vial: +1 Saturation
- Ingesting a traumatic/ancient memory vial: +2 Saturation
- Resting and meditating in pure spring water: -2 Saturation

**Saturation States:**
- **0-1:** Clear mind. Normal human perception.
- **2-3:** Enhanced recall. Whispers of previous authors when examining objects.
- **4-5:** Memory Dysphoria. Difficulty distinguishing personal past from ingested memories.
- **6:** Mind Flood. Temporary loss of personal identity; character acts as the author of the last consumed memory.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Diving Archivist

You swim the flooded stacks with lead boots and a copper helmet, retrieving requested vials from deep vaults.

- **Drive:** *"Protect the liquid memory vials from theft and air exposure."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

### The Memory Scholar

You specialize in analyzing and tasting complex liquid manuscripts, translating ancient memories into written scrolls.

- **Drive:** *"Decode the lost history of the First Dynasty stored in the Vault of Tears."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### The Relic Diver

You explore uncharted flooded sub-vaults looking for un-indexed liquid memories.

- **Drive:** *"Recover lost knowledge from the deepest submerged chambers."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

--

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Dry Vault Manifestation

As the water level drops in Tier 5, a sealed vault of dangerous pre-flood memories dries out. The exposed liquid memories crystallize into violent psychic manifestations that begin hunting archivists in the upper dry galleries.

The player characters must dive into the lower flooded reservoirs, divert water channels to re-submerge Tier 5, and seal the dry vault before the psychic manifestations reach the surface world.
