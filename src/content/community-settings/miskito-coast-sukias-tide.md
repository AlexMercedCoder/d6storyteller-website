## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Set along the vast, mangrove-fringed **Miskito Coast** (spanning the Caribbean shorelines of modern Honduras and Nicaragua), *The Sukia's Tide* immerses players in a world of maritime shamanism and coastal sovereignty. The **Miskito** nation, led by spiritual shamans called **Sukias**, commands the barrier reefs, lagoons, and tide currents. As Spanish armadas attempt to colonize the coast and privateers seek secret coves, the Sukias deploy tide magic and weather-weaving to protect their kingdom.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Towering green hurricane clouds rolling in over white foam reefs; Sukia shamans with sea-turtle shell staffs standing atop coral outcroppings; wooden piraguas cutting through mangrove channels.
2. **Sound:** The deafening crash of Atlantic breakers against barrier reefs; the rhythmic rattle of dried seed pods and conch shell trumpets; the howling of gale-force winds through pine savannas.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Brine-soaked salt spray coating your face; the pungent scent of mangrove mud at low tide; the cold sting of driven tropical rain.

**The Core Tension:** Sovereignty vs. Colonization. Preserving the Miskito Kingdom's freedom requires navigating dangerous treaties with buccaneers without letting foreign powers gain a foothold on the land.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

The Miskito Coast maintained an independent kingdom for centuries, resisting Spanish conquest through skilled canoeing, alliance-making, and deep knowledge of coastal geography. The *Sukia* (traditional shaman and healer) holds an honored place in Miskito spiritual life as a mediator between human communities and nature spirits (*Dawan*).

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** The Miskito nation has a unique, resilient history of self-determination. This setting celebrates Sukia spiritual practices and Miskito maritime skill.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Circle of Sukia Shamans (Dawan Keepers)
Spiritual leaders and healers who commune with sea and sky spirits to guide the Miskito Kingdom.
- **Cultural Association:** Master herbalists, tide forecasters, and spirit callers.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can smell a hurricane three days before the sky turns grey"*
  - *"Sea turtles guide my dugout through the dangerous reef cuts"*

### The Lagoon Navigators (Piragua Captains)
Maritime warriors who pilot fast, shallow-draft canoes built for lagoon warfare.
- **Cultural Association:** Master harpooners, coastal pilots, and lagoon defenders.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"My harpoon never misses a target, above water or below"*
  - *"I can hide three war canoes inside a mangrove root maze without a trace"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Storm Call & Tide Weaving

Characters track a **Sea Spirit Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Calm Water):** Normal rules. Sea spirits are quiet.
- **3–4 (Tide-Risen):** +1d6 to Finesse and Mind in coastal terrain. Can alter water currents or create fog banks. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5–6 (Gale Force):** +1d6 to Force and Finesse. Can summon localized lightning or mini-squalls. Failure causes ship-damaging wave surges.

## 5. Character Archetypes

- **Concept:** A spiritual healer and weather-weaver.
- **Descriptors:** *Master Sukia of the Miskito Kingdom*, *Commands sea-spirit winds*, *Carries a carved hawksbill turtle staff*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the coast free from Spanish armadas and pirate greed."*
- **Flaw:** *"Reluctant to leave the sound of ocean waves."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

- **Concept:** A daring lagoon warrior and canoe pilot.
- **Descriptors:** *Captain of the swift Miskito war canoes*, *Expert harpooner*, *Knows every reef passage from Cape Gracias a Dios to Bluefields*.
- **Drive:** *"Defend our coastal villages from foreign raids."*
- **Flaw:** *"Overly confident in rough sea conditions."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 1

- **Concept:** A bilingual negotiator who manages alliances with privateers.

- **Drive:** *"Use foreign rivalries to protect Miskito independence."*
- **Flaw:** *"Constantly walking a dangerous political tightrope."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 4 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Battle of Gracias a Dios

- **Framing:** A Spanish war galleon runs aground on the outer barrier reef during a storm.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Boarding the stranded galleon before its cannons can align.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Rising hurricane tide in 4 beats.
  3. *Opportunity:* Using Sukia tide magic to lift the canoe fleet over the reef crest.
- **Antagonist:** *Captain Don Rodrigo de la Serna* (Spanish Armada Commander).
  - *Traits:* Heavy cannons, disciplined marine musketeers.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Ship Aground / ☐ Cannons Silenced / ☐ Captured.
