## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The **Soul-Bound** world is a contemporary-adjacent setting where the existence of Animists-people who can absorb animal souls and use their essence-is known, partially regulated, and deeply contested. The Animist Compact is the official body that regulates soul-bonding, trains candidates, and maintains the argument that soul absorption is transformative and collaborative, not exploitative. The Liberators are a growing movement-some Animists, some humans, some animal-adjacent spiritual entities-who argue that soul absorption is theft and that every soul currently held by an Animist deserves freedom.

In the middle are the animals themselves, who have been communicating with certain Animists for years. Their position is more nuanced than either organized faction.

The tone is **philosophically rich supernatural adventure**: high stakes, genuine powers, morally complex relationships between human practitioners and the animal essences they carry, and an underlying question about what it means to hold another being's soul in trust.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** An Animist at full Soul Manifest-a human form surrounded by translucent animal shapes orbiting like constellations, their colors corresponding to species and essence strength (wolf in blue-silver, eagle in gold, whale in deep teal); the moment of soul absorption, which the Animist experiences as a rush of sensory information entirely alien to human experience (wolf-smell, eagle-vision, whale-sound) flooding in simultaneously before the soul settles.
2. **Sound:** The layered consciousness of a multi-soul Animist-wolves think in pack-awareness signals, eagles in topographic data, whales in long-form temporal narrative-all of this running below the Animist's conscious thought, occasionally surfacing as unexpected information at unexpected moments.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The Resonance-the specific physical sensation each soul creates when invoked: the wolf soul creates a tingling at the back of the neck and a sudden expansion of olfactory awareness; the eagle soul creates a mild vertigo and a heightened sensitivity to air pressure; the whale soul creates a deep subsonic hum in the chest and a sudden awareness of everything within 500 meters.

**The Core Tension:** Who speaks for the souls? The Compact says they do, through regulated practice and the argument that souls willing to bond with an Animist choose to do so in some sense. The Liberators say souls cannot consent to an irreversible bond with a being so unlike them. The animals-through the Animists who listen rather than just draw on them-say the question is more complicated than either answer suggests.

## 2. Historical & Organizational Context

Animism in this world has existed as long as human civilization-ancient records describe individuals who took on animal qualities in ways that pre-Compact accounts attribute to divine gift, curse, or madness. The **Animist Compact** formalized the practice approximately 200 years ago, developing the Harmony tracking system, the soul capacity limits (maximum 5 souls), and the Compact's Bonding Protocols (which require an animal's death to occur naturally or through necessity, not be induced for soul acquisition purposes).

The Compact's greatest achievement and greatest controversy: the **Soul Registry**, which tracks every soul currently bonded to an Animist. The Registry was originally created to prevent soul-trafficking (a real problem-non-Compact practitioners would induce animal deaths to harvest souls for sale). The Liberators argue the Registry normalizes the practice it was designed to regulate.

**Awakened Communicators:** A subset of Animists-perhaps 1 in 20-has developed the ability to communicate dialogically with bonded souls rather than just drawing on their power. This is the practice the Compact considers advanced and the Liberators consider proof of their argument: animals in dialogue describe the bonding experience in terms that are not consistently positive.

## 3. Core Factions

### The Animist Compact
The official regulatory body. Trains candidates, maintains the Soul Registry, manages relations with non-Animist populations, and advocates for the ethical treatment of bonded souls.
- **The Field Hunters:** Compact-licensed Animists who use their powers for sanctioned purposes (emergency response, conflict resolution, investigation).
- **The Soul Caretakers:** Compact specialists who work with Animists approaching Spiritual Rupture-managing Harmony restoration and, when necessary, guiding the controlled release of a soul the Animist can no longer sustain.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I carry a wolf, a dolphin, and a hummingbird. The Compact says my manifest is balanced. The hummingbird says the Compact is thinking about balance incorrectly."*

### The Liberators
A coalition of former Animists, animal rights advocates, and spiritual entities. Their methods range from peaceful advocacy to direct action (helping bonded souls escape, which causes Ruptures and is therefore illegal).
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I carried seven souls for twelve years. When I released them, they didn't scatter. They waited. Three of them chose to rebond immediately. Two left and didn't return. Two-I still don't know what they chose. That's the part the Compact doesn't want in the Registry."*

### The Awakened Voices
Animists who can communicate dialogically with their bonded souls and are increasingly serving as translators in the debate between Compact and Liberators. Their testimony is politically inconvenient for both sides.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"My eagle spent three months asking me detailed questions about what I would do after she was gone. I realized she was deciding. She made a choice to stay. I don't know if that makes bonding ethical or if it means she's just very patient."*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Soul Manifest Deck

**Soul Manifest:**
- Maximum 5 bonded souls.
- Each soul has: Species, Essence Rating (1-3), Domain, and Harmony (starts at 5).

**Using Souls:**
- Declare soul and domain: +1d6 per Essence Rating to domain-appropriate actions.
- Cost: -1 Harmony from that soul.

**Harmony Restoration:**
- Spend 1 full scene living in alignment with the soul's nature: +1 Harmony.
- Genuine insight about the soul's nature or meaningful connection with them: +2 Harmony (Storyteller's discretion).

**Spiritual Rupture:**
- Occurs when a soul's Harmony reaches 0 and the Animist refuses to release.
- Immediate Severe psychological condition.
- Soul leaves involuntarily-the Animist cannot bond any soul for one session.

**Awakened Communicator (advanced ability):**
- Available at the start of a second campaign with an Animist character, or as a special narrative development.
- Can have a two-way conversation with bonded souls.
- Each soul has a personality, preferences, and opinions. The Storyteller plays the souls.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Compact Field Hunter
A senior Animist with a full manifest of five souls, all long-term bonds. She is simultaneously the Compact's most effective field operative and its most uncomfortable employee-her eagle soul has been in dialogue with her for eight years and has begun asking questions she can't answer in Compact-acceptable terms.
- **Quote:** *"The eagle's name is not a sound I can make. I think of her as 'Thermals.' She's been asking me the same question for three years: 'If you died, what would happen to me?' I've been looking for an answer in the Compact's literature. I haven't found one."*
- **Suggested Descriptors:** *"Wolf-soul intuition for pack dynamics in any group"*, *"Eagle-soul spatial awareness at a level that is technically impossible for a human"*, *"Dolphin-soul communication ability that works across distances that should prevent it"*, *"Eight years of dialogue with a soul who makes me question everything I think I know about what I do".*
- **Drive:** *"Find an honest answer to Thermals' question."*
- **Flaw:** *"I am very good at my job. I am increasingly uncertain that 'my job' is ethical."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### Archetype 2: The Liberator Convert
A former Compact Hunter who released all five of their souls two years ago after a dialogue session revealed that one of their bonded souls had been in sustained distress for six of the eight years they were bonded. The soul had never expressed this during power use-only in dialogue, which the Animist had only recently developed the ability to engage in. Now works with the Liberators to help Animists develop dialogue ability, believing that Animists who truly hear their souls will make better ethical decisions.
- **Quote:** *"She was in pain for six years. She showed me the power I asked for. She never showed me the pain because I never asked. I didn't know to ask. I know now. That's the only thing I can do with what I learned."*
- **Suggested Descriptors:** *"Former five-soul Compact Hunter-knows every regulation and exactly why each one is insufficient"*, *"Has a method for developing Awakened Communication that the Compact considers unauthorized"*, *"The two souls from my former manifest who chose to stay nearby as free entities occasionally help me"*, *"I know what Spiritual Rupture feels like from the inside. I survived it. I want to help others avoid it."*
- **Drive:** *"Build a practice of Animism that starts with listening."*
- **Flaw:** *"My certainty that I was wrong before makes me too certain I'm right now."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

### Archetype 3: The Newly Bonded
A person who absorbed their first soul three weeks ago-accidentally, at the death of a family pet. They haven't registered with the Compact. They don't know the Liberators exist. The soul is a border collie with a very specific set of opinions about everything, including the fact that this human needs to make some decisions soon.
- **Quote:** *"She won't stop helping me. Every time I'm in a situation she thinks I'm managing poorly, I get a herding instinct. She's been herding me for three weeks. I don't know what to do. She seems to know exactly what to do. We are having a communication breakdown."*
- **Suggested Descriptors:** *"Accidental first-time Animist with no training and a border collie soul who has very strong opinions"*, *"Manages the herding instinct with varying success"*, *"Has no idea what Harmony means but has instinctively been maintaining it through being very attentive to the dog's preferences"*, *"The dog apparently likes jazz, open spaces, and clearly organized plans. We are compatible in two of three areas."*
- **Drive:** *"Figure out what this is, what it means, and what the dog actually wants-ideally in that order."*
- **Flaw:** *"I keep making decisions based on what I think the dog wants, which is not the same as asking the dog what she wants."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

## 6. Condition Palette

**Physical Conditions:**
- **Minor:** *Soul Static* (too many souls active simultaneously-sensory overload), *Harmony Flicker* (a soul's Harmony is very low-they are less responsive, more distressed).
- **Moderate:** *Essence Bleed* (a soul's qualities are bleeding into the Animist's base personality unbidden), *Multi-Manifest Strain* (running all five souls simultaneously-physical exhaustion from the spiritual load).
- **Severe:** *Spiritual Rupture* (a soul has left violently-no soul bonding for one session, severe psychological condition), *Soul Addiction* (dependence on soul-power to the point where natural human function feels insufficient).

**Social Conditions:**
- **Minor:** *Compact Attention* (your unregistered or irregular practice has been noticed), *Liberator Contact* (approached for recruitment or information).
- **Moderate:** *Compact Investigation* (formal inquiry into your soul manifest practices), *Liberator Action* (they've decided to attempt to free one of your bonded souls, whether you want them to or not).
- **Severe:** *Compact Suspension* (license suspended, all soul use considered illegal), *Soul Tribunal* (formal proceeding to determine the status of contested bonded souls).

**Psychological Conditions:**
- **Minor:** *Species Bleed* (thinking patterns temporarily influenced by a dominant soul), *Harmony Guilt* (awareness that you haven't restored a soul's Harmony in too long).
- **Moderate:** *Identity Diffusion* (uncertain which thoughts are yours and which are the souls'), *Ethical Paralysis* (unable to use soul powers due to moral uncertainty about the practice).
- **Severe:** *Soul Grief* (a beloved soul has left or been freed-profound loss regardless of circumstances), *Soul Hoarding* (pathological resistance to releasing any soul even at Harmony 0-Rupture risk at maximum).

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Dialogue Request

**Framing:** A registered Compact Hunter has submitted an extraordinary formal request to the Soul Tribunal: she wants her bonded wolf soul to testify. The wolf-who she has been in dialogue with for four years-has requested the opportunity to speak for itself in the formal proceeding about whether the Compact's bonding protocols should be reformed. This has never happened. The Compact doesn't technically have a protocol for it. The Liberators see it as the case they've been waiting for. The media is covering it. Every Animist with a dialogue-capable relationship is being asked whose side they're on.

The characters must navigate the political pressure from both organized factions while the actual wolf soul-who has an agenda more nuanced than either side wants-waits to speak.

**3-Layer Scene Design:**
1. **Immediate Threat:** The Compact's most senior faction wants to quietly deny the testimony request before it sets a precedent. They've asked the registering Hunter to withdraw the request "voluntarily." She's refused. They're escalating.
2. **Ticking Clock:** The Tribunal hearing is in 72 hours. If the testimony request is denied procedurally before then, it cannot be appealed for five years. If it's granted and the wolf testifies poorly (or says something that validates the Compact's worst fears), the resulting crackdown will set Animist dialogue practice back a generation.
3. **Opportunity:** Three other dialogue-capable Animists in the city have received quiet communications from their bonded souls: the souls are aware of the proceeding. They have things they want said. They've been waiting for this moment longer than the Animists knew.

**Antagonist: Compact Director Ellara Voss** - A brilliant administrator who genuinely believes the Compact's regulatory framework protects both Animists and bonded souls from worse outcomes than regulated practice produces. She is not wrong about the alternative history where the Compact doesn't exist. She is absolutely wrong about what the bonded souls would tell a Tribunal if given the chance-and she knows it, and that's why she's trying to prevent it.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

The relationship between humans and animals is one of the oldest moral questions in human history-we have used animals for food, labor, companionship, and spiritual practice since before recorded history, and the ethical frameworks we've developed around this use are incomplete, contradictory, and deeply culturally specific. The Soul-Bound setting makes this relationship literal and unavoidable: you are carrying another being's soul, and you cannot avoid reckoning with what that means. The Harmony mechanic forces regular moments of alignment with the bonded soul's nature-not just using their power, but living in their terms. The dialogue ability makes the animals active participants in their own situation rather than resources. Together, these create a setting where the central ethical question cannot be resolved by ignoring it, and where the most interesting dramatic moments happen in the relationship between an Animist and the souls they carry.
