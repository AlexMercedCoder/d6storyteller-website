## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** Set in the deep pre-Classic era of Mesoamerica (c. 1200–400 BCE) in the lush river basins of Tabasco and Veracruz, *The Jaguar of Olman* explores the origin story of Mesoamerican civilization: the **Olmec**. As the first sculptors of colossal stone heads and masters of rubber-working, the Olmec elite carry the sacred blood of the *Were-Jaguar* (*Tepeyollotl's Children*). Players embody the ritual protectors of La Venta and San Lorenzo, defending the jade portals against shadow entities from the underworld.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Colossal basalt stone heads carved with serene, powerful faces staring out over green rainforest clearings; glowing green jade celts polished to a mirror shine; heavy rubber balls bouncing in ceremonial ball courts.
2. **Sound:** The deep, rumbling growl of a jaguar echoing through mangrove roots; rain pouring over giant clay ceremonial platforms; the clinking of jade beads against obsidian pectorals.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The smooth, cool weight of polished jade against skin; the distinctive scent of natural rubber sap and burning copal; damp river clay beneath bare feet.

**The Core Tension:** Creation vs. Oblivion. As the first great Mesoamerican culture, every ritual and discovery sets the template for thousands of years of future civilizations. Failure means wiping out the spiritual foundation of the world.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

The Olmec are universally recognized as the "mother culture" (*cultura madre*) of Mesoamerica, establishing iconic traditions: monumental stone carving, jade carving, ceremonial ballgames, and theWere-Jaguar deity motif. Sites like La Venta, San Lorenzo, and Tres Zapotes form the heartland of this ancient world.

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** Olmec archaeology is foundational to Indigenous Mexican heritage. This setting honors the artistic, architectural, and spiritual genius of the Olmec people.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Were-Jaguar Bloodline (Tepeyollotl Kin)
Shapeshifter priest-warriors who embody the raw strength and stealth of the jaguar.
- **Cultural Association:** Guardians of sacred jade offerings and colossal stone monuments.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"My eyes reflect the green light of raw jade in total darkness"*
  - *"I can leap across river gorges with the force of a hunting jaguar"*

### The Rubber-Ball Masters (Ulama Keepers)
Athletes and ritualists who play the sacred rubber ballgame to maintain cosmic order.
- **Cultural Association:** Champions of physical agility, orbital symbolism, and elite diplomacy.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"My hip strikes can propel a heavy rubber ball through stone rings"*
  - *"I have never lost a ceremonial match when the sun's position was at stake"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Jaguar Co-Essence

Characters track an **Olman Essence Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Human):** +1d6 to Mind and Presence. Stone portals are dormant.
- **3–4 (Awakened):** +1d6 to Finesse. Can sense jade deposits and subterranean water veins. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5–6 (Jaguar Apex):** +1d6 to Force and Finesse. Superhuman leaping and claws. Failure triggers wild predatory fury.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Were-Jaguar Priest
- **Concept:** A sacred guardian who protects the jade caches of La Venta.
- **Descriptors:** *Wielder of the Were-Jaguar spirit*, *Master of ceremonial jade celts*, *Spiritual leader of San Lorenzo*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the first jade portals pure from underworld corruption."*
- **Flaw:** *"Prone to violent feral outbursts when threatened."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### Archetype 2: The Colossal Stone Sculptor
- **Concept:** A master artisan who carves the legendary basalt heads.
- **Descriptors:** *Carver of the colossal stone heads*, *Knows how to move multi-ton basalt blocks*, *Voice like grinding stone*.
- **Drive:** *"Immortalize our rulers and gods in unbreakable stone."*
- **Flaw:** *"Slow to act without thorough deliberation."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### Archetype 3: The Rubber-Game Champion
- **Concept:** An elite athlete whose victories keep cosmic balance.
- **Descriptors:** *Master of the sacred Ulama ballgame*, *Agile as a river otter*, *Icon to young warriors*.
- **Drive:** *"Win every ritual match to ensure bountiful maize harvests."*
- **Flaw:** *"Overly competitive in non-court situations."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 4 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Awakening at La Venta

- **Framing:** Underworld entities threaten to crack the central basalt monument at La Venta during an eclipse.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Defending the stone head from shadow-beasts.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Solar eclipse lasting 4 beats.
  3. *Opportunity:* Placing four ceremonial jade celts at the compass points to seal the rift.
- **Antagonist:** *The Shadow-Jaguar of Xibalba*.
  - *Traits:* Spectral claws, dark smoke aura.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Shadow Form / ☐ Distorted / ☐ Banished.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

The Olmec established the artistic and spiritual foundations for all Mesoamerican civilizations that followed. This setting celebrates the dawn of Mesoamerican myth and architecture.
