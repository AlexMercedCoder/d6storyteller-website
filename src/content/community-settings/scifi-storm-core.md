> *"Thirteen people died in the Gale-7 incident. The Consortium called it a weather event outside modeling parameters. We called it a maintenance failure. The difference cost thirteen people their lives and cost the Consortium twelve hours of production delay."*
> - Storm Workers' Union annual report, 2219

## 1. Setting Overview

Storm Core civilizations are powered by drama. Every city's lights depend on manufactured storms being successfully harvested by people riding platforms into the most dangerous weather conditions on the planet. The workers know this gives them leverage. They have used it.

The Storm Workers' Union is the most powerful labor organization in the world not because its members are particularly politically sophisticated, but because when they strike, the lights go out. City governments negotiate with the Union the way they negotiate with weather: seriously, respectfully, aware that the thing they are negotiating with does not actually care about their preferences.

The automated array announcement changes that calculus.

**Tone:** Industrial labor drama with spectacular working conditions. The political conflict is familiar - automation vs. workers - set in a context where the stakes are physically enormous and the workers can cause power outages that last weeks.

## 2. The Storm Economy

**Storm Platforms** are man-made structures that ride manufactured weather events, anchored by evocation specialists who keep them stable enough for energy collection arrays to function. Operating a platform requires constant active attention. A moment of inattention at Storm Attunement 3 is survivable. At Storm Attunement 1, it is not.

**The Evocation Consortium** runs the storm farms and employs the workers. It also funds the research that produced the automated array and has been aware of the automation timeline for six years without telling the Union.

**The Automated Array** can theoretically harvest a storm event without human operators. It is currently 80% reliable. The Consortium considers 80% reliable acceptable. Storm workers consider anything below 99.9% unacceptable, because below 99.9% means more Gale-7 incidents.

## 3. Factions

**The Storm Workers' Union** - The principal labor power. Currently calling an emergency session on automation. Internal debate between strike-now and negotiate-first factions.

**The Evocation Consortium** - The employer. Currently hoping the automation announcement catches the Union off-guard enough to delay a meaningful response.

**Independent Storm Riders** - Non-Union specialists who work freelance, mostly for emergency storm events. Complicated relationship with the Union (they undercut rates; the Union saved them twice when their employers abandoned them in dangerous conditions).

## 4. Unique Mechanic

**Storm Attunement (1-6) + Charge Pool (Attunement x2):**
In a storm event: spend 1 Charge for +1d6 per Attunement to Force or energy actions.
Outside a storm: sense manufactured or natural storms within 10 km, know composition.
Charge fully restores between storm events.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Platform Veteran
20 years on storm platforms. Attunement 5. Has survived three events that killed colleagues. Was on the preliminary automation test team before resigning when she realized what the data would be used for.
- **Drive:** *"Make the Union understand that the 80% reliability figure is not a technical problem - it is a policy choice."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### The Consortium Engineer
Designed one component of the automated array. Proud of the work. Also has access to the full reliability dataset, which shows 80% under normal conditions and 47% in storm events above Category 4 - which make up 30% of all harvests.
- **Drive:** *"Correct the reliability figure in the public announcement before the Union bases a decision on wrong numbers."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario

**The Category 5 Test:** The Consortium has scheduled a live demonstration of the automated array in a Category 5 manufactured storm. The Union has called a work stoppage for the same day. The demonstration site is staffed by automation-only. The Category 5 parameters are outside the test dataset. The emergency response protocol for a failed automated harvest requires human operators - who are on strike. The storm is already being manufactured. It cannot be un-manufactured.
