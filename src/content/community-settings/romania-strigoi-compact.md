## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The Strigoi are real, they've been real for centuries, and the reason civilization persists is an agreement that most people don't know about.

Romanian folklore surrounding the Strigoi is extremely rich and significantly different from the literary vampire tradition: the Strigoi can be living (people with certain birth signs who can send their souls out at night) or undead (those who rose from improper burial). They drain vitality in a slow, family-focused pattern rather than with sudden lethal violence. They can be detected and destroyed by specific traditional means. And they have been a managed reality in Transylvanian communities for as long as anyone remembers.

The Cunnoateri (the "those who know") are real: the community members who have the traditional knowledge to identify Strigoi, manage them, and perform the protective rituals. The fictional extension of this setting is that some Cunnoateri have developed the compact -- a formal arrangement that transforms the Strigoi problem from crisis management to ongoing governance.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Transylvanian landscape as it genuinely is: mixed-religion villages, ancient forests, the Carpathian mountain backdrop, and the layered architectural evidence of centuries of overlapping cultures; a village cemetery where the graves are properly arranged and the crossroads nearby are marked with the specific protective signs only the Cunnoateri fully understand.
2. **Sound:** The seasonal sounds of a Transylvanian village, the church bells that serve double duty as both sacred call and Strigoi-repellent at specific times, the sound of a compact negotiation (formal, careful, conducted with the specific courtesies the tradition requires), and the absence of sound that means a Strigoi is nearby and the local fauna has noticed.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The smell of garlic and specific herbs that the knowledgeable use strategically, the cold of the Transylvanian winter that the Strigoi prefer, the warm communal spaces of a village that is more careful than it appears about what comes in from the cold, and the specific physical sensations that indicate someone has been visited by a Strigoi (easy to miss if you don't know what to look for, unmistakable once you do).

## 2. The Compact Framework

### The Honoring Strigoi
Living-dead who have accepted the compact's terms: limited feeding from willing participants, avoidance of family members without consent, no hunting of children. In exchange, they receive Cunnoateri protection from mob action and from other Strigoi who would exploit them. These Strigoi are the compact's enforcement on their side.

### The Rogue Strigoi
Those who never joined or who have violated the compact. They hunt without agreement, drain family members progressively, and give the entire Strigoi population the reputation that makes mob action against all of them a constant risk.

### The Cunnoateri Council
The humans who maintain the compact: Cunnoateri representatives from several villages who meet at established intervals to review compact performance and address violations. They have limited tools for enforcement and significant responsibility.

### The Church Investigation
The church knows something is going on. Their investigator is not wrong that there's an organized phenomenon. Their proposed solution (full eradication of all Strigoi) would end the compact and leave communities without the protection the honoring Strigoi provide. Navigating the investigation while maintaining the compact is an ongoing challenge.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The Compact Violation:** A compact-honoring Strigoi has broken terms. The Cunnoateri Council must decide on consequences -- but the violation was provoked by a human action that also violated the agreement. Both sides have legitimate grievances. A solution that addresses both without collapsing the compact is required.

2. **The New Strigoi:** A recently deceased community member has risen as Strigoi. They are newly dead and confused, not yet fully into the Strigoi nature, and currently targeting their own family in the classic progression. Approaching them before they fully become what they're becoming offers a chance to integrate them into the compact -- if they're willing.

3. **The Solomonar's Intervention:** One of the legendary weather-sorcerers has arrived and has opinions about the compact. They believe the arrangement is sustainable. They also believe it requires revision. Their proposed revisions are extensive. Their power to enforce their preferences is significant.

4. **The Rogue Network:** Multiple rogue Strigoi in a region have apparently been in contact with each other and are operating as a coordinated group. This has never happened before. The compact-honoring Strigoi are alarmed. The Cunnoateri must investigate whether this is organized resistance to the compact or something more dangerous.

5. **The Old Strigoi:** An ancient Strigoi -- pre-compact, pre-Christian, ancient enough to remember the original Dacian traditions -- has awakened. They are not party to the compact. They have their own code that predates the current arrangement. Determining whether they can be integrated or must be dealt with requires engaging with a being whose perspective on centuries is entirely different from any living person's.
