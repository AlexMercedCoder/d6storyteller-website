## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** In the kingdom of Solvaria, magic is real, functional, and profoundly annoying to maintain. The **Itinerant Thaumaturges** (IT) guild keeps civilization running through a combination of arcane expertise, sleep deprivation, and a deep, bone-level resentment of users who think magic is simple.

The players work in the IT Dungeon (it's literally in the dungeon; real estate in the castle was expensive and the dungeon was empty since they replaced the rack with an enchanted alternative). Their workspace features:
- **Crystal Ball Network (CBN):** 400 interlocked crystal balls carrying messages, scrying feeds, and royal communications, all maintained by three people
- **The Arcane Array:** A room-sized magical construct running on an operating system written by a wizard who died 200 years ago and left no documentation
- **The Ticket Queue:** A magical ledger that fills itself in faster than it can be emptied and occasionally gains sentience

**Tone:** This is pure workplace comedy with a fantasy skin. The nobility are oblivious power users. The IT staff are competent, exhausted, and increasingly philosophical. The problems are genuinely medieval (dragon attack on the server room, enchantment memory leaks, a noble who keeps trying to pirate their crystal ball content) combined with the eternal problems of IT work (nobody reads the documentation, everybody thinks they know better, the budget is always getting cut).

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A dungeon lit by floating arcane foxfire spheres (energy-efficient, maintenance nightmare), walls covered in chalked network diagrams that haven't been accurate since the last "upgrade," shelves of grimoires that are actually technical manuals for systems that no longer exist, and a help desk counter with a queue ticket dispenser that has never been empty.
2. **Sound:** The low harmonic hum of 400 crystal balls in standby, the clatter of a message-scroll printer that jams every sixth scroll, distant shouting from a noble who cannot get his enchanted quill to connect to the CBN, and the dead silence of a Monday morning before the first complaint arrives.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Cold stone floors and the slightly ozone smell of active enchantments, the papery dust of ancient documentation grimoires, the warmth radiating from the Arcane Array's processing core when it's under load, and the lukewarm tea that sustains IT staff through 14-hour shifts.

**The Core Tension:** The IT staff have the actual power to hold civilization together or let it collapse, but no political authority, no budget, and no respect. Every crisis is solvable but solving it requires navigating institutional absurdity.

## 2. The IT Dungeon Structure

### First Tier: Help Desk
The front line. Two staff members. They handle the Queen's advisor who cannot figure out how to access her scrying account, the knight whose enchanted armor keeps sending error runes, and the baker who insists their bread oven needs "network access" for reasons that remain unclear.

### Second Tier: Network Operations
The CBN team. They know where every packet of magical information travels through the kingdom. This knowledge has made them profoundly calm in emergencies and profoundly irritated in normal times.

### Third Tier: The Arcane Array Specialists
The people who maintain the core magical infrastructure. They speak to each other in jargon that even other IT staff find opaque. They have not taken vacation in several years. They are aware the Array has three critical vulnerabilities and they wrote a 40-page report about it in 1423 that nobody read.

### The Director of Thaumaturgy
A political appointee who does not understand magic, manages the IT department's budget with aggressive optimism, and regularly proposes "innovative solutions" that are technically impossible. The players must regularly explain why reality does not work the way the Director believes it does.

## 3. Common Tickets (Adventure Seeds)

1. **TICKET-8847: Dragon on Server Rack:** A juvenile dragon has nested on the Arcane Array's primary processing core, attracted by the heat signature. Cannot simply remove dragon. Cannot simply lose the Arcane Array. The noble who donated the dragon as a "morale gift" is asking for updates.

2. **TICKET-8901: The King's Scrying Mirror:** The King cannot open his scrying mirror. He has tried explaining this four times in increasingly large letters. He believes the problem is with the mirror. The mirror is fine. The problem is the King has caps lock on his magical authentication rune. Explaining this requires political navigation at the highest level.

3. **TICKET-8823: Unauthorized Enchantment:** A mid-level lord has installed a personal enchantment on the CBN that is consuming 40% of total magical bandwidth to stream crystal-ball entertainment from the offshore content guild. Removing it requires proof of unauthorized use. The lord is on the IT oversight committee.

4. **TICKET-9001: Critical - Arcane Array Blue Rune of Death:** The Array has crashed. This has taken down the CBN, the royal scrying network, the castle gates (enchanted), and the enchanted plumbing for seven floors of the palace. Nobody outside IT has noticed yet. They have approximately 45 minutes.

5. **TICKET-8756: Documentation Request:** Someone needs documentation for a magical system installed in 1243. No documentation exists. The original implementer is dead. The system controls something important. Players must reverse-engineer it before a scheduled maintenance window requires them to actually modify it.
