> *"The west ward held for six weeks against everything Consortium could throw at it. It fell in eleven minutes when a Ward-Breaker found a maintenance gap in the residential overlay. Six weeks of engineering lost to eleven minutes of oversight."*
> - Ward Engineer Command debrief, northern theater, Conflict 7

## 1. Setting Overview

The Warded Worlds have not had mass casualties in warfare for 40 years. This is sometimes called progress. What it actually means is that warfare has become a profession for specialists - Ward Engineers and Ward-Breakers - rather than a mass mobilization. Civilian populations are not killed in ward-to-ward conflict. They are economically strangled as their city's resources are choked off. Sieges last longer. Suffering is more prolonged and less spectacular.

The Ward-Breakers' Guild operates as a mercenary contractor neutral to all national allegiances. Their code requires that they not assist in penetrating residential wards - commercial, military, and governmental wards only. This code is interpreted creatively by members who are paid well enough.

**Tone:** Military thriller with an engineering brain. The stakes in any given engagement are measured in hours of maintenance and centimeters of gap in the ward geometry, not in weapons.

## 2. The Ward Architecture Industry

**National Ward Academies** train Ward Engineers in 6-year programs. Graduates are assigned to the National Ward Command and serve 10-year mandatory contracts. The best are assigned to major city defense networks.

**The Ward-Breakers' Guild** recruits from former Ward Engineers and from independent talent. Guild members operate under contract, neutral flag, and professional code. Guild fees are based on Ward Rating difficulty: a Rating 6 penetration costs more than most nations' annual defense budgets.

**Gray Zone Contractors** - Ward-Breakers who operate outside the Guild, for clients the Guild will not take, at rates the Guild will not accept. Technically illegal. Practically very busy.

## 3. Factions

**The Ward Command (multi-national)** - The collective of national Ward Engineer corps. Technically hostile to each other's nations. Maintains a professional information-sharing network because ward architecture standards benefit everyone.

**The Ward-Breakers' Guild** - Neutral mercenary organization. Currently dealing with a member accused of breaching a residential ward during a conflict, which would void the neutrality that makes the Guild valuable.

**The Vulnerable City** - A mid-tier city whose Ward Engineer corps was gutted by budget cuts. Their Ward Rating 2 network is publicly known. Every neighboring nation has noted it.

## 4. Unique Mechanic

**Ward Ratings (1-6) and Penetration:**
- Action against a ward: roll vs. Ward Rating as difficulty.
- Ward-Breaker Penetration: spend 1 scene analyzing, reduce effective Rating by Finesse for 1 attempt.
- Ward Engineer: raise Rating by 1 per hour of work (max 6).
- Rating 6: stops all conventional force; requires Masterwork Penetration (Finesse vs. difficulty 5).

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Ward Engineer
Maintains the eastern residential overlay of a mid-tier city. Knows the Ward Rating is 2 in three sections due to budget cuts. Her repair requests have been denied for 18 months. Has started keeping personal documentation of the denied requests.
- **Drive:** *"Create a record that shows the budget decision caused the breach, before the breach happens."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

### The Guild Ward-Breaker
Senior member assigned to investigate the residential ward breach accusation against a colleague. Problem: the colleague is the Guild's most technically capable member, and the investigation is being funded by the nation that benefited from the breach.
- **Drive:** *"Conduct an investigation that is honest, knowing the result may be used dishonestly."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario

**The Maintenance Gap:** The vulnerable city's Ward Command has contacted the characters - a Ward-Breaker who identified the gap in their defenses from outside and a Ward Engineer willing to work pro bono. The city cannot hire the Guild. They cannot fix the gap themselves. They need the gap patched before their eastern neighbor's diplomatic crisis becomes a military one. The eastern neighbor's Ward-Breakers have been seen on the border road for three days.
