## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** In the lush cloud forests and coastal deltas of Costa Rica, the **Boruca (Brunka)** nation maintains the sacred secret of the **Stone Spheres of Diquís**—perfectly spherical megaliths carved from granodiorite under the divine inspiration of the creator god *Sibö*. During the historic *Baile de los Diablitos* (Dance of the Little Devils), Boruca warriors wear fierce carved wooden balsa masks representing forest spirits to repel Spanish conquistadors (represented as the Bull).

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Perfectly smooth stone spheres ranging from tennis-ball size to two meters across, half-covered in rainforest orchids; vibrant hand-carved balsa masks with jaguar fangs and devil horns; the iridescent emerald tail feathers of quetzals gliding through canopy fog.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic drumming of hollow log drums and balsa maracas; the roaring chant of masked Boruca dancers echoing through narrow mountain gorges; rain pattering softly on giant monstera leaves.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The unnervingly smooth, cool surface of granodiorite stone spheres; the scent of freshly carved balsa wood and natural berry dyes; crisp, moisture-laden cloud forest air.

**The Core Tension:** Harmony vs. Encroachment. Protecting the sacred balance of Sibö's creation against a force that seeks to mine the mountains and shatter the spheres.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

Costa Rica is famous for the mysterious Diquís stone spheres (a UNESCO World Heritage site) and the living tradition of the *Baile de los Diablitos* celebrated by the Boruca indigenous community. In Bribri and Boruca cosmology, *Sibö* created the earth and placed the stone spheres as cannonballs of thunder god *Tara* to defend the land.

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** The Boruca mask-making and *Baile de los Diablitos* traditions are sacred symbols of cultural survival. This setting presents Boruca heritage with deep respect for indigenous craftsmanship and environmental harmony.

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Order of the Sphere-Carvers (Diquís Artisans)
Master stonemasons who use ancient sand-rubbing and heat techniques to shape granodiorite into perfectly balanced spheres.
- **Cultural Association:** Geometers, earth-callers, and keepers of Sibö's law.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can feel the exact center of balance in any stone with a single touch"*
  - *"My hands can polish rough rock until it reflects the night sky"*

### The Masked Diablitos (Balsa Warriors)
Warriors who carve intricate devil and jaguar masks to embody forest spirits in battle.
- **Cultural Association:** Forest defenders, ritual dancers, and guerrilla tacticians.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"When I wear the jaguar mask, no Spanish horse can catch my stride"*
  - *"My dance turns the enemy's momentum back upon their own lines"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Harmonic Sphere Resonance

Characters track a **Sphere Balance Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Unsteady):** Normal rules. Stone manipulation is impossible.
- **3–4 (Harmonic):** +1d6 to Mind and Finesse. Can roll or reposition stone spheres effortlessly regardless of weight. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5–6 (Granite Resonance):** +1d6 to Force and immunity to physical harm. Failure turns skin briefly to stone (reduces Finesse to 1d6).

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Master Mask-Carver (Diablito Leader)
- **Concept:** A Boruca artisan who crafts empowered balsa masks for the ritual dance.
- **Descriptors:** *Carver of legendary spirit masks*, *Leader of the Baile de los Diablitos*, *Knows every secret trail in Talamanca*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep our culture unbroken through the dance of resistance."*
- **Flaw:** *"Refuses to fight without my sacred mask."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### Archetype 2: The Diquís Stone-Master
- **Concept:** An elder who knows how to awaken the dormant power of the stone spheres.
- **Descriptors:** *Keeper of the granodiorite spheres*, *Channels Sibö's thunder*, *Built like the mountain rock*.
- **Drive:** *"Protect the sacred spheres from being looted or shattered."*
- **Flaw:** *"Slow to adapt when plans change quickly."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

- **Descriptors:** *Moves silently through Monteverde cloud mist*, *Guided by quetzal spirit omens*, *Expert archer*.
- **Drive:** *"Defend the virgin forest and its sacred creatures."*
- **Flaw:** *"Uncomfortable in open lowland spaces."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Charge of the Iron Bull

- **Framing:** A Spanish conquistador captain brings heavy horses and cannons into the Diquís valley to break a stone sphere for gold rumor.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* The *Baile de los Diablitos* dancers face a cavalry charge.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Cannon fuse lit; 3 beats until impact on the main sphere.
  3. *Opportunity:* Rolling a two-meter stone sphere down the slope to block the cannon path.
- **Antagonist:** *Captain Don Esteban de Cordoba* (The Iron Bull).
  - *Traits:* Heavy plate armor, ruthless mounted charge.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Mounted / ☐ Unhorsed / ☐ Broken Armor.
