> *"We do not extract what the earth cannot give. We have not extracted beyond the line's capacity in three thousand years. The Grid set its extraction rates by economic modeling, not by listening to the line. We have been trying to explain the difference."*
> - Line-Walker Elder testimony, Grid Authority public hearing, dismissed as anecdotal

## 1. Setting Overview

The Leyline Grid works. Cities are powered, warm, and productive. The engineers are competent and the infrastructure is maintained. The problem is not technical failure. It is the extraction rate, which was set by economists and geologists who understood nodes as quantified resources and did not consult the people who had been managing those resources for three millennia.

The Line-Walkers have documentation going back 300 years showing node depletion patterns. Their methodology is rigorous - they are not romantics claiming the earth has feelings. They are empiricists who have been measuring the same nodes for generations and know what overextraction looks like before the geological consequences become undeniable. They have been trying to enter that data into the official record for 40 years.

**Tone:** Ecological crisis drama set in a world where the crisis is geological and the authority that should be listening is not listening for structural reasons, not malicious ones.

## 2. The Grid Ecosystem

**Grid Authority Engineers** maintain the extraction infrastructure and are responsible for node monitoring. Their monitoring systems are sophisticated. They measure what the extraction equipment generates. They do not measure what the Line-Walkers measure.

**The Line-Walker Confederacy** is a coalition of nomadic cultures that have lived with the ley-lines for millennia. Their knowledge of node behavior is unmatched. Their legal standing to testify before the Grid Authority has been challenged at every regulatory hearing.

**The Deep Tremor** - a series of geological events in the western region that the Grid Authority attributes to natural seismic activity. The Line-Walkers say three of the affected nodes were at 90%+ depletion before the tremors. The Grid Authority says correlation is not causation.

## 3. Factions

**The Grid Authority** - The regulatory body that manages extraction rights and sets rates. Has 40 years of Line-Walker depletion data that has been formally received and not formally incorporated into rate calculations.

**The Line-Walker Confederacy** - Advocates for extraction rate reduction and node recovery periods. Currently in formal legal proceedings before the Grid Authority's Review Board.

**The Independent Geomancers** - Unaffiliated engineers who have reviewed the Line-Walker data and are professionally uncomfortable with the Rate Authority's methodology. A few have begun speaking publicly.

## 4. Unique Mechanic

**Node Resonance (1-6):**
- 3+: Sense node health, extraction rate, remaining capacity.
- 5+: Emergency Stabilization - stop depletion collapse for 1d6 hours (Mind roll difficulty 3).
- 80%+ depleted: All Resonance readings -1.
- 95%+ depleted: Node destabilizes, potential localized geological events.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Grid Authority Engineer
Assigned to the western monitoring district. Has been receiving Line-Walker depletion reports for three years as part of a data-sharing pilot program. Has run the numbers. The Line-Walkers are right about nodes 7, 12, and 23. Has not filed a formal report because she does not know what happens to her career if she does.
- **Drive:** *"File the report before node 12 gives her a reason to wish she had."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

### The Line-Walker Data Keeper
Third-generation custodian of the Confederacy's node documentation. Has three centuries of depletion data organized, cross-referenced, and ready for submission. Has been trying to find a Grid Authority engineer willing to formally receive it for four years.
- **Drive:** *"Get the data into the official record before the western nodes trigger an event that makes the data moot."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario

**Node 12:** A Line-Walker sensor at Node 12 in the western district has gone dark - not mechanically, but because the node has gone quiet in the way the Line-Walker data says precedes total depletion. The Grid Authority shows Node 12 at 67% capacity. The Line-Walkers show it at 93% depletion. Both sets of measurements are accurate - they are measuring different things. The Grid Authority's next extraction draw from Node 12 is scheduled in 18 hours.
