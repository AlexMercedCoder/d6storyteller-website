## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

The Pearl Republic is a nation built on the edge of the Laran Sea shelf, its harbor positioned directly above the sunken city of Aneth. The Republic's founding generation were fishers who discovered that the pearls forming in Aneth's ruins were different from ordinary pearls: dense, warm to the touch, and containing, when cracked open in the correct ritual manner, a complete experiential memory from the civilization they came from.

The Republic has grown wealthy by harvesting, authenticating, and trading these memories. Foreign scholars pay enormous sums for authenticated Aneth memories. The Republic's own citizens use them as a form of education, cultural practice, and occasionally therapy. The ethical questions this raises have been debated for two hundred years without resolution.

**Tone:** Beautiful, melancholic, and morally complex. The setting asks what it means to carry someone else's complete sensory experience, whether the dead have rights over their memories, and what happens to a society that treats the past as a tradable commodity.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Republic's harbor at dawn: divers departing in small boats as the water shifts from black to green, the buoys marking the dive sites above Aneth's major districts, the pearl sorting houses on the dockside where authenticated memories are catalogued and stored. The ruins themselves: visible from thirty feet in clear water, the mineralized city preserved in extraordinary detail, buildings intact, streets legible, and the pearl-growths clustered most densely in the former residential quarters.
2. **Sound:** The particular silence of deep diving, broken by the diver's own heartbeat and the low resonance of the mineralized city's acoustic properties. The cracking sound when a pearl is opened correctly: a specific tone that experienced handlers can distinguish from incorrect openings. The murmur of a diver recounting a memory to a transcriptionist, trying to find words for a sensory experience that came from someone else.
3. **Touch and Smell:** The weight and warmth of an authenticated pearl, which holds body temperature regardless of the water it was retrieved from. The cold shock of the Laran Sea, which the divers acclimate to gradually and which never fully feels natural. The smell of the sorting houses: salt, polish compound, and the specific chemical trace of pearl-mineral that authenticators learn to identify.

## 2. The Republic

**The Dive Authority** manages all sanctioned retrieval operations: dive site licensing, diver certification, and the authentication process that distinguishes genuine Aneth pearls from the natural pearls that form in the same waters. The Authority's Director, a former senior diver named Carla Soto, runs the operation with the precision of someone who has spent thirty years managing a valuable and irreplaceable resource.

**The Memory Archive** is the Republic's national collection of opened and transcribed memories. The Archive contains authenticated accounts of Aneth at every level of society, spanning approximately two centuries before the sinking. Its head archivist, Dr. Nev Wren, has dedicated her career to building a complete picture of Aneth from its fragments. She is aware that the picture can never be complete because the pearls represent only the people in whom the mineralization process occurred, which was not everyone.

**The Diver Neighborhoods** are where the Republic's divers live: working-class streets near the harbor, distinct from the scholarly and commercial districts that have grown up around the memory trade. The neighborhoods have a complex culture shaped by generations of people who regularly carry complete memories from dead strangers and must find ways to integrate those experiences into their own lives.

**The Deep Sites** are the restricted dive zones at the bottom of Aneth's administrative district, where the pearl concentrations are highest and the depths most dangerous. Access requires senior certification and Authority permission. The Deep Sites produce the most complete memories, including some that appear to be from Aneth's governing class, and the political content of those memories is occasionally inconvenient.

## 3. Factions

**The Dive Authority** represents the Republic's institutional interest in the memories as a managed resource. The Authority controls certification, site access, and authentication, which gives it enormous leverage over who benefits from the pearl trade. Critics argue that the Authority's certification fees systematically exclude divers from poorer families, concentrating access among those who can afford the training.

**The Memory Scholars** are the academics and researchers who study authenticated Aneth memories. Most are foreign academics on Republic visas. Their lead figure, Professor Orlan Dix, has published extensively on Aneth's pre-sinking culture and has become an internationally recognized authority on a civilization he experiences entirely through other people's memories. He treats the memories as texts. The Republic's divers treat them as experiences.

**The Diver Collective** advocates for divers' rights: better pay, safer conditions, and most importantly a voice in how the memories are used after authentication. Collective leader Mira Sull argues that the Aneth dead's memories belong to the community that retrieves them, not the institutions that trade them. She has introduced the concept of "memory consent" into the Republic's legal discourse, arguing that using someone's complete experiential memory for commercial profit requires ethical consideration.

**The Aneth Revivalists** believe the pearl memories contain enough information to reconstruct Aneth's culture, language, and social practices as a living tradition. Their leader, an elderly diver named Tam who has opened over three hundred pearls, argues that the Republic has inherited the obligation to carry Aneth forward, not just to sell it. Tam's movement is growing and makes the Authority uncomfortable because it treats the memories as something more than a commodity.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Memory Clarity

When a character opens a pearl, track **Memory Clarity** from 0 to 6.

**Clarity modifiers:**
- Diver is calm and focused: +2
- Diver is injured or frightened: -2
- Retrieval depth was within certification: no modifier
- Retrieval depth exceeded certification: -1
- Pearl was authenticated before opening: +1
- Pearl was damaged during retrieval: -2

**Clarity effects:**
| Clarity | Memory Quality |
|---------|----------------|
| 5-6 | Complete. Full sensory experience, coherent narrative, clear identity of the original person. |
| 3-4 | Clear. Vivid experience with occasional gaps. Identity is clear but some context is missing. |
| 1-2 | Fragment. Impressionistic. Emotional intensity without narrative coherence. Identity may be ambiguous. |
| 0 | Merged. Multiple memories layered together. Disorienting and potentially destabilizing for the opener. |

**Memory effects on the opener:** A character who opens multiple pearls in quick succession accumulates Memory Debt: the experiences blend with their own memories, making it harder to distinguish between their actual past and the experiences they have absorbed. Recovery requires rest and deliberate grounding work.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Senior Diver

You have been diving Aneth for fifteen years and have opened over two hundred pearls. You carry fragments of two hundred lives in a memory that is theoretically your own, and you have developed the professional practice of keeping them separate from your actual history. The Collective is right that divers deserve better pay and more agency. You are also the best diver in the Republic and the Authority knows it, which means you have leverage neither side is fully comfortable with you exercising.

- **Drive:** *"Find a way to use your position to change how the Republic treats the memories and the people who retrieve them, before the trade makes irreplaceable history into a commodity catalog."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Memory Scholar

You study Aneth through authenticated memories. You have experienced hundreds of Aneth lives through transcriptions and, occasionally, through direct opening of authenticated pearls. You are aware that your scholarship is entirely dependent on a trade that may be ethically compromised. You are also aware that the memories you study are irreplaceable records of a civilization that would otherwise be entirely lost. You live in the tension between these two facts.

- **Drive:** *"Complete the historical record of Aneth before the ethical questions about the pearl trade force the Republic to restrict access in ways that make your work impossible."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 4 | Presence 2

### The Revivalist Leader

You are Tam. You have opened more pearls than anyone alive and you believe you understand what Aneth was better than any scholar who has only read transcriptions. You believe the Republic has an obligation to Aneth that goes beyond commerce: to carry their language, their practices, and their community forward as a living culture, not just an archive. The Authority considers your movement a challenge to its jurisdiction. The scholars consider it romanticism. Your divers consider it a calling.

- **Drive:** *"Build a living Aneth tradition from the memories your community has spent generations retrieving, before the commercial pressure on the pearls consumes the meaning of what you are diving for."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 2 | Presence 4

## 6. Starter Scenario and Why It Matters

**The Governor's Pearl:** A diver has retrieved a pearl from the Deep Site's administrative district that, when opened by the Authority's senior authenticator, appears to contain the memory of Aneth's last governor, recorded in the final hours before the sinking. The memory contains, among other things, a complete account of why the city sank: not a geological event but a deliberate act by Aneth's own emergency council, who flooded the city rather than surrender it to an approaching fleet. The fleet belonged to a predecessor state of the current Republic. The Republic's founding mythology treats the sinking as a tragedy. The pearl describes a choice. The Authority wants to seal the pearl. The Revivalists want to open it publicly. The Scholar wants to authenticate and publish. The Collective wants the diver who retrieved it to have control over what happens to it. The characters must navigate the conflicting claims while determining what the Republic owes its founding tragedy.

**Why It Matters:** Pearl Republic asks who owns the past. The Aneth dead cannot consent to their memories being traded, studied, or used to rewrite the Republic's founding narrative. The divers who retrieve the memories are paid workers who have no authority over the product they produce. The scholars who study the memories are guests who have no claim over the culture they analyze. The Republic that sells the memories treats its deepest inheritance as inventory. The setting is about the ethics of memory, the commodification of the dead, and the question of who speaks for a civilization that cannot speak for itself.
