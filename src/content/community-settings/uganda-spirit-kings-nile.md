## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The Great Lakes region of East Africa is home to one of Africa's most fascinating historical legends: the **Bachwezi Empire**. According to oral history in Uganda, Rwanda, and Tanzania, the Bachwezi were a mythical race of demi-god rulers who possessed supernatural knowledge, introduced cattle, ironworking, and crater-lake engineering, and then vanished suddenly into the deep crater lakes when their empire collapsed.

In this setting, the Bachwezi did not die; they stepped sideways into the spirit realm, leaving behind their **Embandwa** mediums and their **Engoma** (royal drums). Drums in Ugandan culture are not musical instruments - they are spiritual legal entities with titles, property, and living souls. The power of the Nile itself flows from the harmony between the living kings and the hidden Bachwezi realm.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The emerald green hills of Uganda cascading down to crater lakes of impossible depth; mist rising from Murchison Falls on the Victoria Nile; a golden Bachwezi spirit manifesting during a drum ceremony, translucent and glowing with celestial light.
2. **Sound:** The deep, booming bass of the Mujaguzo royal drums reverberating through the earth; the thundering roar of the Nile river rapids; the intricate polyrhythmic chanting of spirit mediums in full trance.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The damp, warm humidity of equatorial rainforest; the rich, earthy aroma of crushed banana leaves (*matooke*) cooking; the vibrations of drumheads shaking the chest.

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

Uganda's historical kingdoms (Buganda, Bunyoro-Kitara, Toro, Ankole) share deep connections to the **Bachwezi** legend and **Kubandwa** spirit-possession cults.

Key elements include the **Kabaka** (King of Buganda), **Engoma** (sacred drums as living regalia), and **Lake Victoria** (Nnalubaale - house of the gods).

--

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Embandwa Mediums
Initiates of the Bachwezi cult who allow the ancient spirit kings to speak through them.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"When King Wamara speaks through my throat, my eyes glow with crater-lake fire"*
  - *"I can read the spiritual currents of the Nile by watching the ripple of water across a drumhead"*

### The Royal Drum Keepers (Abakama b'Engoma)
Hereto-hereditary guardians of the living sacred drums of Buganda and Bunyoro.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"This drum was carved from a single sacred tree eight hundred years ago; it has never stopped vibrating"*
  - *"If a false king strikes this drum, his arms will turn to ash"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Engoma Resonance

- **1-2 (Unattuned):** Standard physical play. Cannot feel spirit rhythms.
- **3-4 (Attuned):** Can sense spiritual activity, predict rain, and communicate with river spirits. +1d6 to Mind. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5-6 (Bachwezi-Possessed):** Channeling a spirit king. +1d6 to all rolls. Can manipulate water currents, command animals, and levitate. **Risk:** The spirit king may refuse to leave the body.

## 6-7. Conditions & Starter Scenario

**Conditions:** *Drum-Deafened*, *Spirit-Possessed*, *Nile Water-Lungs*, *Royal Ban*, *Crater Lake Curse*.

**Starter Scenario: The Sunken Drum.** The sacred drum *Mujaguzo* has been stolen and thrown into a crater lake to prevent the crowning of the new Kabaka. The characters must dive into the supernatural depths of the crater lake, deal with the guardian water spirits, and recover the drum before the river dries up.
