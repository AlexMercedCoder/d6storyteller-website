> *"The conversion rate for stone-to-food is 10:1. The actual conversion cost is 1.8:1. The difference is the administrative fee that funds the Republic's civic services. This is not corruption. This is governance."*
> - Conversion Authority Director, parliamentary testimony, Year 58

## 1. Setting Overview

The Alchemical Republic demonstrates what happens when a technology that could eliminate scarcity meets an economy built on managing it. The technology works. The natural conversion ratios are efficient. People do not need to be hungry or lack materials. The Republic has not eliminated hunger. It has created a licensed system for converting materials at rates that make conversion affordable for the wealthy and technically accessible - at sacrifice - for the poor.

The outer districts, historically underserved, have the highest concentration of unlicensed alchemists. These are not people who trained in secret to undermine the state. They are people who needed food and had stone and learned that the conversion process is not actually complicated. The Republic's response has been to treat unlicensed conversion as a serious crime, because the economics of licensed conversion depend on it remaining the only legal option.

**Tone:** Political drama that asks who benefits from maintaining artificial scarcity after the means to end it already exist.

## 2. The Conversion Economy

**Licensed Alchemists** are the highest-paid professionals in the Republic. Their licenses require 8 years of training, significant fees, and binding agreement to operate only at published Conversion Authority rates.

**The Outer District Collective** is an informal network of unlicensed alchemists who convert at natural ratios for their communities. Their members have been arrested, had licenses denied, and had equipment confiscated. They have not stopped, because their neighbors need food and have stone.

**The Conversion Authority** administers rates, licenses, and enforcement. Employs licensed alchemists, legal teams, and enforcement officers. Is funded by the spread between natural ratios and published rates.

## 3. Factions

**The Conversion Authority** - The economic regulator. Genuinely believes the rate system funds important services. Also employs people whose entire careers depend on the current system.

**The Outer District Collective** - Unlicensed alchemists doing practical work. Currently facing an Authority crackdown following a viral broadcast showing a member converting rubble to food in two minutes at no cost to the community.

**The Parliamentary Reform Bloc** - A small political faction arguing for rate reform. Has been arguing for 15 years. Has not gotten a vote to the floor.

## 4. Unique Mechanic

**Natural Conversion Ratios (actual magical cost):**
- Simple: 1:1, no roll
- Moderate: 2:1, 1 Mind action
- Complex: 5:1, Mind roll difficulty 2
- Masterwork: 10:1, Mind roll difficulty 3

**Authority Rates** are 5-20x the natural cost. Licensed alchemists must charge Authority rates. Unlicensed conversion is a criminal offense.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Collective Alchemist
Unlicensed, Outer District, converts at natural ratios for community members. Currently the subject of an Authority warrant after the broadcast. Has nowhere to go that is not already known to the Authority.
- **Drive:** *"Keep converting until the parliamentary vote or until they take the equipment."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 4 | Presence 1

### The Reform Bloc Member
Parliamentary representative who has been pushing rate reform for 15 years. The viral broadcast may finally be the political moment she needs. Also aware that the Authority has significant political connections and the reform vote could end her career.
- **Drive:** *"Get the rate reform vote to the floor before the Authority's legal response buries the story."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

## 6. Starter Scenario

**The Conversion Record:** The Outer District Collective has obtained documentation - leaked from inside the Conversion Authority - showing the actual natural conversion ratios and the published rate markup calculations. This is the first time the internal figures have been publicly available. The Authority has filed emergency injunctions. The Collective needs to get the documentation to the Reform Bloc before the injunctions are served. The Authority's enforcement team is already in the Outer District.
