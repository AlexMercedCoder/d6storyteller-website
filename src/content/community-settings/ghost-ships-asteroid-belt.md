## 1. Setting Overview and Tone

Sector 7 sits in the shadowed region of the main asteroid belt, far from commercial shipping lanes. Hundreds of derelict hulls from ancient wars and failed colonial expeditions drift in perpetual silence, tumbling through dark rock fields.

**Tone:** Moody, eerie, atmospheric space horror. Emphasizes dark corridors, flickering flashlights, resource management, and the unknown secrets of abandoned vessels.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Narrow beams of suit flashlights cutting through floating ice crystals and zero-G dust inside pitch-black ship hallways. Massive asteroid silhouettes blocking out distant stars.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic sound of your own breathing inside an EVA helmet. Heavy boots clanking against frozen metal decks. Distant, unexplained thumps echoing through the hull structure.
3. **Touch and Smell:** Bitterness of stale suit oxygen. The freezing chill creeping through suit insulation when touching unpowered bulkheads.

--

## 2. The Graveyard

**The Iron Tomb:** A massive military cruiser broken in half, drifting near the belt's center.

**The Hauler *Prometheus*:** An automated freighter whose cargo holds have remained locked for eighty years.

**Sector 7 Outpost:** A tiny, improvised refueling station built inside a hollowed-out asteroid.

--

## 3. Factions

**The Collectors:** Independent salvage teams operating out of Asteroid Outpost 7.

**The Iron Remnant:** A cult of cybernetic scavengers who modify their bodies using parts harvested from ancient military androids.

**Corporate Reclamation Teams:** High-tech mercenaries sent by Earth megacorps to recover specific classified black boxes before Collectors find them.

--

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Sensory Isolation

Track **Sensory Isolation** from 0 to 6.

**Isolation Triggers:**
- Comms blackout inside heavy lead-shielded hulls: +1 Isolation
- Helmet flashlight failure or power loss: +2 Isolation
- Separated from the boarding team: +1 Isolation

**Isolation Levels:**
- **0-1:** Clear spatial orientation. Normal operational status.
- **2-3:** Mild paranoia, hearing phantom suit warnings, minor skill check penalty.
- **4-5:** Severe disorientation, visual hallucinations (seeing movement in shadows), major check penalties.
- **6:** Sensory Panic. Immediate panic check required.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Boarding Lead

You enter dark airlocks first with a shotgun and a plasma torch.

- **Drive:** *"Lead the team into the wreck and clear the path to the bridge."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 1 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Black-Box Tech

You hack ancient control consoles and extract encrypted flight logs.

- **Drive:** *"Extract the ship's data core before the reactor goes critical."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

### The Salvage Recon

You scout zero-G corridors and secure tethers between the skiff and the wreck.

- **Drive:** *"Find the highest value cargo modules and secure our exit route."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 2 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario: The Signal from *Sovereign*

A 100-year-old battleship, the *Sovereign*, suddenly broadcasts a distress signal on an obsolete military frequency. The Collectors dock their skiff to the airlock, finding the ship perfectly preserved in vacuum, with no signs of the original 400-person crew.

As the team reaches the bridge, the main engines auto-ignite, locking the doors and turning the weapon systems toward the approaching Corporate Reclamation vessel. The players must find out what happened to the crew and override the ship's computer before the battle begins.
