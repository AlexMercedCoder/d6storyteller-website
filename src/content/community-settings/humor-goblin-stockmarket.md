## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** What would financial markets look like if designed by beings with excellent pattern recognition, poor impulse control, and complete indifference to the conventions that make markets stable? The GSX found out.

The **Goblin Stock Exchange** was established when goblins realized that adventurers clearing dungeons created predictable cycles of treasure supply, which created trading opportunities, which created the market. Three hundred years later the market has evolved (mutated?) into a full-spectrum financial system trading everything from dungeon loot spot prices to 50-year dragon bond futures.

The players navigate this market as professionals: they have genuine financial skills, they understand some of what's happening, and they are making decisions every day in a system that is fundamentally irrational in ways that are also, sometimes, rational in ways no theory fully explains.

**Tone:** This is financial satire meets fantasy comedy. The goblins are genuinely competent within their own framework. The framework is genuinely insane. Both of these things are true simultaneously.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** A trading floor the size of a cathedral, floor traders (mostly goblins, some other species who've adapted) moving in controlled chaos that looks random but has its own logic, a massive central rune-ticker displaying prices in real time, price boards that update so fast their information is always slightly historical, and compliance officers watching from an elevated gallery with the resigned expression of beings who know their rules will be violated and are focused on documenting how.
2. **Sound:** The roar of simultaneous trading, a sound that experienced traders can read for market sentiment better than any data feed, the sharp ring of the market opening bell (enchanted to be heard across the city), occasional celebration when someone's bet pays off, and the very specific silence of a significant market event that has not yet been priced in.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The physical chaos of a trading floor: floor tickets, position cards, and market data in constant motion, the smell of magical computation (a sharp, metallic scent that experienced traders associate with profit opportunities), and the weight of a position book that represents more value than most kingdoms.

**The Core Tension:** The market is fundamentally irrational and fundamentally real. Fortunes are made and lost on the GSX. Understanding it is impossible. Not understanding it is worse.

## 2. The GSX Structure

### The Listing Board
What trades on the GSX:
- **Dungeon Loot Commodities:** Real-time prices for gold, gems, magical artifacts, weapons (new vs. used condition)
- **Adventuring Party IPOs:** Parties can list themselves as investment vehicles; returns are highly variable
- **Dragon Bond Futures:** Long-term instruments based on projected dragon hoard growth; extremely stable unless the dragon dies
- **Enchantment Derivatives:** Synthetic instruments whose value is derived from spell prices; virtually nobody understands them; the market for them is enormous
- **Kingdom Credit Default Swaps:** Insurance against kingdom collapse; the most morally complex product on the exchange

### The Regulators
The GSX Compliance Division employs both goblins and non-goblins. The non-goblins enforce rules. The goblins interpret them. The interaction between these two functions is the primary source of the exchange's regulatory character.

### The Market Makers
The entities providing liquidity to the market. Some are legitimate firms. Some are ancient financial entities that predate the exchange. One is a dragon who treats the GSX as a hobby and whose participation is simultaneously extremely helpful and extremely dangerous to the market's stability.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The Short Squeeze:** Someone is short-selling a major adventuring party's stock based on advance knowledge of their failure. The party is real, the knowledge may be legitimate or may be manufactured. The players must determine which, trade appropriately, and prevent a market manipulation that could cascade.

2. **The Dragon Bond Crisis:** The dragon backing a major bond position is rumored to have died. The GSX is in freefall. Investigation reveals the dragon is alive but someone wants the market to think otherwise. A crisis of manipulation and truth in a market that has no reliable truth mechanism.

3. **The Compliance Investigation:** The Compliance Division has launched an investigation into trading practices during last month's dungeon loot spike. The players are either the investigators or the investigated. Either way, the trail leads to someone with enough market power to make the investigation itself dangerous.

4. **The New Instrument:** A goblin quant has designed a new financial instrument so complex that even its creator cannot fully model its behavior. The GSX is considering listing it. The players have been assigned to evaluate it. Their evaluation will either block a historic financial innovation or prevent a catastrophic market failure.

5. **The Crash:** The GSX is in freefall. Not normal goblin-market freefall. Real, systemic, cascade freefall. Something triggered it. Whatever triggered it is still in the market. Find it, stop it, and try not to lose everything in the meantime.
