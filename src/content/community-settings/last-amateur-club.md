## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

Millfield is a working-class town surrounded by the looming glass spires of immortal corporate syndicates. While the rest of the world watches artificial, 200-year-long sporting leagues played by cyborgs and vampiric elites, Millfield FC plays 90-minute matches on real grass. The club is run by volunteers: the baker makes halftime pies, the retired mechanic fixes the turnstiles, and the team consists of local plumbers, teachers, and shopkeepers.

The threat is constant. Corporate conglomerates want Millfield's ground because it sits on a powerful natural node, and because its continued existence is an embarrassing reminder that sports can exist without corporate ownership.

**Tone:** Warm, gritty, underdog drama with speculative corporate elements. It pits community solidarity against soul-crushing commercialism.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** Floodlights cutting through thick English fog, rain-soaked green grass, hand-painted wooden scoreboards, contrasted against glowing holographic corporate ads in the sky.
2. **Sound:** The clattering of wooden turnstiles, 2,000 local fans singing acapella anthems, the thump of a muddy leather ball, and the quiet hum of corporate drone surveillance overhead.
3. **Touch & Smell:** Cold rain, hot meat pies, damp wool scarves, fresh mud, and liniment ointment in a small, cramped locker room.

## 2. The Club & Town

**The Boundary Pitch** is Millfield FC's home ground. Built in 1892, its wooden stands are creaky but sturdy, and its grass is meticulously tended.

**The Red Lion Pub** serves as the informal boardroom where fans, players, and committee members discuss tactics, drink ale, and vote on club decisions.

**The Corporate Spire (Aethelgard Corp)** looms three miles away, a 100-story monolith of black glass where immortal executives monitor financial metrics and map out takeover strategies.

**The Charter Vault** inside the local library holds the original 1892 deed, written on vellum, granting the land to the town's people in perpetuity as long as no player receives a corporate salary.

--

## 3. Factions

**Millfield FC Committee** consists of local volunteers like Chairman Arthur Pendelton and Manager Sarah Jenkins. They operate on a shoestring budget and absolute passion.

**Aethelgard Sports Syndicate** is the corporate behemoth attempting to purchase the ground. Led by Senior Director Vance (an immortal executive who hasn't felt joy in three centuries), they use legal harassment, bribed referees, and buyout offers.

**The Millfield Ultras** are the diehard local supporters who organize matchday atmosphere, maintain the stands, and physically defend the stadium from corporate surveyors.

**The Talent Scouts** are mercenary recruiters trying to lure Millfield's best young local players away with life-changing corporate contracts.

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Community Spirit

Track **Community Spirit** from 0 to 6 (starts at 4).

**Boosting Spirit (+1):**
- Fielding an all-local starting lineup
- Winning a match against a corporate-sponsored feeder team
- Community work (stadium repairs, youth coaching)
- Formally rejecting a corporate buyout offer

**Eroding Spirit (-1):**
- Accepting corporate sponsorship or ad placements
- Selling a key local player to a syndicate
- Using ringers or mercenaries instead of local talent
- Losing a home match badly due to low morale

**Thresholds:**
- **5-6:** Unshakable. The stadium is a fortress; players gain +1d6 to rolls at home.
- **2-4:** Vulnerable. Internal debates about finances divide fans and players.
- **1:** Crisis. Buyout votes are called; corporate agents infiltrate the board.
- **0:** Corporate Takeover. The charter is nullified; the stadium is demolished for a corporate arena.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Volunteer Player-Manager
You work a day job as an electrician and spend your nights planning tactics for Saturday's match. You refuse to let money ruin the game you love.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the club alive for the next generation."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 1 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### The Star Local Youth
You're 18 years old and the most talented striker in the region. Corporate scouts are offering your family millions, but your heart is with your hometown team.
- **Drive:** *"Prove that passion beats corporate conditioning."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### The Veteran Activist Supporter
You've missed three matches in forty years. You know every line of the 1892 charter and will fight every legal battle to protect the ground.
- **Drive:** *"Expose corporate corruption and safeguard the charter."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 3 | Presence 2

## 6. Starter Scenario & Why It Matters

**The Cup Tie & The Injunction:** Millfield FC has miraculously qualified for the regional cup third round, paired against Aethelgard's flagship multi-million-credit squad. On Friday night, Aethelgard's lawyers serve an emergency eviction injunction claiming the 1892 charter has a missing signature. Simultaneously, they offer Millfield's star striker a 5-year corporate contract. The team must solve the legal threat, keep their star player focused, and play the match of their lives on Saturday afternoon.

**Why It Matters:** The Last Amateur Club captures the soul of sports: community, tradition, and belonging, standing against the encroaching tide of hyper-commodification.
