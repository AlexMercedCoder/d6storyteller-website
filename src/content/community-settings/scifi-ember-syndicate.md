> *"The Syndicate wants to know how cold we are willing to get. We want them to know we have been asking that question about our wages for twenty years."*
> - Fire Workers' Union Strike Announcement, Day 1

## 1. Setting Overview

The Ember Syndicate's position is structurally sound: they own the infrastructure, have 30 days of reserves, and believe the threat of a cold city will crack the strike in under two weeks. This calculation has worked five times before. The sixth strike ended when three workers died in a fire-corridor accident that the post-incident review attributed to understaffing the Syndicate had implemented to pressure the union during negotiations. The union is aware of the calculation.

The automated flame-vessel announcement is the triggering event, but the underlying conflict is older: fire workers are essential labor in a world that runs on fire magic. The Syndicate has spent 60 years extracting that labor at the lowest rate the strike calendar allows. Automation is not a new idea - it is the answer to the recurring question of what you do when essential workers know they are essential.

**Tone:** Industrial drama with a countdown. The characters are in the middle of a conflict where the stakes are physical - cold, fuel, fire, winter - and both sides are doing political calculation while real people freeze or do not.

## 2. The Fire Economy

**The Syndicate Refineries** process raw magical flame into stable fuel cells. The process requires skilled pyromancers who can read fuel quality in real time and adjust output accordingly. The automated vessels cannot do this. This fact is in the Syndicate's own technical documentation, in a footnote.

**The Fire Workers' Union** has 40,000 members across the Syndicate's operations. The strike vote was 87% in favor. The 13% who voted against are still walking picket lines because they agree with the principle and disagree with the timing.

**The Independent Fire Brokers** are small pyromantic operators outside the Syndicate who could theoretically supplement fuel production. The Syndicate's exclusive supply contracts with city distribution networks have been preventing this for 30 years. Those contracts are what the union is now asking the city government to review.

## 3. Factions

**The Ember Syndicate** - The energy corporation. Calculating and patient. Has broken five strikes. Has documentation suggesting the automated vessels are not as capable as announced.

**The Fire Workers' Union** - 40,000 members on strike. United on the principle; divided on tactics. Has the city's winter in their hands.

**The City Government** - Technically neutral. Receives significant tax revenue from the Syndicate. Also responsible for its citizens not dying in the cold. These two things are in conflict.

## 4. Unique Mechanic

**Flame Channel (1-6):**
- 3+: Ignite, sustain, or extinguish magical fires without tools.
- 5+: Produce fuel-grade flame (power standard systems) per 1 Channel action per scene.
- Industrial production: Channel 4, 1 full scene per day of fuel.
- Automated vessels: Channel 2 equivalent. Cannot manage variable conditions.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### The Union Shop Captain
Runs the picket line at Refinery 7. Channel 5. Has turned down three separate Syndicate offers to cross the line in exchange for personal contract guarantees. Has also been quietly meeting with independent fire brokers to understand what supplemental production is actually possible.
- **Drive:** *"Get supplemental production numbers before the city government uses the cold as a reason to break the strike."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 2 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 2

### The Syndicate Engineer
Wrote the automated vessel capability assessment that became the footnote. Has been asked by Syndicate leadership to rewrite it more favorably before the city government requests technical documentation. Has not rewritten it yet.
- **Drive:** *"Decide whether honest documentation is worth the professional consequences before the request becomes an instruction."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 1 | Mind 5 | Presence 1

## 6. Starter Scenario

**Day 22:** The reserves are at 8 days. The city government has called emergency sessions. The Syndicate is offering binding arbitration with terms the union calls surrender and the city calls reasonable. An independent fire broker has told the characters she can supply 40% of the city's needs from non-Syndicate sources - but only if the city rescinds the exclusive supply contracts. The Syndicate's lawyer has informed the city that rescinding those contracts while the strike is ongoing would be a breach of the existing supply agreement, triggering penalties larger than the city's annual budget. The city government's emergency session is tomorrow morning.
