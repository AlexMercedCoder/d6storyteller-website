## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** In 16th-century Nicaragua, the volcanic island of **Ometepe** (rising dramatically from Lake Nicaragua) is the sacred center of the Nicarao and Chorotega nations. As Spanish forces attempt to conquer the lake trade routes, local leaders deploy a unique combination of spiritual power and theatrical satire - embodying the spirit of *El Güegüense*, the brilliant trickster figure of Nicaraguan heritage.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The majestic twin cones of Concepción (fire) and Maderas (mist) towering over Lake Nicaragua; golden petroglyphs carved into basalt boulders along the shoreline; the fins of freshwater bull sharks carving through lake waves.
2. **Sound:** The rhythmic stamping of wooden masks during street dances; the roar of steam vents from Concepción's crater; vibrant laughter of villagers outsmarting tax collectors.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The warm, sulfur-tinted breeze off Lake Nicaragua; smooth basalt petroglyph stones baked by the sun; the aroma of corn chicha brewing in clay jars.

**The Core Tension:** Open Warfare vs. Satirical Subversion. Direct battle against Spanish armor is deadly, but sharp wit, double-talk, and volcanic omens can defeat an empire without firing a shot.

--

## 2. Historical & Mythological Context

Nicaragua's cultural roots blend Chorotega and Nicarao traditions with the famous satirical theater dance *El Güegüense* (recognized by UNESCO as a Masterpiece of Oral and Intangible Heritage). Ometepe Island and Lake Nicaragua (home to unique freshwater bull sharks) form a legendary setting of myth and history.

> **Note on Respectful Portrayal:** *El Güegüense* is a proud symbol of Nicaraguan cultural wit and non-violent resistance. This setting honors that legacy by making humor and cleverness lethal weapons against tyranny.

--

## 3. Core Factions & Traditions

### The Dancers of El Güegüense (Macho Ratón Guild)
Satirists and folk performers who use double-talk, masks, and street theater to disarm colonial authorities.
- **Cultural Association:** Masters of disguise, legal loopholes, and double meanings.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"I can convince a governor to sign his own tax exemption with a smile"*
  - *"My wooden mask hides every expression while I read his secrets"*

### The Lake-Shark Callers (Nicarao K'i)
Mystics who commune with the freshwater bull sharks of Lake Nicaragua (*Lake Cocibolca*).
- **Cultural Association:** Aquatic scouts and guardians of Ometepe's shores.
- **Descriptor Examples:**
  - *"The freshwater sharks answer my drumbeat when foreign boats approach"*
  - *"I can swim across Lake Cocibolca in the dark without touching bottom"*

## 4. Unique Mechanic: Satiric Wit

Characters track a **Trickster Meter** (1–6):
- **1–2 (Direct):** Standard physical rolls. Social rolls require straightforward checks.
- **3–4 (Witty):** +1d6 to Presence and Mind when using satire, bluffing, or riddles. Costs 1 Momentum.
- **5–6 (Master Trickster):** Successes automatically cause opponents to humiliate themselves. Failure causes volcanic ash fallout.

## 5. Character Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Master Trickster (El Güegüense)
- **Concept:** A street performer and merchant who uses double-talk to protect his village.
- **Descriptors:** *Flawless double-talker*, *Wears the golden horse-mask of resistance*, *Knows every Spanish law better than the judge*.
- **Drive:** *"Outsmart the oppressors and keep our people laughing."*
- **Flaw:** *"Cannot resist making a joke even when a blade is at my throat."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 2 | Mind 2 | Presence 3

### Archetype 2: The Island Shark-Caller
- **Concept:** A Nicarao warrior who patrols Lake Cocibolca.
- **Descriptors:** *Rides the lake waves in a dugout canoe*, *Commands the freshwater sharks*, *Tattooed with basalt petroglyphs*.
- **Drive:** *"Keep the sacred waters of Ometepe free from conquest."*
- **Flaw:** *"Distrusts anyone born away from the lake."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 3 | Finesse 2 | Mind 1 | Presence 2

### Archetype 3: The Petroglyph Carver
- **Concept:** A Chorotega artisan who reads volcanic prophecies written in stone.
- **Descriptors:** *Carves sacred symbols into basalt*, *Reads the volcanic ash omens*, *Keeper of ancient oral lineages*.
- **Drive:** *"Record our history in stone so it outlasts empires."*
- **Flaw:** *"Obsessed with ancient prophecies."*
- **Approach Distribution:** Force 1 | Finesse 3 | Mind 3 | Presence 1

## 7. Starter Scenario: The Dance of the Governor's Tax

- **Framing:** The Spanish governor arrives on Ometepe to seize the island's corn harvest.
- **3-Layer Scene:**
  1. *Immediate Threat:* Staging a street performance that delays the tax collection.
  2. *Ticking Clock:* Concepción volcano rumbling; eruption in 4 beats.
  3. *Opportunity:* Using the distraction to swap the tax corn for volcanic rocks.
- **Antagonist:** *Governor Don Pedro de Guzman* (Pompous Colonial Official).
  - *Traits:* Vain, gullible when flattered.
  - *Condition Track:* ☐ Flattered / ☐ Outsmarted / ☐ Humiliated.

## 8. Why This Setting Matters

Nicaragua's history is defined by creative resilience, poetic spirit, and dramatic natural beauty. This setting celebrates *El Güegüense* as a timeless model of intelligent resistance.
