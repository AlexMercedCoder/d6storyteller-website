## 1. Setting Overview & Tone

**High-Concept Pitch:** The dead are rising because someone has broken the judgment process. The players must fix it before chaos consumes creation.

The setting draws on the extraordinarily rich Egyptian mythological tradition: the Duat as a navigable landscape through which the dead travel toward judgment, the complex theology of Ma'at as the force that sustains cosmic order, the 42 Assessors and their specific domains, and the vivid mythology of Ra's nightly journey through the twelve hours of the underworld.

The players are Sem-Priests of Anubis: religious specialists trained in the funerary traditions, capable of navigating the Duat in spiritual form, and charged with maintaining the correct functioning of the death-process. They work where most people don't go: at the boundary.

**Sensory Pillars:**
1. **Sight:** The Hall of Two Truths illuminated by a light with no visible source, the scales of judgment standing at its center, Anubis presiding with absolute precision over the weighing, the 42 Assessors arranged in their ceremonial positions -- and the wrong ones silent, their places empty; the Duat's twelve hours as distinct landscapes, each ruled by a different deity and presenting different challenges to the soul in transit.
2. **Sound:** The incantations of the 42 Negative Confessions spoken by a soul presenting itself for judgment, the sound of the Feather of Ma'at settling on its scale, the howl of Ammit waiting in the shadows for hearts that fail the weighing, and the deep silence of a judgment hall where something has gone wrong.
3. **Touch & Smell:** The heat of the Egyptian desert, the cool dark of temple interiors, the complex smell of the natron salts used in mummification, the incense burned to carry prayers to the gods, and the cold emptiness that follows a soul whose heart was consumed by Ammit.

## 2. The Duat's Geography

### The Twelve Hours
Each hour of the Duat is a distinct realm: the first hour is the entrance, vast and dark; the seventh hour is guarded by Apophis, the serpent of chaos, who attempts to devour Ra's solar bark every night; the twelfth hour emerges into the eastern horizon where Ra is reborn. The Sem-Priests can navigate these realms in spiritual form, but each hour's guardian must be correctly propitiated.

### The Hall of Two Truths
Where all souls are ultimately judged. The 42 Assessors question the soul. The heart is weighed against the Feather of Ma'at. Thoth records the outcome. Horus and Set act as advocates. If the heart is heavier than the feather, Ammit devours it and the soul ceases entirely. If equal or lighter, the soul passes to the Field of Reeds.

### The Breaking
Something has corrupted the weighing process. Souls are arriving at the Hall and finding it empty, or finding the scales broken, or -- most disturbing -- finding the scales tilted before the weighing begins.

## 3. Adventure Seeds

1. **The Restless Pharaoh:** A recently deceased Pharaoh's spirit has not passed to the Field of Reeds and is instead walking the Kemet, disrupting divine order by the sheer weight of a royal soul out of place. Finding why requires entering the Duat and determining what the Pharaoh's heart contains that prevents the weighing.

2. **The Missing Assessor:** One of the 42 Assessors has vanished from the Hall. No soul can be fully judged without all 42. Whoever removed the Assessor has effectively paralyzed the death process for that soul's specific category of moral question. Finding and restoring the Assessor requires traveling into the Duat's dangerous outer regions.

3. **The Human Corruptor:** The manipulation of the scales is coming from the living world, not the divine. Someone in Egypt has found a way to interfere with the judgment of specific souls -- either saving the corrupt or damning the innocent. The motive is political: controlling who passes judgment gives power over divine favor in the living world.

4. **The Apophis Rising:** The Serpent of Chaos that Ra defeats every night is getting stronger. It's not defeating Ra yet, but the victories are becoming narrower. Something is feeding Apophis -- and the source may be connected to the corrupted Hall. The players must find what is strengthening chaos before Ra's next failure is permanent.

5. **The Soldier's Heart:** A soldier who died heroically has a heart heavy with justified violence. The weighing is uncertain: was the killing in service of Ma'at or against it? The soul cannot pass until the Assessors reach a verdict. The soul's loved ones are beginning to feel its presence in the living world. The verdict must be reached.
