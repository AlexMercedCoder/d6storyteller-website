The Cybernetics, Speculative & Alternate Tech Companion explores the relationship between technology and humanity across ten settings where the tools you use define who you are and what you risk.

Technology in the D6 Storyteller system is never neutral. Every device, graft, or digital system comes with a trade-off built directly into its mechanic. In Genome Revolution, your spliced genes make you powerful — until they make you unpredictable. In Hardwired City, scanning someone's memories gives you the evidence you need and risks destroying your own neural architecture. In Aether Voyagers, your coal supply is both your fuel and your countdown clock.

Each of the ten settings delivers a complete playable experience: evocative world lore, a genre mechanic that makes the technology feel real, three ready-to-play archetypes, a condition palette suited to the setting's hazards, and a starter scenario. The range here is intentional — from the pulp optimism of Rocket Cadets of Sector 7 to the body-horror stakes of Genome Revolution to the whimsical clockwork ecology of Mechanized Realm.

This companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all other volumes in the series. Groups can import cybernetic grafts into fantasy settings or bring alchemical thinking into cyberpunk worlds.
