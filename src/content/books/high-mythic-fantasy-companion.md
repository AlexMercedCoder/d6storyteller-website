The High & Mythic Fantasy Companion takes the D6 Storyteller engine into the grandest scales of imaginative fiction, where gods leave continent-sized corpses, kingdoms sail the sky on floating islands, and dragon riders share pain with their bonded drakes.

This companion delivers ten complete high-fantasy settings, each with a distinct mechanical hook that captures the unique tension of its genre. From the sun-scorched alchemical desert of Salt & Crucible to the frost-lit courts of Summer & Winter Solstice, every setting gives your group a vivid world to inhabit immediately.

Each of the ten settings includes a full overview and sensory pillars to establish tone, a unique genre mechanic that integrates with the D6 dice pool system, three ready-to-play character archetypes with vivid descriptors and approach distributions, a genre-specific condition palette covering physical, social, and psychological harm, and a complete starter scenario with three-layer scene design.

The High & Mythic Fantasy Companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all other companions in the series. Groups can mix settings and mechanics freely, combining the Empathy Resonance of dragon-rider bonds with the Reagent Degradation of alchemical crafting to create hybrid campaigns unlike anything in a single book.
