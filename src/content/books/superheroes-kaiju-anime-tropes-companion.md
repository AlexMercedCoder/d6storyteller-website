D6 Storyteller: Superheroes, Kaiju & Anime Tropes Companion brings the most visually dramatic and emotionally heightened genres of popular fiction to the D6 engine. These settings are built for stories that embrace iconic narrative structures: the underdog's last-round comeback, the hero's sacrifice, the team transformation sequence, the rival who becomes an ally.

Anime tropes and superhero storytelling are not just aesthetic choices; they are narrative mechanics. This companion translates those tropes directly into D6 systems. Ki Charge in Martial Apex means your character spends rounds building up to a climactic attack. Bond of Friendship in Starlight Guardians means cooperation literally unlocks abilities that solo play cannot. Unstable Gene Expression in Ruined Dynasty means rolling duplicate dice produces wild secondary effects: chaos that fits a world where the original heroes' legacy is a radiation zone.

Each of the ten settings delivers a full atmospheric overview, genre mechanic, three archetypes, condition palette, and starter scenario. The range spans gritty street-level vigilantism, cosmic ring-bearer space opera, high-school mech piloting drama, and full kaiju city-defense operations.

This companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all other volumes in the series.
