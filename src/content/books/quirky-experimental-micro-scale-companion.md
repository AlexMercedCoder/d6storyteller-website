D6 Storyteller: Quirky, Experimental & Micro-Scale Companion is the series' most playful volume: a collection of ten settings that deliberately expand the boundaries of what tabletop RPG play can look like. Not every campaign needs to be epic. Sometimes you are a four-inch borrower terrified of the family cat. Sometimes you are a stuffed bear protecting a sleeping child from the closet. Sometimes you are a time cop desperately trying to prevent someone from changing the 1987 World Series.

These settings prove that the fiction-first D6 engine scales to any premise. The Household Hazard mechanic in Borrowers of the Hearth makes a running vacuum cleaner a genuine boss encounter. Narrative Consistency in Living Manuscripts means players must stay true to the plot of the book they've entered or risk the story collapsing around them. Paradox Meter in Chrono Patrol creates genuine tension even in a comedic time-travel setting: the stakes are real, even if the tone is light.

Each of the ten settings includes full world overview, a unique mechanic, three genre-appropriate archetypes, a condition palette, and a complete starter scenario. Tone guidance is included to help groups calibrate their play: these settings range from cozy adventure to biting satire to light horror.

This companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all other volumes in the series.
