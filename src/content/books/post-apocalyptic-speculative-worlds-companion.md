D6 Storyteller: Post-Apocalyptic & Speculative Worlds Companion is the final volume in the core companion series: and it earns that position by going all the way to the end of time. These ten settings explore what remains when civilization collapses, when the planet turns against its inhabitants, or when the universe itself begins to wind down.

Post-apocalyptic fiction is about stakes. Resource mechanics in this companion are not optional texture: they are the engine of conflict. Heat packs in Ice-Age 2099 are currency and survival tool simultaneously; spending them is always a choice between warmth and trade. The Decibel Meter in Sound-Sensitive Horrors makes every action a calculation: how much noise is this roll worth? Entropy Decay in Omega Epoch means the world gets measurably worse as the campaign progresses, and combining abilities across historical eras through Epoch Fusion is both the players' greatest advantage and a sign of how desperate things have become.

Each of the ten settings delivers full atmospheric world-building, a unique survival or speculative mechanic, three archetypes built for harsh conditions, a condition palette calibrated to each setting's specific hazards, and a complete starter scenario. These are worlds designed to produce stories worth telling.

The Post-Apocalyptic & Speculative Worlds Companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all companion volumes in the series.
