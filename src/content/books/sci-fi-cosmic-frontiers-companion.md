The Sci-Fi & Cosmic Frontiers Companion propels the D6 Storyteller engine into the far reaches of speculative fiction: from the cramped corridors of a generation ship three centuries into its voyage to the impossible interior of a star-enclosing Dyson Sphere inhabited by millions of alien species.

Science fiction demands mechanics that feel grounded in the logic of their world. This companion delivers ten settings where resource management, environmental hazards, and the consequences of advanced technology become active elements of play. Running out of oxygen is not just flavor: it changes your position. Overheating your mech armor mid-battle is not just description: it costs you condition slots.

Each of the ten settings in this companion includes full world lore, a unique genre mechanic, three ready-to-play character archetypes built for the setting's specific challenges, a genre-specific condition palette, and a complete starter scenario seed. Whether your group wants to play corporate whistleblowers on a billed-by-the-breath moon base, guerrilla survivors fighting alien assimilation, or transhuman travelers managing digital corruption across the cosmos, this companion provides the framework.

The Sci-Fi & Cosmic Frontiers Companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all companion volumes, allowing groups to blend settings and mechanics across genres.
