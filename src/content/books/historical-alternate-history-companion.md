D6 Storyteller: Historical & Alternate History Companion grounds the D6 engine in the weight of real history and the imaginative potential of speculative deviation. These ten settings occupy actual historical periods (or close parallels) where the documented record collides with mythological forces, supernatural adversaries, and technologies that never were.

History provides drama that invented settings must work hard to match. The personal stakes of a Roman legionary defending a formation under a divine contract, the cultural weight of a samurai maintaining Bushido while negotiating a Yokai pact, the paranoid intensity of Allied soldiers discovering that their enemies have genuine demonic support: these are conflicts rooted in documented human struggle. The D6 Storyteller system translates that weight into mechanics that honor the genre.

Each of the ten settings is carefully constructed to explore its historical period authentically while allowing the fiction-first engine to introduce speculative elements that deepen play rather than contradict the history. Every setting includes full atmospheric overview, a unique mechanic, three archetypes, a period-appropriate condition palette, and a complete starter scenario.

The Historical & Alternate History Companion is fully compatible with the D6 Storyteller Core Rulebook and all companion volumes.
