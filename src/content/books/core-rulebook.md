D6 Storyteller: The Core Rulebook is the complete, self-contained foundation of the fiction-first D6 engine. Written by Alex Merced, it gives players and Storytellers everything they need to run dramatic, narrative-rich games across any genre — all powered by a simple pool of six-sided dice.

At its heart, the D6 Storyteller system asks one guiding question before any dice are rolled: *what is happening in the fiction?* Your character's narrative position — whether you're cornered, holding the high ground, or operating in the dark — determines the size of your dice pool and the magnitude of your potential effect. The dice confirm the story; the fiction drives it.

The Core Rulebook opens with a full explanation of the engine: character creation using vivid descriptors and approach dice (Force, Finesse, Mind, Presence), the complete dice mechanics, conflict resolution, harm and consequences, and a comprehensive Storyteller's guide. Nothing requires proprietary systems or unusual dice — just standard six-sided dice you likely already own.

The second half of the book delivers ten complete starter worlds, each with its own tone, unique mechanical hook, three ready-to-play character archetypes, a genre-specific condition palette, and a starter scenario seed. From alchemical empires and fog-bound detective cities to cyberpunk megacities and flooded post-apocalyptic wastelands, the Core Rulebook is designed to put any group at the table within minutes.
