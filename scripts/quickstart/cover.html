D6 Storyteller

Quickstart

Everything a new table needs for a first session of D6 Storyteller, the fiction-first roleplaying system by Alex Merced. Players describe what they do, pick an approach, roll a handful of six-sided dice, and read the highest die. Print these pages, run session zero, and play.

In this packet

  1. Player Rules Cheat SheetHow a roll works, reading the dice, the four approaches, position and effect, assists, and conditions. Hand it to each player.
  2. Storyteller Quick ReferenceDice results, the position table with failure costs, effect levels, conditions and recovery, Ticking Clocks, and Storyteller principles.
  3. Core Character SheetApproaches, descriptors, Drive and Flaw, conditions, a setting mechanic box, and bonds. One per player.
  4. Session Zero WorksheetCampaign basics, tone, safety tools, table expectations, character connections, and the opening question. Fill it in together.
The full rules, genre advice, and settings are in D6 Storyteller: The Core Rulebook and its companion books.
