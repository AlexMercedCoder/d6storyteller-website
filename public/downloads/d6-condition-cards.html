Condition Tracker Cards

Print and cut out one set of three cards per player. When a character takes a condition, place the matching card face-up in front of them. The mechanical effects are printed right on the card so nobody forgets them.

Tip: Print on cardstock. Use Ctrl+P → Landscape → Save as PDF.

Condition Recovery Quick Reference

Conditions improve through the fiction, not through mechanics. Shaken clears with a brief rest or a calming moment. Hurt requires medical attention, extended rest, or a deliberate recovery scene. Out needs rescue, intervention, or significant time. The Storyteller decides when recovery happens based on what the story demands. Conditions do not automatically clear between sessions.
